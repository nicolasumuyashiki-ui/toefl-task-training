💡 Conversation 攻略のコツ💡 Conversation strategy tips

📝

メモの取り方How to take notes

問題は何か？解決策は何か？をキーワードだけメモ。全文書き取ろうとしない！Note only the keywords for 'What is the problem? What is the solution?' Do not try to transcribe everything!

🔚

最後の発言に注意Watch the closing line

結論・次のアクションは会話の終盤に出ることが多い。「I'll...」に全集中！Conclusions and next actions usually come near the end. Lock in on 'I'll...'!

🔄

2人の役割を区別Distinguish the two roles

学生＝問題を持ち込む側、スタッフ＝解決策を提示する側。役割を意識してメモStudent = the one with the problem; staff = the one with the solution. Take notes with the roles in mind.

🗣️ Conversation 1 — ワークスタディの申請🗣️ Conversation 1 — Work-study application

場面：Financial Aid Office ｜ 学生（男性）＋ アドバイザー（女性）Setting: Financial Aid Office | Student (male) + Advisor (female)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Hi, I was wondering if I'm eligible for the work-study program this semester. I applied last month but haven't heard back.
[Advisor] Let me pull up your file. Could I have your student ID number?
[Student] Sure, it's 4-7-2-9-0-1.
[Advisor] Okay, I see your application here. It looks like you haven't submitted your most recent tax documents. We need those to verify your financial need.
[Student] Oh, I thought the documents I submitted with my FAFSA would be enough.
[Advisor] Unfortunately, the work-study program requires additional verification. You'll need to upload your most recent tax return through the student portal by the end of this week.
[Student] Got it. And if everything checks out, when could I start working?
[Advisor] Once your documents are approved, usually within three business days, you can browse available positions on the employment page. Most on-campus jobs offer between ten and fifteen hours per week.
[Student] That sounds perfect. I'll upload those documents today. Thanks for your help.
1
Why hasn't the student's work-study application been processed?
Detail
(A) He missed the application deadline.
(B) He is missing required tax documents.
(C) His GPA does not meet the minimum requirement.
(D) The program is already full for this semester.
解説：Explanation: アドバイザーが「you haven't submitted your most recent tax documents」と明確に述べている。税関連書類の未提出が原因。The advisor says clearly, 'you haven't submitted your most recent tax documents.' The missing tax paperwork is the cause.
🔑 原因問題は「Why hasn't...」：スタッフの説明部分に理由が示される。「It looks like...」の後に注目。Cause questions ('Why hasn't...'): the reason is shown in the staff's explanation. Focus after 'It looks like...'
2
What will the student do next?
Detail
(A) Schedule another appointment with the advisor.
(B) Submit a new FAFSA application.
(C) Upload tax documents through the student portal.
(D) Apply directly to on-campus employers.
解説：Explanation: 学生は最後に「I'll upload those documents today」と明言。アドバイザーも「upload your most recent tax return through the student portal」と指示している。At the end, the student says, 'I'll upload those documents today,' and the advisor instructs, 'upload your most recent tax return through the student portal.'
🔑 「次に何をするか」は常に会話の締めに出る：最後の1〜2文に全集中！「I'll...」が直接のヒント。'What to do next' always comes at the conversation's wrap-up. Focus on the last 1–2 sentences! 'I'll...' is the direct hint.

🗣️ Conversation 2 — 実験結果の食い違い🗣️ Conversation 2 — Mismatched experiment results

場面：Chemistry Lab ｜ 学生A（女性）＋ 学生B / ラボパートナー（男性）Setting: Chemistry Lab | Student A (female) + Student B / lab partner (male)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student A] Hey, did you look at our experiment results from last Thursday? My numbers are totally different from yours.
[Student B] Really? I got a concentration of 0.45 moles per liter. What did you get?
[Student A] I got 0.32. That's a pretty big difference. Do you think one of us made a measurement error?
[Student B] It's possible. I remember the solution looked a bit cloudy when I was measuring it. Maybe some of the solid hadn't fully dissolved yet.
[Student A] That could explain why your reading was higher. The undissolved particles might have affected the spectrophotometer reading.
[Student B] Good point. Should we ask Professor Tanaka if we can redo the experiment before the lab report is due?
[Student A] I think that's a good idea. The report is due on Monday, so we'd need to do it by Friday at the latest.
[Student B] I'll email the professor right now and ask if the lab is available this Friday afternoon.
3
What can be inferred about the cause of the different results?
Inference
(A) The solution was not fully dissolved during measurement.
(B) They used different chemicals for the experiment.
(C) The spectrophotometer was broken during the test.
(D) They conducted the experiment on different days.
解説：Explanation: Student Bが「the solution looked a bit cloudy」「some of the solid hadn't fully dissolved yet」と述べ、Student Aも「undissolved particles might have affected the spectrophotometer reading」と同意。溶液が完全に溶けていなかったことが原因。Student B says, 'the solution looked a bit cloudy,' 'some of the solid hadn't fully dissolved yet,' and Student A agrees, 'undissolved particles might have affected the spectrophotometer reading.' The cause is incomplete dissolution.
🔑 推論問題：2人が議論して合意した結論が答え。「That could explain...」「Good point」など同意の表現に注目。Inference: the agreed conclusion of the two speakers is the answer. Watch for agreement signals like 'That could explain...' / 'Good point.'
4
What will the students most likely do before the deadline?
Inference
(A) Submit the report with both sets of data.
(B) Ask the professor to extend the deadline.
(C) Use the average of their two results.
(D) Redo the experiment after getting the professor's approval.
解説：Explanation: Student Bは「I'll email the professor right now」と述べ、Student Aも「I think that's a good idea」と同意。教授の許可を得て実験をやり直す流れが推測できる。Student B says, 'I'll email the professor right now,' and Student A agrees, 'I think that's a good idea.' We can infer they'll get permission to repeat the experiment.
🔑 推論問題：2人の合意から次の行動を推測。「Should we...」→「I think that's a good idea」→「I'll email...」の流れが答えのヒント。Inference: infer the next action from the agreement. The flow 'Should we...' → 'I think that's a good idea' → 'I'll email...' is the hint.
