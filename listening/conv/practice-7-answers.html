💡 Conversation 攻略のコツ

📝

メモの取り方

問題は何か？解決策は何か？をキーワードだけメモ。全文書き取ろうとしない！

🔄

問題解決パターン

挨拶→問題提示→解決策提案→結論。キャンパス会話の多くはこのパターンに従う

😮

感情表現に注目

"That's a problem" "That would be great" などの反応から話者の状況を把握する

🗣️ Conversation 1 — 寮の部屋変更リクエスト

場面：Housing Office ｜ 学生（男性）＋ コーディネーター（女性）

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[Student] Hi, I'd like to request a room change. I'm currently in Baker Hall, but I was wondering if there are any openings in Greenfield Residence.
[Coordinator] I see. May I ask why you'd like to switch?
[Student] Well, my roommate has a completely different schedule from mine. He stays up until three in the morning, and I have an eight AM class every day. It's really affecting my sleep.
[Coordinator] I understand. That's a common issue. Have you tried discussing quiet hours with your roommate first?
[Student] I have, but it hasn't worked out. He agreed to use headphones, but the light from his desk lamp still keeps me awake.
[Coordinator] Okay. Greenfield does have a few single rooms available, but they cost about two hundred dollars more per semester. Would that be all right?
[Student] That's fine. At this point, getting enough sleep is worth the extra cost.
[Coordinator] Great. I'll need you to fill out a transfer request form. If everything is approved, you could move in as early as next Monday.
1
Why does the student want to change rooms?
Detail
(A) His current room is too small for his belongings.
(B) His roommate's schedule is disrupting his sleep.
(C) He wants to live closer to the science building.
(D) His room has a maintenance problem.
解説： 学生は「He stays up until three in the morning」「It's really affecting my sleep」と明確に述べている。ルームメイトの生活リズムの違いが睡眠に影響している。
🔑 ヒント位置：会話の冒頭（学生の2発話目）— 問題提示パターンの典型。最初の数発話で理由が語られる。
2
What does the coordinator tell the student about Greenfield Residence?
Detail
(A) It is fully occupied this semester.
(B) It requires a minimum GPA to apply.
(C) Single rooms are available but cost more.
(D) It is being renovated until next month.
解説： コーディネーターは「a few single rooms available」「cost about two hundred dollars more per semester」と説明している。空き有り＋追加費用の2つの情報。
🔑 解決策パートの「but」に注意：「空きがある、でも高い」のように条件付きの解決策が出る場合、両方の要素を含む選択肢が正解。

🗣️ Conversation 2 — 中間試験後の追加点

場面：Professor's Office ｜ 学生（女性）＋ 教授（男性）

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[Student] Professor Davis, do you have a moment? I wanted to ask about extra credit opportunities for your sociology class.
[Professor] Sure, come in. What's on your mind?
[Student] Well, I didn't do as well as I expected on the midterm, and I'm worried about my final grade. I was hoping there might be some way to earn additional points.
[Professor] I see. I don't typically offer extra credit assignments, but there is something you could do. The department is hosting a guest lecture next Thursday by Dr. Yamamoto on urban sociology. If you attend and write a one-page summary connecting her talk to our course material, I can add five points to your midterm score.
[Student] That sounds great. What time is the lecture?
[Professor] It starts at four PM in the auditorium. I'd also suggest visiting the writing center before submitting your summary. They can help you polish your work.
[Student] I'll definitely do that. Thank you so much, Professor Davis.
[Professor] You're welcome. And remember, the final exam is worth forty percent, so strong performance there can really turn things around.
3
What can be inferred about why the student is worried?
Inference
(A) She wants to change her major to sociology.
(B) She needs a deadline extension for an assignment.
(C) She is struggling to understand the course material.
(D) Her midterm score was lower than she expected.
解説： 学生は「I didn't do as well as I expected on the midterm」と述べ、「worried about my final grade」と続けている。期待を下回る中間成績が心配の原因だと推測できる。
🔑 推論問題：「なぜ心配しているか」は発言の行間を読む。"as well as I expected" が期待と現実のギャップを示す。
4
What will the student most likely do next?
Inference
(A) Attend a guest lecture and write a summary.
(B) Retake the midterm exam the following week.
(C) Drop the course and register again next semester.
(D) Form a study group with her classmates.
解説： 学生は教授の提案（ゲスト講義に出席してサマリーを書く）に「That sounds great」と同意し、講義の時間も確認している。次に取る行動として最も妥当。
🔑 推論問題：「What will...most likely do next」は会話の結論から推測。学生の「I'll definitely do that」が決定的なヒント。
