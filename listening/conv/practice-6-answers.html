💡 Conversation 攻略のコツ💡 Conversation strategy tips

📝

メモの取り方How to take notes

問題は何か？解決策は何か？をキーワードだけメモ。全文書き取ろうとしない！Note only the keywords for 'What is the problem? What is the solution?' Do not try to transcribe everything!

😮

感情表現に注目Watch for emotion words

"That's really helpful" "I appreciate that" などの反応から話者の状況を把握するCatch the speaker's situation from reactions like 'That's really helpful' or 'I appreciate that.'

🔄

2人の役割を区別Distinguish the two roles

学生＝問題を持ち込む側、スタッフ＝解決策を提示する側。役割を意識してメモStudent = the one with the problem; staff = the one with the solution. Take notes with the roles in mind.

🗣️ Conversation 1 — 食物アレルギー対応🗣️ Conversation 1 — Food allergy accommodation

場面：Campus Dining Hall Office ｜ 学生（女性）＋ マネージャー（男性）Setting: Campus Dining Hall Office | Student (female) + Manager (male)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Excuse me, I wanted to talk to someone about the meal options in the dining hall. I have a severe peanut allergy, and I've been finding it really difficult to know which dishes are safe for me.
[Manager] I'm sorry to hear that. We do take food allergies very seriously. Have you noticed any specific dishes that were a concern?
[Student] Well, last Tuesday the stir-fry was listed as vegetarian, but there was no mention of peanut oil, which was actually used in the sauce. I only found out when I asked the kitchen staff directly.
[Manager] That shouldn't happen. All allergen information should be displayed on the menu cards. I'll look into why it was missing that day.
[Student] I appreciate that. Is there a way I could get advance notice of the weekly menu so I can plan ahead?
[Manager] Absolutely. We actually post the full weekly menu on our website every Sunday evening. I can also add you to our allergy notification list, which sends an email alert whenever a dish contains one of the major allergens.
[Student] That would be perfect. Could I also suggest putting clearer labels directly on the serving stations?
[Manager] That's a great idea. We've been considering color-coded labels for the top eight allergens. I'll bring your suggestion to our next staff meeting and fast-track the implementation.
1
Why does the student visit the dining hall office?
Detail
(A) To complain about the taste of the food.
(B) To ask about the dining hall's operating hours.
(C) To apply for a job in the kitchen.
(D) To discuss food allergy accommodation.
解説：Explanation: 学生の最初の発言で「I have a severe peanut allergy」と述べ、食事の安全性について相談に来ている。会話全体がアレルギー対応の改善について。The student says at the start, 'I have a severe peanut allergy,' coming to discuss meal safety. The whole conversation is about improving allergy responses.
🔑 目的問題は冒頭に答え：学生の最初の発話で「なぜ来たか」が明確に述べられるパターン。Purpose at the start: the typical pattern is the student's first line saying 'why I came.'
2
What does the manager offer to do for the student?
Detail
(A) Prepare special meals exclusively for her.
(B) Add her to an allergy notification email list.
(C) Refund her meal plan fees.
(D) Transfer her to a different dining hall.
解説：Explanation: マネージャーは「I can also add you to our allergy notification list, which sends an email alert whenever a dish contains one of the major allergens」と提案。具体的な行動の提示。The manager offers, 'I can also add you to our allergy notification list, which sends an email alert whenever a dish contains one of the major allergens.' That's a concrete next action.
🔑 「offer to do」は解決策パートに：スタッフが提示する具体的なアクションが正解。"I can..." "I'll..." の後に注目。'Offer to do' belongs to the solution part. 'I can...' / 'I'll...' precedes the right answer.

🗣️ Conversation 2 — 研究論文のテーゼ絞り込み🗣️ Conversation 2 — Narrowing a research paper thesis

場面：Writing Center ｜ 学生（男性）＋ チューター（女性）Setting: Writing Center | Student (male) + Tutor (female)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Hi, I signed up for a tutoring session because I'm struggling with the structure of my research paper. My professor said my thesis is too broad.
[Tutor] That's actually one of the most common issues we see here. Can you tell me what your paper is about?
[Student] It's for my sociology class. I'm writing about how social media affects young people. But I'm not sure how to narrow it down.
[Tutor] Okay, that's definitely a very broad topic. One approach is to focus on a specific aspect, like the impact of social media on academic performance among college students. That gives you a clear population and a measurable outcome.
[Student] That makes sense. But how do I restructure my thesis statement once I narrow it down?
[Tutor] A strong thesis should include your topic, your position, and a preview of your main supporting points.
[Student] That's really helpful. Should I rewrite my introduction first, or adjust the body paragraphs?
[Tutor] I'd recommend starting with the thesis, then revising the body paragraphs to align with your new focus. Once those are solid, rewrite the introduction last so it accurately previews your argument.
3
What can be inferred about the student's approach to his paper?
Inference
(A) He could not find enough sources for the paper.
(B) He missed the deadline for his assignment.
(C) He chose a topic that was too broad.
(D) He did not understand the grading rubric.
解説：Explanation: 学生は「I'm writing about how social media affects young people」と述べ、チューターも「that's definitely a very broad topic」と同意している。教授の指摘と合わせて、テーマが広すぎたことが推測できる。The student says, 'I'm writing about how social media affects young people,' and the tutor agrees, 'that's definitely a very broad topic.' Combined with the professor's comment, we can infer the topic was too broad.
🔑 推論問題：話者の発言から状況を推測する。「too broad」というキーワードが複数回登場することに注目。Inference: infer the situation from the speaker's words. Note that 'too broad' shows up multiple times.
4
What will the student most likely do first after this meeting?
Inference
(A) Revise the thesis statement for his research paper.
(B) Rewrite the introduction paragraph from scratch.
(C) Add more references to the bibliography section.
(D) Change the topic to something completely different.
解説：Explanation: チューターは「I'd recommend starting with the thesis」と助言し、学生は「That's really helpful」と受け入れている。面談後にまずテーゼの修正に取り組むと推測できる。The tutor advises, 'I'd recommend starting with the thesis,' and the student replies, 'That's really helpful.' We can infer the student will work on revising the thesis first after the meeting.
🔑 推論問題：「What will...most likely do」は会話の結論から次のアクションを推測する。チューターの助言＋学生の同意がヒント。Inference: 'What will...most likely do' is answered by inferring the next step from the conclusion. Tutor's advice + student's agreement is the hint.
