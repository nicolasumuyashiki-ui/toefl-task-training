💡 Conversation 攻略のコツ💡 Conversation strategy tips

📝

メモの取り方How to take notes

問題は何か？解決策は何か？をキーワードだけメモ。全文書き取ろうとしない！Note only the keywords for 'What is the problem? What is the solution?' Do not try to transcribe everything!

🔄

問題解決パターンProblem-solving pattern

挨拶→問題提示→解決策提案→結論。キャンパス会話の多くはこのパターンに従うGreeting → problem → proposed solution → conclusion. Most campus conversations follow this pattern.

😮

感情表現に注目Watch for emotion words

Oh, that's a relief! / That's frustrating. / Really? → 話者の感情は頻出の設問ポイント'Oh, that's a relief!' / 'That's frustrating.' / 'Really?' → speakers' feelings are a frequent question target.

🗣️ Conversation 1 — 予防接種の予約🗣️ Conversation 1 — Vaccination appointment

場面：キャンパス保健センター — 学生（男性）と受付（女性）Setting: Campus Health Center — Student (male) and Receptionist (female)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to a conversation.
[STUDENT] Hi, I'd like to schedule a vaccination appointment. I need to get my flu shot before the deadline.
[RECEPTIONIST] Sure. The campus health center is offering flu vaccinations every weekday this month. Do you have a preference for a day?
[STUDENT] I'm free on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Are there any available slots?
[RECEPTIONIST] Let me check. Tuesday at two-thirty is open, and Thursday at three o'clock is available as well.
[STUDENT] I'll take Tuesday at two-thirty. Do I need to bring anything?
[RECEPTIONIST] Just your student ID and your health insurance card. If you don't have insurance, the vaccination is still covered by the student health fee.
[STUDENT] Great, so there's no extra charge either way?
[RECEPTIONIST] That's correct. We'll also update your immunization record in the university system automatically after your visit.
[STUDENT] Perfect. Thank you for your help!
1
Why does the student visit the health center?
Detail
(A) To pick up a prescription from the pharmacy.
(B) To get a physical examination on campus.
(C) To register for the student health insurance plan.
(D) To schedule a flu vaccination appointment.
解説：Explanation: 学生の最初の発言で「I'd like to schedule a vaccination appointment」と明確に述べている。処方箋の受け取り(A)、健康診断(B)、保険の登録(C)はいずれも言及されていない。The student says clearly at the start, 'I'd like to schedule a vaccination appointment.' Picking up a prescription (A), a checkup (B), and insurance enrollment (C) are not mentioned.
🔑 ヒント位置：会話の冒頭（学生の1発話目）— 目的問題の答えは最初の発話に出る典型パターン。Hint location: the conversation's first line — the typical pattern where purpose questions are answered in the opening turn.
2
What will happen after the student receives the vaccination?
Detail
(A) He will need to pay a separate fee.
(B) He will need to submit paperwork to the registrar.
(C) His immunization record will be updated automatically.
(D) He will receive a follow-up phone call.
解説：Explanation: 受付係は「We'll also update your immunization record in the university system automatically」と述べている。追加料金は不要(A)と明確に否定、登録局への書類提出(B)やフォローアップ電話(D)は言及されていない。The receptionist says, 'We'll also update your immunization record in the university system automatically.' (A) — the cost is explicitly denied; (B) and (D) are not mentioned.
🔑 ヒント位置：会話の終盤（受付の最後の発言）— 「接種後に何が起こるか」は締めの部分で語られる。Hint location: near the end (the receptionist's last line) — 'what happens after the shot' is told in the wrap-up.

🗣️ Conversation 2 — 成績の不服申立て🗣️ Conversation 2 — Grade appeal

場面：学部長室 — 学生（女性）と学部長（男性）Setting: Dean's Office — Student (female) and Dean (male)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to a conversation.
[STUDENT] Thank you for seeing me, Dean Harris. I'd like to discuss the possibility of appealing my grade in Economics 201.
[DEAN] Of course. Can you explain why you believe the grade should be reconsidered?
[STUDENT] I received a C-plus on my final paper, which brought my overall grade down to a B-minus. I think there may have been a calculation error because my midterm and participation grades were both A's.
[DEAN] I see. Have you spoken with your professor about this first?
[STUDENT] Yes, I emailed Professor Wallace, but he said the grade stands because the final paper was weighted at forty percent.
[DEAN] That's an important detail. For a formal appeal, you'll need to submit a written request explaining your case, along with copies of your graded assignments. The committee reviews appeals within two weeks.
[STUDENT] Is there any chance the grade could be changed?
[DEAN] It depends on the evidence. If the committee finds a grading error, they can recommend a change. But if the professor followed the syllabus correctly, the grade will likely remain the same.
3
What can be inferred about why the student visits the dean?
Inference
(A) She believes there may be an error in her grade.
(B) She wants to transfer to a different economics class.
(C) She needs to drop a course before the deadline.
(D) She wants to request an incomplete for the semester.
解説：Explanation: 学生は「I think there may have been a calculation error」と述べ、中間・参加点がAなのに最終成績がB-だと説明。成績計算にミスがあると推測して学部長を訪れたことが分かる。The student says, 'I think there may have been a calculation error,' and explains midterm and participation are A but the final grade is B−. We can infer she came to see the dean suspecting a calculation error.
🔑 推論問題：学生の「I think there may have been...」から動機を推測する。「なぜ来たか」を行間から読み取る。Inference: infer motivation from the student's 'I think there may have been...' Read 'why she came' between the lines.
4
What can be inferred about the likely outcome of the appeal?
Inference
(A) The committee will automatically change the grade.
(B) The grade will likely stay if the syllabus was followed.
(C) The student must rewrite the final paper first.
(D) The professor will be required to meet with her.
解説：Explanation: 学部長は「if the professor followed the syllabus correctly, the grade will likely remain the same」と述べている。教授がシラバスに従っていれば成績は変わらない可能性が高いと推測できる。The dean says, 'if the professor followed the syllabus correctly, the grade will likely remain the same.' We can infer the grade is unlikely to change if the syllabus was followed.
🔑 推論問題：「likely outcome」は学部長の条件付き発言から推測。「But if...」の後の情報が結論のヒント。Inference: 'likely outcome' is inferred from the dean's conditional. Information after 'But if...' is the conclusion hint.
