🗣️ Conversation 1 — 奨学金の更新リスク

場面：Financial Aid Office ｜ 学生（男性）＋ アドバイザー（女性）

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[Student] Hi, I received an email saying my scholarship might not be renewed for next year. I'm not sure what happened.
[Advisor] Let me pull up your file. It looks like your GPA dropped below the three-point-five minimum requirement last semester.
[Student] I had a rough semester. I was dealing with some family issues, and my grades suffered because of it.
[Advisor] I understand. There is an appeal process you can go through. You'll need to write a letter explaining the circumstances and provide any supporting documentation.
[Student] What kind of documentation would I need?
[Advisor] A letter from a doctor, a counselor, or even a professor who can verify your situation. The scholarship committee reviews each case individually.
[Student] And if the appeal is approved, will I get the full scholarship amount?
[Advisor] Yes, but you'll be placed on academic probation for one semester. That means you'll need to bring your GPA back up to three-point-five by the end of the next term.
[Student] That's fair. I'm already doing much better this semester. I'll start working on the appeal letter right away.
1
Why is the student's scholarship at risk?
Detail
(A) He missed the scholarship application deadline.
(B) His GPA fell below the required minimum.
(C) He changed his major without notifying the office.
(D) The scholarship program was discontinued this year.
解説： アドバイザーが「your GPA dropped below the three-point-five minimum requirement」と明確に述べている。GPA低下が奨学金リスクの原因。
🔑 ヒント位置：会話の冒頭（アドバイザーの1発話目）— 問題提示パターンの典型。
2
What must the student do to appeal the decision?
Detail
(A) Retake the courses he failed last semester.
(B) Meet with the university president in person.
(C) Write a letter and provide supporting documentation.
(D) Pay a processing fee to the financial aid office.
解説： アドバイザーは「write a letter explaining the circumstances and provide any supporting documentation」と具体的に説明。手紙＋証拠書類の2つが必要。
🔑 解決策は会話中盤に：「What must...do」は具体的な手順を問う。アドバイザーの提案部分に注目。

🗣️ Conversation 2 — 化学実験の手順確認

場面：Chemistry Lab ｜ 学生（女性）＋ TA（男性）

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[Student] Excuse me, I'm a little confused about step three of the experiment. It says to heat the solution to sixty degrees, but how long should I keep it at that temperature?
[TA] Good question. You need to maintain it at sixty degrees for exactly ten minutes. Use the digital thermometer to monitor it.
[Student] And what happens if the temperature goes above sixty?
[TA] If it exceeds seventy degrees, the compound will break down and the experiment won't produce accurate results. So keep a close eye on it.
[Student] Got it. Also, the instructions mention adding the catalyst after heating. Should I add it all at once?
[TA] No, add it gradually — about one milliliter at a time. Stir the solution between each addition and wait thirty seconds before adding more.
[Student] That makes sense. One more thing — do I need to wear the safety goggles the entire time?
[TA] Absolutely. Keep your goggles and gloves on whenever you're working with chemicals. Safety is the top priority in this lab.
3
What does the TA imply about temperature control in the experiment?
Inference
(A) Even a small rise above the limit could ruin the results.
(B) The equipment will be damaged if overheated.
(C) The temperature does not need to be monitored closely.
(D) The experiment can be restarted easily if it overheats.
解説： TAは「If it exceeds seventy degrees, the compound will break down」「So keep a close eye on it」と述べている。わずか10度の超過で結果が台無しになることが暗示されている。(C)は逆 — 「close eye」で注意が必要と暗示。
🔑 推論問題：「So keep a close eye on it」から温度管理の重要性を推測。条件文＋注意喚起のパターン。
4
What can be inferred about the catalyst from the TA's instructions?
Inference
(A) It must be added all at once for best results.
(B) It should be added before heating the solution.
(C) It only works when the solution is at room temperature.
(D) Adding it too quickly could affect the experiment's outcome.
解説： TAは「add it gradually — about one milliliter at a time. Stir the solution between each addition and wait thirty seconds」と慎重な手順を説明。急いで加えると実験結果に影響することが推測できる。(A)は逆 — 一度に加えてはいけない。
🔑 推論問題：「gradually」「wait thirty seconds」から急いではいけないことを推測。具体的な指示から暗黙の理由を読み取る。
