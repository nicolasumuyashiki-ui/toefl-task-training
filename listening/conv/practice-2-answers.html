Conversation 攻略のコツConversation strategy tips

📝

メモの取り方How to take notes

問題は何か？解決策は何か？をキーワードだけメモ。全文書き取ろうとしない！Note only the keywords for 'What is the problem? What is the solution?' Do not try to transcribe everything!

🔄

問題解決パターンProblem-solving pattern

挨拶→問題提示→解決策提案→結論。キャンパス会話の多くはこのパターンに従うGreeting → problem → proposed solution → conclusion. Most campus conversations follow this pattern.

😮

感情表現に注目Watch for emotion words

That's actually a great idea! / Hmm, we really need... → 話者の感情は頻出の設問ポイント'That's actually a great idea!' / 'Hmm, we really need...' → speakers' feelings are a frequent question target.

Conversation 1 — Academic Advisor's Office

場面：Academic Advisor's Office ｜ 学生（男性）+ アカデミックアドバイザー（女性）Setting: Academic Advisor's Office | Student (male) + Academic advisor (female)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Hi, Professor Daniels. I was hoping to talk to you about changing my major. I'm currently in economics, but I've been thinking about switching to environmental science.
[Advisor] Of course. What's making you consider the change?
[Student] Well, I took an environmental studies elective last semester and really enjoyed it. I feel more passionate about it than my economics courses.
[Advisor] That's a good reason. However, you should know that switching at this point means you'd need to complete several prerequisite courses, including Introduction to Ecology and Earth Systems Science.
[Student] Would that push back my graduation date?
[Advisor] It could, by one semester. But there is another option. You could keep your economics major and add an environmental science minor. That way, you'd still graduate on time and get exposure to the field.
[Student] That's actually a great idea. I hadn't thought about a minor. Can I still take the upper-level environmental science courses with a minor?
[Advisor] Yes, you can take up to three upper-level courses as part of the minor. Let me print out the minor requirements for you, and you can decide by the end of the week.
1
Why does the student visit the academic advisor?
Detail
(A) To drop a course he is currently failing.
(B) To discuss changing his major to another field.
(C) To ask about study abroad opportunities.
(D) To request a letter of recommendation.
解説：Explanation: 学生は冒頭で「I was hoping to talk to you about changing my major」と明確に述べている。会話の最初の発言で目的が示される典型的なパターン。The student says at the start, 'I was hoping to talk to you about changing my major,' clearly stating the purpose. Putting the purpose in the first turn is a typical pattern.
🔑 目的問題は冒頭に答え。「I was hoping to...」が目的を示すシグナルワード。Purpose at the start. 'I was hoping to...' is the signal phrase.
2
What does the advisor suggest as an alternative?
Detail
(A) Taking a gap year to explore his interests.
(B) Transferring to a different university program.
(C) Adding an environmental science minor while keeping economics.
(D) Completing both majors at the same time.
解説：Explanation: アドバイザーは「You could keep your economics major and add an environmental science minor」と提案。専攻を維持しつつ副専攻を追加する代替案。(D)のダブルメジャーではなく、マイナー（副専攻）の追加。The advisor proposes, 'You could keep your economics major and add an environmental science minor' — keeping the major and adding a minor. This is not the double major in (D); it's adding a minor.
🔑 「But there is another option」は代替案のシグナルワード。その直後に答えがある。'But there is another option' is the alternative-suggestion signal — the answer follows it.

Conversation 2 — University Library Front Desk

場面：University Library ｜ 学生（女性）+ 司書（男性）Setting: University Library | Student (female) + Librarian (male)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Hi, I'd like to reserve a group study room for this Saturday. We have a big project presentation on Monday.
[Librarian] Sure. How many people will be in your group?
[Student] There will be five of us. We need a room with a whiteboard and a projector so we can practice our presentation.
[Librarian] Let me check availability. Unfortunately, all the large rooms with projectors are already booked for Saturday. We do have a medium-sized room available, but it only has a whiteboard — no projector.
[Student] Hmm, we really need the projector. Is there anything else available?
[Librarian] Well, there is one option. The media center on the third floor has presentation rooms that are available on a first-come, first-served basis on weekends. They have projectors and screens.
[Student] Oh, I didn't know about that. What time does the media center open on Saturdays?
[Librarian] It opens at nine AM. I'd suggest arriving early to make sure you get a room. They also have a portable speaker system you can borrow if you need to practice with audio.
3
What can be inferred about why the student needs a projector?
Inference
(A) She wants to practice her group presentation with slides.
(B) She needs to watch a recorded lecture for class.
(C) The librarian requires one for all study rooms.
(D) Her professor asked her to record the presentation.
解説：Explanation: 学生は「a big project presentation on Monday」があり「practice our presentation」するためにプロジェクターが必要と述べている。スライドを使ったプレゼン練習をしたいことが推測できる。The student mentions 'a big project presentation on Monday' and needs the projector to 'practice our presentation,' so we can infer the goal is to rehearse a slide-based presentation.
🔑 推論問題：「practice our presentation」＋プロジェクターの要求から目的を推測。Inference: infer the goal from 'practice our presentation' + the projector request.
4
What will the student most likely do on Saturday morning?
Inference
(A) Reserve a room at a different campus library.
(B) Reschedule the study session to a weekday instead.
(C) Use the medium-sized room without a projector.
(D) Arrive early at the media center to get a room.
解説：Explanation: 司書が「I'd suggest arriving early to make sure you get a room」とアドバイスし、学生が「Oh, I didn't know about that」と興味を示している。メディアセンターに早めに行くことが推測できる。The librarian advises 'I'd suggest arriving early to make sure you get a room,' and the student says 'Oh, I didn't know about that' — showing interest. We can infer the student will arrive early at the media center.
🔑 推論問題：会話の結末から次のアクションを推測。学生の反応が肯定的ならその提案に従う可能性が高い。Inference: infer the next action from the conclusion. A positive student reaction usually means they'll follow that suggestion.
