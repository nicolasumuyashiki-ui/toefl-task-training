Conversation 攻略のコツConversation strategy tips

📝

メモの取り方How to take notes

問題は何か？解決策は何か？をキーワードだけメモ。全文書き取ろうとしない！Note only the keywords for 'What is the problem? What is the solution?' Do not try to transcribe everything!

🔄

問題解決パターンProblem-solving pattern

挨拶→問題提示→解決策提案→結論。キャンパス会話の多くはこのパターンに従うGreeting → problem → proposed solution → conclusion. Most campus conversations follow this pattern.

😮

感情表現に注目Watch for emotion words

That's a problem. / That would be great. → 話者の感情は頻出の設問ポイント'That's a problem.' / 'That would be great.' → speakers' feelings are a frequent question target.

Conversation 1 — Residence Hall Office

場面：Residence Hall Office ｜ 学生（男性）+ レジデンスアドバイザー（女性）Setting: Residence Hall Office | Student (male) + Resident advisor (female)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Hi, I need to talk to someone about the noise in my building. My neighbor plays loud music almost every night until two AM.
[Advisor] I'm sorry to hear that. Have you tried talking to your neighbor directly about it?
[Student] I did, twice actually, but nothing has changed. Last night was the worst. I have an exam tomorrow and I couldn't study at all.
[Advisor] I understand your frustration. In cases like this, the first step is to file a formal noise complaint. I can help you fill out the form right now.
[Student] Will that actually make a difference? I don't want to make things awkward between us.
[Advisor] The complaint is handled confidentially. Your neighbor won't know who filed it. After we receive the complaint, we'll issue a written warning. If the behavior continues, there are further consequences, including possible room reassignment.
[Student] Okay, that sounds fair. Can I also request a temporary room change while this is being resolved? I really need to sleep properly this week.
[Advisor] Absolutely. We have a few empty rooms on the third floor. I can arrange a temporary move for you starting tonight.
1
Why does the student visit the residence advisor?
Detail
(A) To request a permanent room transfer to another building.
(B) To complain about a noisy neighbor in his building.
(C) To report a broken lock on his door.
(D) To ask about extending the quiet hours policy.
解説：Explanation: 学生の最初の発言で「I need to talk to someone about the noise in my building」と明確に述べている。(A)は永久的な部屋移動ではなく、騒音の苦情が目的。一時的な移動は後から依頼。The student says right away, 'I need to talk to someone about the noise in my building.' (A) — the goal isn't a permanent room change but the noise complaint. The temporary move is requested later.
🔑 目的問題は冒頭に答え。「I need to talk to someone about...」が目的を示すシグナル。Purpose at the start. 'I need to talk to someone about...' is the signal.
2
What does the advisor offer to do for the student?
Detail
(A) Speak directly to the noisy neighbor on his behalf.
(B) Move him to a different building permanently.
(C) Help file a complaint and arrange a temporary room change.
(D) Provide him with noise-canceling headphones to use.
解説：Explanation: アドバイザーは「I can help you fill out the form」（正式な苦情の提出）と「I can arrange a temporary move for you starting tonight」（一時的な部屋移動）の2つを提案。両方を含む(C)が正解。The advisor offers two things: 'I can help you fill out the form' (the formal complaint) and 'I can arrange a temporary move for you starting tonight' (a temporary room change). (C) covers both, so it's correct.
🔑 2つのアクションが含まれる選択肢に注目。片方だけの選択肢は不完全。Pay attention to options that include both actions; an option with only one is incomplete.

Conversation 2 — Career Services Center

場面：Career Services Center ｜ 学生（女性）+ キャリアカウンセラー（男性）Setting: Career Services Center | Student (female) + Career counselor (male)

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[Student] Hi, I'm looking for help with my resume. I'm applying for a summer internship at a marketing firm, and I want to make sure my resume stands out.
[Counselor] Great, let me take a look at what you have so far. Do you have a copy with you?
[Student] Yes, here it is. I'm worried that I don't have enough work experience since I've mostly done volunteer work and class projects.
[Counselor] Actually, that's perfectly fine for an internship application. Employers know students may not have extensive professional experience. What matters is how you present your skills and accomplishments.
[Student] So I should focus more on what I learned from those experiences?
[Counselor] Exactly. For example, your volunteer work at the community center — instead of just listing it, describe the specific campaign you organized and the results you achieved. Use numbers whenever possible.
[Student] That makes sense. Should I also include my social media project from my marketing class?
[Counselor] Definitely. That's directly relevant to the position. I'd also recommend adding a skills section at the top highlighting your proficiency in tools like Canva and Google Analytics.
3
What does the counselor imply about the student's experience?
Inference
(A) Her volunteer work and class projects are sufficient for an internship.
(B) She should get a part-time job before applying.
(C) She cannot qualify without professional work experience.
(D) She needs a recommendation letter from her professor.
解説：Explanation: カウンセラーは「that's perfectly fine for an internship application」「What matters is how you present your skills and accomplishments」と述べ、ボランティア活動やクラスプロジェクトで十分であることを暗示している。The counselor says, 'that's perfectly fine for an internship application,' 'What matters is how you present your skills and accomplishments,' implying volunteer work and class projects are enough.
🔑 推論問題：「that's perfectly fine」は肯定的な評価のシグナル。直接的には言わないが十分であることを暗示。Inference: 'that's perfectly fine' is a positive evaluation signal — implies sufficient even without saying it directly.
4
What can be inferred about how the student will revise her resume?
Inference
(A) She will apply to a different company with fewer requirements.
(B) She will get more work experience before applying.
(C) She will remove the volunteer work from the resume.
(D) She will add specific details about her skills and accomplishments.
解説：Explanation: カウンセラーは「describe the specific campaign you organized and the results you achieved」「adding a skills section」とアドバイス。学生はスキルの強調と具体的な実績を追加してレジュメを修正することが推測できる。(C)はボランティア活動を削除するのではなく、詳しく書くことを推奨。The counselor advises, 'describe the specific campaign you organized and the results you achieved,' 'adding a skills section.' We can infer the student will revise the resume by emphasizing skills and adding concrete achievements. (C) — the advice is to expand on volunteer work, not delete it.
🔑 推論問題：カウンセラーの複数のアドバイスを総合して次のアクションを推測。Inference: combine the counselor's pieces of advice to infer the next action.
