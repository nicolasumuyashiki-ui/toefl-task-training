Talk 1 — How Birds Navigate During Migration (Life Science)

話者：教授 ｜ Standard

スクリプトを見る（シグナルワードをハイライト付き）
Today I'd like to discuss something truly remarkable — how birds navigate during migration. Every year, millions of birds travel thousands of kilometers between their breeding and wintering grounds, and scientists have long wondered how they find their way so accurately.

It turns out that birds rely on several different navigation tools. One of the most important is the Earth's magnetic field. Research has shown that certain birds have tiny particles of magnetite in their beaks, which act like a built-in compass. In addition to this magnetic sense, birds also use the position of the sun during the day and the stars at night to orient themselves.

Perhaps most surprisingly, some species can even detect patterns of polarized light in the sky, which remain visible even on cloudy days. Young birds on their first migration often follow experienced adults, but experiments have demonstrated that many species are born with an innate sense of direction — they know which way to fly even without guidance.
1
What is the talk mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) The dangers birds face during migration.
(B) The methods birds use to navigate during migration.
(C) The reasons why birds migrate seasonally.
(D) The effects of climate change on bird migration.
解説： 冒頭で「how birds navigate during migration」と主題を明示。磁場・太陽・星・偏光など複数のナビゲーション手段を説明。(A)危険性、(C)渡りの理由、(D)気候変動は言及なし。
🔑 Main Idea問題：「Today I'd like to discuss...」の直後がトピック。
2
Why does the speaker mention magnetite in birds' beaks?
Purpose
(A) To explain how birds detect the Earth's magnetic field.
(B) To describe how birds find food during migration.
(C) To compare bird navigation with human technology.
(D) To show why some birds cannot migrate.
解説： 講師は「tiny particles of magnetite in their beaks, which act like a built-in compass」と述べ、地磁気を感知する仕組みの具体例として言及。(B)食料探し、(C)人間技術との比較、(D)渡りができない鳥は言及なし。
🔑 Purpose問題：magnetiteは磁場感知の具体的メカニズムとして紹介されている。
3
According to the speaker, what do some birds use on cloudy days?
Detail
(A) Sound waves from the ocean.
(B) Landmarks on the ground.
(C) Patterns of polarized light.
(D) Signals from other birds.
解説： 講師は「some species can even detect patterns of polarized light in the sky, which remain visible even on cloudy days」と明確に述べている。(A)(B)(D)は言及なし。
🔑 Detail問題：「even on cloudy days」がキーフレーズ。直前の情報が答え。
4
What can be inferred about young birds' navigation ability?
Inference
(A) They must learn entirely from adult birds.
(B) They cannot migrate without a group.
(C) They only use the sun for navigation.
(D) They have some navigation skills from birth.
解説： 講師は「many species are born with an innate sense of direction — they know which way to fly even without guidance」と述べている。生まれながらにナビゲーション能力を持つ。(A)「entirely」は不正確、(B)(C)は言及なし。
🔑 Inference問題：「innate（生まれつきの）」がキーワード。直接的な言い換えを選ぶ。

Talk 2 — The Impact of the Printing Press on European Society (History)

話者：教授 ｜ Harder

スクリプトを見る（シグナルワードをハイライト付き）
Let's turn our attention to one of the most transformative inventions in human history — the printing press. When Johannes Gutenberg developed his movable-type printing press around 1440, he probably had no idea how profoundly it would change European society.

Before the printing press, books were copied by hand, mostly by monks in monasteries. This process was incredibly slow and expensive, which meant that books were rare luxury items. Gutenberg's invention changed all of that. Suddenly, it became possible to produce hundreds of copies of a book in the time it previously took to make just one.

The first major impact was on literacy. As books became cheaper and more widely available, more people had the opportunity to learn to read. This was especially significant because, for the first time, knowledge was no longer controlled exclusively by religious institutions.

The printing press also played a crucial role in the spread of new ideas. Martin Luther's famous Ninety-Five Theses, for example, were printed and distributed across Europe within weeks — something that would have been impossible before Gutenberg.

Scientists, too, benefited enormously. Researchers could now share their findings quickly and accurately, which accelerated the pace of scientific discovery. However, not everyone welcomed the printing press. Some religious leaders feared that widespread access to books would lead to the spread of dangerous or heretical ideas. Despite this opposition, the printing press continued to transform society, laying the foundation for the modern information age.
5
What is the talk mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) The biography of Johannes Gutenberg.
(B) The role of monks in preserving knowledge.
(C) The history of book manufacturing techniques.
(D) How the printing press transformed European society.
解説： 講義全体が印刷機がヨーロッパ社会に与えた影響（識字率、思想の普及、科学の発展）を説明。(A)グーテンベルクの伝記ではない、(B)修道士は背景情報、(C)製本技術全般ではない。
🔑 Main Idea問題：「Let's turn our attention to...」で導入されたトピックが答え。
6
According to the speaker, what happened to literacy after the printing press was introduced?
Detail
(A) It declined because books were too complicated.
(B) It increased as books became more affordable.
(C) It remained limited to religious institutions.
(D) It was restricted by government regulations.
解説： 講師は「As books became cheaper and more widely available, more people had the opportunity to learn to read」と述べている。(A)低下は逆、(C)宗教機関に限定されなくなった、(D)政府規制は言及なし。
🔑 Detail問題：因果関係「books became cheaper → more people learned to read」を聞き取る。
7
Why does the speaker mention Martin Luther's Ninety-Five Theses?
Purpose
(A) To illustrate how the printing press helped spread new ideas.
(B) To explain the origins of the Reformation.
(C) To compare religious and scientific writing.
(D) To describe Gutenberg's personal beliefs.
解説： ルターの95ヶ条の論題が「printed and distributed across Europe within weeks」されたことを、印刷機が新しいアイデアの普及を助けた具体例として挙げている。(B)宗教改革の起源の説明が目的ではない。
🔑 Purpose問題：「for example」のシグナルワードが具体例であることを示す。
8
Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a result of the printing press?
NOT/EXCEPT
(A) Faster sharing of scientific discoveries.
(B) Increased literacy rates.
(C) The establishment of public libraries.
(D) Opposition from some religious leaders.
解説： 講師が印刷機の結果として挙げたのは、科学的発見の共有の加速(A)、識字率の向上(B)、宗教指導者の反対(D)。公共図書館の設立(C)は言及なし。
🔑 NOT/EXCEPT問題の鉄則：消去法で解く。本文に言及のある3つを消し、残った1つが正解。
