Talk 1 — The Psychology of Color in Marketing (Social Science)

話者：教授 ｜ Standard

スクリプトを見る（シグナルワードをハイライト付き）
Today we're going to look at how color influences consumer behavior. This is a key concept in marketing psychology. Research shows that up to 90 percent of snap judgments about products can be based on color alone.

For example, red is often used to create a sense of urgency, which is why you frequently see it in clearance sales. Blue, on the other hand, is associated with trust and reliability. That's the reason many banks and technology companies use blue in their logos. Green tends to signal health and nature, making it a popular choice for organic food brands.

However, it's important to understand that color associations are not universal. They can vary significantly across cultures. In some Asian countries, for instance, white is linked to mourning rather than purity. So when companies expand internationally, they need to carefully reconsider their color choices to avoid sending the wrong message to potential customers.
1
What is the talk mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) The history of advertising techniques.
(B) Cultural differences in business practices.
(C) Why certain companies are more successful than others.
(D) How colors affect consumer decisions in marketing.
解説： 冒頭で「how color influences consumer behavior」と主題を明示。マーケティングにおける色の心理的効果を全体で説明。(A)広告技術の歴史、(B)文化の違いは部分的、(C)企業の成功要因全般ではない。
🔑 Main Idea問題：「Today we're going to look at...」が主旨のシグナル。
2
According to the speaker, why do many banks use the color blue?
Detail
(A) Because blue is associated with trust and reliability.
(B) Because blue is the cheapest color to produce.
(C) Because government regulations require it.
(D) Because customers prefer blue over all other colors.
解説： 講師は「Blue...is associated with trust and reliability. That's the reason many banks and technology companies use blue」と明確に述べている。シグナルワード「That's the reason...」が因果関係を示す。
🔑 Detail問題：「That's the reason...」のシグナルが因果関係の答えを直接示す。
3
Why does the speaker mention Asian countries?
Purpose
(A) To argue that Asian markets are the most important.
(B) To show that color meanings can differ across cultures.
(C) To explain why Asian companies are successful.
(D) To compare Asian and European advertising budgets.
解説： 講師は「color associations are not universal. They can vary significantly across cultures」と述べた後、アジアの例を挙げている。「For instance,...」が具体例のシグナル。
🔑 Purpose問題：「for instance」が具体例の導入。前の一般論（色の意味は文化で異なる）を裏付ける例として使用。
4
What can be inferred from the talk?
Inference
(A) A single color strategy works effectively in every country.
(B) Red is the most effective color for all types of marketing.
(C) Companies should research local color preferences before entering new markets.
(D) Color has no real impact on purchasing decisions.
解説： 講師は「companies expand internationally, they need to carefully reconsider their color choices」と述べている。新市場参入前に色の好みを調査すべきだと推論できる。(A)単一戦略は不適、(B)赤は緊急性に効果的だが万能ではない、(D)色は影響があると述べている。
🔑 Inference問題：最後の文に結論がある。「need to carefully reconsider」が示唆の核心。

Talk 2 — Ancient Egyptian Agriculture Along the Nile (History)

話者：教授 ｜ Harder

スクリプトを見る（シグナルワードをハイライト付き）
Let's turn our attention to one of the earliest and most successful agricultural systems in human history — farming along the Nile River in ancient Egypt. The Nile was absolutely central to Egyptian civilization, and the key to understanding why lies in its annual flooding cycle.

Each year, typically between June and September, heavy rains in the Ethiopian highlands would cause the Nile to overflow its banks. When the floodwaters eventually receded, they left behind a layer of rich, dark silt that was incredibly fertile. The Egyptians called this fertile land "kemet," meaning "the black land," in contrast to "deshret," the red, barren desert beyond the river valley.

Now, the Egyptians didn't simply wait for the floods and hope for the best. They developed an elaborate system of irrigation canals and basins to control and distribute the floodwaters more effectively. The main crops grown were emmer wheat and barley, which were used to make bread and beer — the staples of the Egyptian diet. Flax was also cultivated extensively for making linen cloth.

What's particularly remarkable is how this agricultural system supported one of the most advanced civilizations of the ancient world for over three thousand years. The surplus food production allowed the development of specialized crafts, monumental architecture, and a complex social hierarchy. In other words, it was the Nile's predictable flooding and the Egyptian farmers' ingenuity that made possible everything from the pyramids to hieroglyphics.
5
What is the talk mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) The construction of the Egyptian pyramids.
(B) Trade routes in the ancient world.
(C) The discovery of hieroglyphics.
(D) How ancient Egyptian farming along the Nile sustained a civilization.
解説： 冒頭で「farming along the Nile River」と述べ、ナイル川沿いの農業が文明をどう支えたかを全体で説明。(A)ピラミッドは末尾で軽く触れるだけ、(B)(C)は言及なし。
🔑 Main Idea問題：「Let's turn our attention to...」がトピック導入のシグナル。
6
According to the speaker, what did the term "kemet" refer to?
Detail
(A) The barren desert surrounding the Nile.
(B) A type of crop grown by Egyptian farmers.
(C) The fertile black land left by the Nile's floods.
(D) An ancient Egyptian irrigation tool.
解説： 講師は「The Egyptians called this fertile land 'kemet,' meaning 'the black land'」と明確に定義。「meaning...」が言い換え・定義のシグナル。(A)砂漠は「deshret」、(B)(D)は不正確。
🔑 定義問題：「called X, meaning...」のパターンで導入される。meaningの後が定義そのもの。
7
Why does the speaker mention bread and beer?
Purpose
(A) To explain what the main crops were used for.
(B) To describe Egyptian religious ceremonies.
(C) To compare Egyptian and Greek diets.
(D) To argue that Egyptians had unhealthy eating habits.
解説： 講師は「emmer wheat and barley, which were used to make bread and beer — the staples of the Egyptian diet」と述べている。主要作物の用途を説明するために言及。(B)(C)(D)は言及なし。
🔑 Purpose問題：パンとビールは小麦と大麦の「用途」の具体例として提示されている。
8
What can be inferred about the relationship between agriculture and Egyptian civilization?
Inference
(A) Agriculture played only a minor role in Egyptian society.
(B) Food surpluses from farming enabled cultural and architectural achievements.
(C) The pyramids were built before farming began along the Nile.
(D) Egyptian farmers refused to use irrigation technology.
解説： 講師は「surplus food production allowed the development of specialized crafts, monumental architecture, and a complex social hierarchy」と述べている。農業の余剰がピラミッドやヒエログリフを可能にした。(A)minor roleではない、(C)農業が先、(D)灌漑を開発した。
🔑 Inference問題：「In other words,...」のシグナルで要約。農業の余剰→文化的成果という因果を推論。
