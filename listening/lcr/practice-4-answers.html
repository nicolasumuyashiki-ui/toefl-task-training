💡 LCR 攻略のコツ💡 LCR strategy tips
1
最初の2語に全集中 — Do you→Yes/No、What→Wh疑問文、Could you→依頼、How about→提案Focus on the first 2 words — Do you→Yes/No, What→Wh-question, Could you→request, How about→suggestion
2
質問タイプを判断 — Yes/No、5W1H、依頼、提案、確認など。タイプに合う応答パターンを予測Identify the question type — Yes/No, 5W1H, request, suggestion, confirmation, etc. Predict the matching response pattern.
3
自然な応答を選ぶ — 「直接的すぎる答え」は疑う。会話を前に進める選択肢＝正解Pick the natural-sounding reply — Doubt "too direct" answers. The choice that moves the conversation forward is usually correct.
4
否定形に注意 — Isn't...? / Don't you...? は確認の意味。意味を逆に取らないWatch for negatives — Isn't...? / Don't you...? are confirmations. Don't flip the meaning.
