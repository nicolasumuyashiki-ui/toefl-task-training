Announcement 1

Setting: Campus Dining Services — Dining Services Manager
[Narrator] Listen to an announcement from campus dining services.

Good morning, everyone. Dining Services is happy to announce that we will be opening a brand-new food court in the west wing of the student center starting next Monday. The new food court will feature five different stations, including a salad bar, a grill station, an Asian cuisine counter, a sandwich and wrap bar, and a dessert station. Operating hours will be from eleven a.m. to eight p.m., Monday through Friday. One of the most exciting features is our new mobile ordering system. You can place your order through the campus dining app up to thirty minutes in advance and pick it up at the express window without waiting in line. All meal plan options will be accepted at every station, and students paying with their campus card will receive a ten-percent discount during the first two weeks. If you have any dietary concerns or food allergy questions, each station will have ingredient information posted on digital screens above the counter.

Q1. What is the announcement mainly about?

A) Changes to the campus meal plan pricing.
B) The opening of a new food court in the student center.
C) A new campus dining app for delivery orders.
D) Extended dining hall hours during finals week.

Q2. Why does the speaker mention the campus dining app?

A) To describe how students can order food in advance and skip the line.
B) To announce a new delivery service on campus.
C) To explain the new meal plan pricing structure.
D) To promote a partnership with a food delivery company.

Announcement 2

Setting: Campus Security Office — Director of Campus Safety
[Narrator] Listen to an announcement from the campus safety office.

Attention all students, faculty, and staff. This is an important update from the Office of Campus Safety regarding the new emergency notification system that will go into effect starting this Friday. The university has upgraded its alert system to include text messages, push notifications through the campus app, and automated phone calls in addition to the existing email alerts. In the event of a campus emergency, such as severe weather, a security threat, or a building evacuation, you will receive an immediate notification through all registered channels. To make sure you receive these alerts, please verify that your contact information is up to date in the student portal by this Thursday. You can update your phone number and add up to two emergency contacts. We will be conducting a campus-wide test of the system this Friday at noon, so please do not be alarmed when you receive a test message. If you do not receive the test alert, contact the campus safety office so we can troubleshoot the issue.

Q3. When will the campus-wide test of the new system take place?

A) This Thursday at noon.
B) Next Monday at nine AM.
C) This Wednesday at three PM.
D) This Friday at noon.

Q4. What can be inferred about the university's previous alert system?

A) It was completely unreliable and never worked properly.
B) It only sent alerts to faculty and staff members.
C) It had fewer notification channels than the upgraded system.
D) It was recently involved in a major security failure.
