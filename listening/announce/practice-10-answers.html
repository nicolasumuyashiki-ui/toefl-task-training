💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ💡 Announcement strategy tips

🎯

4つの焦点で聞くListen for the four W's

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さないDon't miss WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE.

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答えPurpose answers come at the start

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！The answer to 'What is the announcement about?' is in the first one or two sentences. Focus on the opening!

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾Recommendations come at the end

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多いThe answer to 'What does the speaker recommend?' is often hidden in the last line.

📢 Announcement 1 — 新フードコート開設📢 Announcement 1 — New food court opens

場面：キャンパスダイニングサービス ｜ 話者：ダイニングサービスマネージャーSetting: Campus dining services | Speaker: Dining services manager

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement from campus dining services.
[SPEAKER] Good morning, everyone. Dining Services is happy to announce that we will be opening a brand-new food court in the west wing of the student center starting next Monday. The new food court will feature five different stations, including a salad bar, a grill station, an Asian cuisine counter, a sandwich and wrap bar, and a dessert station. Operating hours will be from eleven a.m. to eight p.m., Monday through Friday. One of the most exciting features is our new mobile ordering system. You can place your order through the campus dining app up to thirty minutes in advance and pick it up at the express window without waiting in line. All meal plan options will be accepted at every station, and students paying with their campus card will receive a ten-percent discount during the first two weeks. If you have any dietary concerns or food allergy questions, each station will have ingredient information posted on digital screens above the counter.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) Changes to the campus meal plan pricing.
(B) The opening of a new food court in the student center.
(C) A new campus dining app for delivery orders.
(D) Extended dining hall hours during finals week.
解説：Explanation: マネージャーは冒頭で「we will be opening a brand-new food court in the west wing of the student center」と述べており、新しいフードコートの開設がアナウンスの主旨。(A)はミールプラン価格変更は主題ではない。(C)はモバイル注文は機能の一つでありデリバリーサービスではない。(D)は期末試験期間の時間延長は言及されていない。The manager opens with 'we will be opening a brand-new food court in the west wing of the student center' — opening a new food court is the main point. (A) meal plan price changes aren't the theme. (C) mobile ordering is just one feature, not delivery. (D) extended hours during finals aren't mentioned.
🔑 冒頭の「happy to announce that...opening a brand-new food court」が主旨。mobile ordering は詳細にすぎないので(C)は不正解。Main point: 'happy to announce that...opening a brand-new food court.' Mobile ordering is a detail, so (C) is wrong.
2
Why does the speaker mention the campus dining app?
Purpose
(A) To describe how students can order food in advance and skip the line.
(B) To announce a new delivery service on campus.
(C) To explain the new meal plan pricing structure.
(D) To promote a partnership with a food delivery company.
解説：Explanation: スピーカーは「You can place your order through the campus dining app up to thirty minutes in advance and pick it up at the express window without waiting in line」と述べている。アプリの言及は、事前注文で行列を回避できる便利な機能を説明するため。(B)はデリバリーサービスではなく、ピックアップ方式。(C)はミールプラン価格の説明ではない。(D)は外部企業との提携は言及なし。The speaker says, 'You can place your order through the campus dining app up to thirty minutes in advance and pick it up at the express window without waiting in line.' The app mention is to explain a convenient feature for skipping lines via pre-ordering. (B) it's pickup, not delivery. (C) it's not an explanation of meal plan prices. (D) external partnerships aren't mentioned.
🔑 目的問題：「place your order through the campus dining app...pick it up at the express window without waiting in line」が手がかり。アプリへの言及＝事前注文による待ち時間短縮を説明するため。Purpose question: 'place your order through the campus dining app...pick it up at the express window without waiting in line' is the cue. Mention of the app = explaining how pre-ordering shortens wait time.

📢 Announcement 2 — 緊急通知システムのアップグレード📢 Announcement 2 — Emergency alert system upgrade

場面：キャンパス安全管理室 ｜ 話者：キャンパス安全担当ディレクターSetting: Campus safety office | Speaker: Director of campus safety

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement from the campus safety office.
[SPEAKER] Attention all students, faculty, and staff. This is an important update from the Office of Campus Safety regarding the new emergency notification system that will go into effect starting this Friday. The university has upgraded its alert system to include text messages, push notifications through the campus app, and automated phone calls in addition to the existing email alerts. In the event of a campus emergency, such as severe weather, a security threat, or a building evacuation, you will receive an immediate notification through all registered channels. To make sure you receive these alerts, please verify that your contact information is up to date in the student portal by this Thursday. You can update your phone number and add up to two emergency contacts. We will be conducting a campus-wide test of the system this Friday at noon, so please do not be alarmed when you receive a test message. If you do not receive the test alert, contact the campus safety office so we can troubleshoot the issue.
3
When will the campus-wide test of the new system take place?
Detail
(A) This Thursday at noon.
(B) Next Monday at nine AM.
(C) This Wednesday at three PM.
(D) This Friday at noon.
解説：Explanation: 「We will be conducting a campus-wide test of the system this Friday at noon」と明確に述べられている。(A)は木曜日は連絡先情報の更新期限であり、テストの日ではない。(B)(C)は言及されていない日時。Clearly stated: 'We will be conducting a campus-wide test of the system this Friday at noon.' (A) Thursday is the deadline for updating contact info, not the test. (B) (C) are unmentioned dates.
🔑 WHEN問題：「this Friday at noon」が根拠。「by this Thursday」（連絡先更新期限）と「this Friday at noon」（テスト実施）を混同しないように。WHEN question: 'this Friday at noon' is the basis. Don't confuse 'by this Thursday' (deadline to update contacts) with 'this Friday at noon' (test).
4
What can be inferred about the university's previous alert system?
Inference
(A) It was completely unreliable and never worked properly.
(B) It only sent alerts to faculty and staff members.
(C) It had fewer notification channels than the upgraded system.
(D) It was recently involved in a major security failure.
解説：Explanation: スピーカーは「The university has upgraded its alert system to include text messages, push notifications through the campus app, and automated phone calls in addition to the existing email alerts」と述べている。「in addition to the existing email alerts」から、以前のシステムはメールアラートのみだったと推論できる。つまり、アップグレード前は通知チャネルが少なかった。(A)は「completely unreliable」とまでは述べていない。(B)は教職員のみとは述べていない。(D)はセキュリティ上の重大な失敗は言及なし。The speaker says, 'The university has upgraded its alert system to include text messages, push notifications through the campus app, and automated phone calls in addition to the existing email alerts.' From 'in addition to the existing email alerts,' we infer the previous system was email-only. So before the upgrade there were fewer notification channels. (A) not described as 'completely unreliable.' (B) faculty-only isn't stated. (D) major security failures aren't mentioned.
🔑 推論問題：「in addition to the existing email alerts」が手がかり。「existing」= 以前からあった。新たにSMS・プッシュ通知・自動電話を追加 = 以前はチャネルが少なかった。Inference: 'in addition to the existing email alerts' is the cue. 'existing' = was already there. New SMS/push/automated calls added = previously fewer channels.
