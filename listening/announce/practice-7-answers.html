💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ

🎯

4つの焦点で聞く

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さない

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答え

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多い

📢 Announcement 1 — 研究アシスタント募集

場面：教室 ｜ 話者：教授

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a classroom.
[SPEAKER] Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. The biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants to join Professor Chen's lab starting next month. The position involves helping with data collection for a study on freshwater ecosystems. You'll work about ten hours per week, and it counts as an independent study credit. To apply, you'll need to submit a short statement of interest and your transcript to the department office by next Friday. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on research experience, especially for those of you considering graduate school.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) A change in the lecture schedule.
(B) A research assistant position opening.
(C) A new course being offered next semester.
(D) A scholarship opportunity for biology students.
解説： 教授は「the biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants」と明確に述べている。(A)講義スケジュールの変更は言及されていない。(C)新規コースの提供ではなく、研究アシスタントの募集。(D)奨学金については話題に出ていない。
🔑 冒頭の「I have an exciting announcement」の直後に主旨。「looking for...research assistants」= A research assistant position opening。
2
What do students need to submit to apply?
Detail
(A) A resume and two recommendation letters.
(B) A research proposal and lab report.
(C) A statement of interest and transcript.
(D) A completed application form and interview.
解説： 「submit a short statement of interest and your transcript to the department office」と明確に述べられている。(A)履歴書や推薦状は要求されていない。(B)研究提案書やラボレポートは言及されていない。(D)申請書や面接は言及されていない。
🔑 「To apply, you'll need to submit...」の直後が応募要件。「statement of interest and transcript」の2点。

📢 Announcement 2 — 自転車盗難防止の注意喚起

場面：キャンパス安全情報 ｜ 話者：キャンパス警察

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to a campus safety announcement.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon. This is a message from the campus police department regarding bicycle safety. Over the past month, we've received twelve reports of stolen bicycles on campus. Most of these thefts occurred near the east parking lot and the dormitory bike racks. To protect your bicycle, we strongly recommend using a U-lock rather than a cable lock, as cable locks can be easily cut. Always lock your bike to a designated rack, not to trees or handrails. We are also offering free bike registration at the campus police station. Registering your bike makes it much easier for us to recover it if it's stolen. Drop by our office Monday through Friday between nine and five to register yours.
3
What is the main purpose of this announcement?
Main Idea
(A) To announce new bicycle parking areas.
(B) To report an arrest related to bike theft.
(C) To introduce a campus bike-sharing program.
(D) To give tips on preventing bicycle theft.
解説： アナウンス全体が自転車盗難の報告と予防策の提示について。Uロックの推奨、登録サービスなど具体的な予防策を述べている。(A)新しい駐輪場の発表ではない。(B)逮捕報告ではなく、予防策の提示。(C)自転車シェアプログラムは言及されていない。
🔑 アナウンス全体の趣旨を把握する。盗難報告→予防策→登録サービスの流れ = giving tips on preventing bicycle theft。
4
What free service does the campus police offer?
Detail
(A) Bike registration.
(B) Bike repair and maintenance.
(C) Free U-locks for students.
(D) Bicycle safety classes.
解説： 「We are also offering free bike registration at the campus police station」と明確に述べられている。(B)自転車の修理サービスは言及されていない。(C)無料のUロック配布は言及されていない（推奨のみ）。(D)自転車安全講座は言及されていない。
🔑 「offering free bike registration」が根拠。「free」がキーワード — 無料サービスを問う問題。
