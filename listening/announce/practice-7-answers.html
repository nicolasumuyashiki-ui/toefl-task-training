💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ💡 Announcement strategy tips

🎯

4つの焦点で聞くListen for the four W's

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さないDon't miss WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE.

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答えPurpose answers come at the start

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！The answer to 'What is the announcement about?' is in the first one or two sentences. Focus on the opening!

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾Recommendations come at the end

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多いThe answer to 'What does the speaker recommend?' is often hidden in the last line.

📢 Announcement 1 — 研究アシスタント募集📢 Announcement 1 — Research assistant openings

場面：教室 ｜ 話者：教授Setting: Classroom | Speaker: Professor

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a classroom.
[SPEAKER] Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. The biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants to join Professor Chen's lab starting next month. The position involves helping with data collection for a study on freshwater ecosystems. You'll work about ten hours per week, and it counts as an independent study credit. To apply, you'll need to submit a short statement of interest and your transcript to the department office by next Friday. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on research experience, especially for those of you considering graduate school.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) A change in the lecture schedule.
(B) A research assistant position opening.
(C) A new course being offered next semester.
(D) A scholarship opportunity for biology students.
解説：Explanation: 教授は「the biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants」と明確に述べている。(A)講義スケジュールの変更は言及されていない。(C)新規コースの提供ではなく、研究アシスタントの募集。(D)奨学金については話題に出ていない。The professor says clearly, 'the biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants.' (A) lecture schedule changes aren't mentioned. (C) it's not a new course offering — it's a research assistant search. (D) scholarships aren't mentioned.
🔑 冒頭の「I have an exciting announcement」の直後に主旨。「looking for...research assistants」= A research assistant position opening。Main point right after 'I have an exciting announcement.' 'looking for...research assistants' = a research assistant position opening.
2
Why does the professor mention graduate school?
Purpose
(A) To announce a new graduate program in biology.
(B) To require students to apply for graduate school.
(C) To highlight the value of the research experience for future studies.
(D) To explain the prerequisites for the position.
解説：Explanation: 教授は最後に「This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on research experience, especially for those of you considering graduate school」と述べている。大学院進学を考えている学生にとって研究経験が貴重であることを強調するため。(A)新しい大学院プログラムの発表ではない。(B)大学院への応募を要求しているのではない。(D)ポジションの前提条件の説明ではない。The professor closes with, 'This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on research experience, especially for those of you considering graduate school.' Stresses how valuable research experience is for grad-school-bound students. (A) it's not a new grad program announcement. (B) doesn't require applying to grad school. (D) doesn't explain prerequisites.
🔑 目的問題：「especially for those of you considering graduate school」が手がかり。大学院進学を見据えた研究経験の価値を強調している。Purpose question: 'especially for those of you considering graduate school' is the cue. Stresses the value of research experience for grad-school applicants.

📢 Announcement 2 — 自転車盗難防止の注意喚起📢 Announcement 2 — Bicycle theft prevention notice

場面：キャンパス安全情報 ｜ 話者：キャンパス警察Setting: Campus safety information | Speaker: Campus police

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to a campus safety announcement.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon. This is a message from the campus police department regarding bicycle safety. Over the past month, we've received twelve reports of stolen bicycles on campus. Most of these thefts occurred near the east parking lot and the dormitory bike racks. To protect your bicycle, we strongly recommend using a U-lock rather than a cable lock, as cable locks can be easily cut. Always lock your bike to a designated rack, not to trees or handrails. We are also offering free bike registration at the campus police station. Registering your bike makes it much easier for us to recover it if it's stolen. Drop by our office Monday through Friday between nine and five to register yours.
3
What type of lock does the campus police recommend?
Detail
(A) A cable lock with a combination.
(B) A chain lock with a padlock.
(C) An electronic smart lock.
(D) A U-lock.
解説：Explanation: 「we strongly recommend using a U-lock rather than a cable lock, as cable locks can be easily cut」と明確に述べられている。(A)ケーブルロックは切られやすいため推奨されていない。(B)チェーンロックは言及されていない。(C)電子ロックは言及されていない。Clearly stated: 'we strongly recommend using a U-lock rather than a cable lock, as cable locks can be easily cut.' (A) cable locks are not recommended. (B) chain locks aren't mentioned. (C) electronic locks aren't mentioned.
🔑 「strongly recommend using a U-lock」が根拠。「rather than a cable lock」でケーブルロックとの対比も明確。'strongly recommend using a U-lock' is the basis. The contrast with cable locks via 'rather than' is also clear.
4
What can be inferred about the bicycle theft problem on campus?
Inference
(A) It is a growing concern that requires preventive action.
(B) The thefts only occur during nighttime hours.
(C) The campus police have identified the suspects.
(D) Students are not interested in registering their bicycles.
解説：Explanation: 「Over the past month, we've received twelve reports of stolen bicycles」という報告に加え、Uロックの推奨や無料登録サービスの提供など複数の対策を講じていることから、自転車盗難が深刻化しており予防措置が必要だと推論できる。(B)夜間のみとは述べられていない。(C)容疑者の特定は言及されていない。(D)学生の関心については言及されていない。On top of 'Over the past month, we've received twelve reports of stolen bicycles,' multiple measures (U-lock recommendation, free registration) are taken — so we infer bike theft has gotten serious and prevention is needed. (B) night-only isn't stated. (C) suspect identification isn't mentioned. (D) student interest isn't mentioned.
🔑 推論問題：「twelve reports」+「strongly recommend」+「free bike registration」の3点から、問題が深刻化しており対策が必要と推論。Inference: from 'twelve reports' + 'strongly recommend' + 'free bike registration,' infer the issue has worsened and prevention is needed.
