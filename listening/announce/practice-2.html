Announcement 1

Setting: Classroom — Professor
Good afternoon, class. I have an exciting announcement. Our department has arranged a field trip to the local history museum next Thursday, November 7th. We'll meet in front of the science building at 9 AM, and the bus will leave at 9:15 sharp. The museum visit is directly related to our current unit on cultural artifacts, so I expect everyone to attend. Please bring a notebook, as you'll need to complete a short observation worksheet during the visit. If you have any scheduling conflicts, please let me know by this Friday so I can make alternative arrangements.

Q1. What is the announcement mainly about?

A) A change in the classroom schedule
B) A field trip to a local museum
C) A new assignment for the class
D) A guest lecture on cultural artifacts

Q2. What should students do if they cannot attend?

A) Submit a written report instead
B) Email the department secretary
C) Inform the professor by Friday
D) Arrange their own transportation

Announcement 2

Setting: Campus Radio / Security Notice
Attention all students and staff. The campus security office would like to remind everyone about the new bicycle registration requirement that goes into effect starting December 1st. All bicycles parked on campus must display a valid registration sticker. You can register your bicycle for free at the security office in Building C, Monday through Friday, from 8 AM to 5 PM. Unregistered bicycles found on campus after December 15th may be removed. This policy is being introduced to reduce bicycle theft and help recover stolen bikes more quickly.

Q3. What is the purpose of the new policy?

A) To limit the number of bicycles on campus
B) To generate revenue for campus improvements
C) To promote environmental awareness
D) To reduce bicycle theft and aid recovery

Q4. Where can students register their bicycles?

A) At the security office in Building C
B) At the student union front desk
C) Online through the campus portal
D) At any campus parking lot
