Announcement 1

Setting: Classroom — Professor
Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. The biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants to join Professor Chen's lab starting next month. The position involves helping with data collection for a study on freshwater ecosystems. You'll work about ten hours per week, and it counts as an independent study credit. To apply, you'll need to submit a short statement of interest and your transcript to the department office by next Friday. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on research experience, especially for those of you considering graduate school.

Q1. What is the announcement mainly about?

A) A change in the lecture schedule
B) A research assistant position opening
C) A new course being offered next semester
D) A scholarship opportunity for biology students

Q2. What do students need to submit to apply?

A) A resume and two recommendation letters
B) A research proposal and lab report
C) A statement of interest and transcript
D) A completed application form and interview

Announcement 2

Setting: Campus Safety Bulletin — Campus Police
Good afternoon. This is a message from the campus police department regarding bicycle safety. Over the past month, we've received twelve reports of stolen bicycles on campus. Most of these thefts occurred near the east parking lot and the dormitory bike racks. To protect your bicycle, we strongly recommend using a U-lock rather than a cable lock, as cable locks can be easily cut. Always lock your bike to a designated rack, not to trees or handrails. We are also offering free bike registration at the campus police station. Registering your bike makes it much easier for us to recover it if it's stolen. Drop by our office Monday through Friday between nine and five to register yours.

Q3. What is the main purpose of this announcement?

A) To announce new bicycle parking areas
B) To report an arrest related to bike theft
C) To introduce a campus bike-sharing program
D) To give tips on preventing bicycle theft

Q4. What free service does the campus police offer?

A) Bike registration
B) Bike repair and maintenance
C) Free U-locks for students
D) Bicycle safety classes
