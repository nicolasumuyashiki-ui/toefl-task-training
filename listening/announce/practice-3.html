Announcement 1

Setting: Library Orientation Session — Librarian
Good afternoon, everyone. I'm pleased to announce that the university library has just acquired access to three major academic databases: JSTOR, ScienceDirect, and ProQuest. These databases contain millions of peer-reviewed journal articles, and they are now available to all enrolled students free of charge. To access them, simply log in to the library website using your student credentials. Additionally, we will be holding a series of research workshops starting next Monday. The first session will cover how to use these databases effectively to find sources for your research papers. Workshops will run every Monday and Wednesday at three PM in Room 204 of the library. No registration is required — just show up.

Q1. What is the announcement mainly about?

A) A change in library opening hours
B) New database access and upcoming research workshops
C) A renovation of the library building
D) A new library card system for students

Q2. What do students need to do to attend the workshops?

A) Register online at least one week in advance
B) Pay a small participation fee
C) Bring their own laptop computer
D) Simply show up — no registration is required

Announcement 2

Setting: University-wide Email / Campus Broadcast — Dean of Students
Attention all students and faculty. Due to the severe winter storm warning issued for this area, the university has decided to make several changes to tomorrow's schedule. All classes before noon have been canceled. Afternoon classes will proceed as scheduled, but they will be held remotely via the university's online learning platform. The campus dining halls will operate on a limited schedule, opening only from eleven AM to seven PM. All evening events, including the guest lecture in the auditorium and the basketball game, have been postponed to next week. Please check your university email for updates, and stay safe.

Q3. Why are schedule changes being made?

A) Because of a severe winter storm warning
B) Because of a power outage on campus
C) Because of a scheduled building inspection
D) Because of a national holiday

Q4. What will happen to afternoon classes?

A) They will be canceled entirely
B) They will be moved to the weekend
C) They will be held online as scheduled
D) They will be combined into longer sessions
