💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ💡 Announcement strategy tips

🎯

4つの焦点で聞くListen for the four W's

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さないDon't miss WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE.

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答えPurpose answers come at the start

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！The answer to 'What is the announcement about?' is in the first one or two sentences. Focus on the opening!

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾Recommendations come at the end

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多いThe answer to 'What does the speaker recommend?' is often hidden in the last line.

📢 Announcement 1 — 博物館フィールドトリップ📢 Announcement 1 — Museum field trip

場面：教室 ｜ 話者：教授Setting: Classroom | Speaker: Professor

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a classroom.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon, class. I have an exciting announcement. Our department has arranged a field trip to the local history museum next Thursday, November 7th. We'll meet in front of the science building at 9 AM, and the bus will leave at 9:15 sharp. The museum visit is directly related to our current unit on cultural artifacts, so I expect everyone to attend. Please bring a notebook, as you'll need to complete a short observation worksheet during the visit. If you have any scheduling conflicts, please let me know by this Friday so I can make alternative arrangements.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) A change in the classroom schedule.
(B) A field trip to a local museum.
(C) A new assignment for the class.
(D) A guest lecture on cultural artifacts.
解説：Explanation: 教授は冒頭で「Our department has arranged a field trip to the local history museum next Thursday」と述べており、博物館へのフィールドトリップがアナウンスの主旨。(A)教室のスケジュール変更ではなく、課外活動の告知。(C)新しい課題ではなく、フィールドトリップの案内。(D)ゲスト講義は言及されていない。The professor opens with 'Our department has arranged a field trip to the local history museum next Thursday' — a museum field trip is the main point. (A) is not a class schedule change but an extracurricular announcement. (C) is not a new assignment, but a field trip. (D) a guest lecture isn't mentioned.
🔑 冒頭の「I have an exciting announcement」の直後に主旨が来る。「a field trip to the local history museum」が根拠。Main point comes right after 'I have an exciting announcement.' 'a field trip to the local history museum' is the basis.
2
Why does the professor ask students to bring a notebook?
Purpose
(A) To complete an observation worksheet during the visit.
(B) To take notes on the bus ride schedule.
(C) To write questions for the museum guide.
(D) To record attendance at the museum.
解説：Explanation: 教授は「Please bring a notebook, as you'll need to complete a short observation worksheet during the visit」と述べている。ノート持参の理由は観察ワークシートの記入。(B)バスのスケジュールのメモは言及されていない。(C)博物館ガイドへの質問は話題に出ていない。(D)出席の記録は言及されていない。The professor says, 'Please bring a notebook, as you'll need to complete a short observation worksheet during the visit.' The reason for bringing a notebook is to fill out the observation worksheet. (B) (C) (D) are not mentioned.
🔑 目的問題：「as you'll need to...」が理由を示す。「complete a short observation worksheet」が根拠。Purpose question: 'as you'll need to...' shows the reason. 'complete a short observation worksheet' is the basis.

📢 Announcement 2 — 自転車登録義務化📢 Announcement 2 — Bicycle registration requirement

場面：キャンパスラジオ / セキュリティ通知 ｜ 話者：管理者Setting: Campus radio / security notice | Speaker: Administrator

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to a campus security announcement.
[SPEAKER] Attention all students and staff. The campus security office would like to remind everyone about the new bicycle registration requirement that goes into effect starting December 1st. All bicycles parked on campus must display a valid registration sticker. You can register your bicycle for free at the security office in Building C, Monday through Friday, from 8 AM to 5 PM. Unregistered bicycles found on campus after December 15th may be removed. This policy is being introduced to reduce bicycle theft and help recover stolen bikes more quickly.
3
What is the purpose of the new bicycle registration policy?
Purpose
(A) To limit the number of bicycles on campus.
(B) To generate revenue for campus improvements.
(C) To promote environmental awareness.
(D) To reduce bicycle theft and aid recovery.
解説：Explanation: アナウンスの末尾で「This policy is being introduced to reduce bicycle theft and help recover stolen bikes more quickly」と明確に述べられている。(A)自転車の台数を制限する目的ではない。(B)登録は無料であり、収益目的ではない。(C)環境意識の向上は言及されていない。At the end: 'This policy is being introduced to reduce bicycle theft and help recover stolen bikes more quickly.' (A) the goal isn't to limit bike numbers. (B) registration is free, so it's not for revenue. (C) environmental awareness isn't mentioned.
🔑 目的問題：「This policy is being introduced to...」の直後に理由が明示されている。Purpose question: the reason is stated right after 'This policy is being introduced to...'
4
What can be inferred about unregistered bicycles after December 15th?
Inference
(A) They will receive a warning sticker.
(B) Their owners will be required to pay a fine.
(C) They may be taken away from campus.
(D) They will be locked by campus security.
解説：Explanation: 「Unregistered bicycles found on campus after December 15th may be removed」と述べられている。「may be removed」= 撤去される可能性がある = taken away from campus。(A)警告ステッカーの貼付は言及されていない。(B)罰金は言及されていない。(D)施錠は言及されていない。'Unregistered bicycles found on campus after December 15th may be removed.' 'may be removed' = could be taken away from campus. (A) sticker warnings aren't mentioned. (B) fines aren't mentioned. (D) locking isn't mentioned.
🔑 推論問題：「may be removed」の意味を正確に理解する。removed = taken away from campus。Inference: understand what 'may be removed' means. removed = taken away from campus.
