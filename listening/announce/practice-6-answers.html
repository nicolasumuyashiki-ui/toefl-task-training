💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ

🎯

4つの焦点で聞く

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さない

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答え

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多い

📢 Announcement 1 — 期末試験期間の図書館時間延長

場面：図書館 ｜ 話者：司書

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a library.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon, everyone. As you may know, final exams begin in two weeks, and we want to make sure you have plenty of time to prepare. Starting this Monday, the main library will extend its hours. We will be open from seven AM until midnight, Monday through Friday, and from nine AM to ten PM on weekends. Additionally, we are opening two extra study rooms on the third floor, which can be reserved in one-hour blocks through our online booking system. Please note that food and drinks are only permitted in the designated cafe area on the ground floor. If you have any questions about the extended hours or room reservations, please ask at the information desk.
1
What is the main purpose of the announcement?
Main Idea
(A) To announce new library membership requirements.
(B) To inform students about extended library hours during finals.
(C) To introduce a new library catalog system.
(D) To announce the closure of the library for repairs.
解説： 司書は冒頭で「final exams begin in two weeks」と述べ、「the main library will extend its hours」と延長時間を案内している。(A)会員要件の変更は言及されていない。(C)新しいカタログシステムは話題に出ていない。(D)修理のための閉館ではなく、時間延長のお知らせ。
🔑 冒頭の「final exams begin in two weeks」+ 「extend its hours」が主旨。試験期間に合わせた時間延長。
2
Why does the librarian mention the online booking system?
Purpose
(A) To explain how students can reserve the new study rooms.
(B) To encourage students to visit the library website.
(C) To announce a change in the library's return policy.
(D) To remind students about overdue book fees.
解説： 司書は「two extra study rooms on the third floor, which can be reserved in one-hour blocks through our online booking system」と述べている。オンライン予約システムへの言及は、新しい自習室の予約方法を説明するため。(B)ウェブサイト訪問の促進は主目的ではない。(C)返却ポリシーの変更は言及されていない。(D)延滞料金は話題に出ていない。
🔑 目的問題：「which can be reserved in one-hour blocks through our online booking system」が手がかり。予約システムの言及＝自習室の予約方法を説明するため。

📢 Announcement 2 — 学内スポーツ登録締め切り

場面：アスレチックセンター ｜ 話者：コーチ

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement at the athletic center.
[SPEAKER] Hey everyone, Coach Rivera here with an important reminder. The sign-up deadline for intramural sports this spring is this Friday at five PM. We have six sports available this season: basketball, soccer, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, and ultimate frisbee. Teams must have a minimum of eight members to register, and each student can sign up for a maximum of two sports. Registration forms are available at the athletic center front desk or on the campus recreation website. Also, I want to mention that all team captains are required to attend a mandatory orientation session next Monday at six PM in the gymnasium. If you miss the orientation, your team will not be eligible to compete. So make sure you sign up before Friday and captains, mark your calendars for Monday.
3
When is the deadline to sign up for intramural sports?
Detail
(A) Next Monday at six PM.
(B) This Wednesday at noon.
(C) This Friday at five PM.
(D) Next Friday at five PM.
解説： コーチは「The sign-up deadline for intramural sports this spring is this Friday at five PM」と明確に述べている。(A)月曜日の6PMはキャプテンのオリエンテーションの日時。(B)水曜日の正午は言及されていない。(D)「来週の金曜日」ではなく「今週の金曜日」。
🔑 WHEN問題：日時の聞き分けに注意。「this Friday」と「next Monday」を混同しないように。
4
What can be inferred about the orientation session for team captains?
Inference
(A) It will cover the rules and schedule for the season.
(B) It is optional for experienced captains.
(C) It will be recorded for those who cannot attend.
(D) Missing it has serious consequences for the entire team.
解説： コーチは「all team captains are required to attend a mandatory orientation session」と述べ、「If you miss the orientation, your team will not be eligible to compete」と警告している。「mandatory」+「not be eligible to compete」から、欠席するとチーム全体に深刻な影響があると推論できる。(A)ルールやスケジュールの説明は推測可能だが明言されていない。(B)「mandatory」と言っているので任意ではない。(C)録画の提供は言及されていない。
🔑 推論問題：「mandatory」+「If you miss...your team will not be eligible to compete」の2点から、欠席はチーム全体に重大な結果をもたらすと推論。
