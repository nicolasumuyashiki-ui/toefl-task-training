Announcement 1

Setting: Campus Health Center — Email / Bulletin Board Notice
Attention all students and faculty. As flu season approaches, the campus health center will be offering free flu vaccinations starting Monday, November 4th, through Friday, November 15th. Vaccinations will be available from nine AM to four PM on weekdays, and no appointment is necessary — just walk in with your student or faculty ID. In addition to vaccinations, the health center is distributing free hand sanitizer kits at the front desk. We strongly encourage everyone to wash their hands frequently, avoid touching their face, and stay home if they are feeling unwell. If you experience flu-like symptoms, please call the health center at extension 2200 before coming in, so we can take proper precautions.

C) To introduce a new health insurance policy
D) To recruit volunteers for a medical study

A) Go directly to the emergency room

D) Visit the campus pharmacy for medication

Announcement 2

Setting: Classroom — Professor
Before we start today, I have an important announcement. I will not be here next Wednesday because I'll be presenting a paper at a conference in Chicago. There will be no class that day. However, this does not mean you have a free day. I'm assigning a reading — chapters seven and eight — and I'd like you to write a one-page response summarizing the key arguments. This is due at the beginning of class the following Monday. Also, I've posted a recorded lecture on the course website that covers the same material. I strongly recommend watching it before doing the reading, as it will help you understand the more complex concepts. If you have any questions while I'm away, feel free to email me, though I may be slow to respond.

Q3. Why is the professor canceling class?

A) The classroom is being renovated
B) The professor is sick
C) The professor is attending a conference
D) A university holiday is scheduled

Q4. What does the professor recommend students do before the reading?

A) Visit the professor's office for a pre-reading discussion
B) Form study groups with classmates
C) Complete a practice quiz on the course website
D) Watch a recorded lecture posted on the course website
