Announcement 1

Setting: Campus Facilities Office — Maintenance Notice
Attention all residents of Oakwood Hall and Riverside Dormitory. The campus maintenance department will be conducting a scheduled test of the water system this Saturday, March 15th, from eight AM to two PM. During this time, water service will be temporarily interrupted in both buildings. We recommend that you store enough drinking water the night before and plan any laundry or showers accordingly. If the testing is completed ahead of schedule, service will be restored earlier. We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation.

Q1. What is the purpose of the announcement?

A) To inform residents about a water system test and temporary interruption
B) To announce a new water filtration system
C) To report a broken water pipe in the dormitory
D) To remind students to conserve water on campus

Q2. What are residents advised to do?

A) Stay in their rooms during the testing period
B) Store drinking water the night before
C) Report any water problems to the front desk
D) Move to a different dormitory temporarily

Announcement 2

Setting: Lecture Hall — Professor
Before we begin today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. Next Wednesday, we'll be welcoming Dr. Sarah Chen from the National Institute of Environmental Research as a guest speaker. Dr. Chen is one of the leading experts on ocean plastic pollution, and she will be presenting her latest findings on microplastics in marine ecosystems. The talk will be held in Auditorium B at three PM instead of our usual classroom. Attendance is required for this class, and I want you to prepare at least two questions to ask during the Q-and-A session. This is a wonderful opportunity to hear from someone working at the forefront of environmental science.

Q3. What is the professor announcing?

A) A change in the course syllabus
B) A cancellation of next week's class
C) A guest speaker visiting the class
D) A new research project for students

Q4. What does the professor require students to do?

A) Write a summary of the guest lecture
B) Read Dr. Chen's research paper before the talk
C) Submit a report on ocean pollution
D) Attend the talk and prepare at least two questions
