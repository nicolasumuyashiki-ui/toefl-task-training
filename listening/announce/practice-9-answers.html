💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ💡 Announcement strategy tips

🎯

4つの焦点で聞くListen for the four W's

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さないDon't miss WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE.

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答えPurpose answers come at the start

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！The answer to 'What is the announcement about?' is in the first one or two sentences. Focus on the opening!

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾Recommendations come at the end

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多いThe answer to 'What does the speaker recommend?' is often hidden in the last line.

📢 Announcement 1 — 図書館セルフチェックアウト導入📢 Announcement 1 — Library self-checkout introduction

場面：大学図書館 ｜ 話者：司書Setting: University library | Speaker: Librarian

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a university library.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon, everyone. I'm pleased to announce that our library has just installed a new self-checkout system. Starting next Monday, you'll be able to borrow and return books using the kiosks located near the entrance on the first floor. To use the system, simply scan your student ID, then scan the barcode on each book you wish to borrow. The screen will confirm your transaction and print a receipt with the due date. For returns, just place your books on the designated shelf next to the kiosk, and the system will automatically process them. Please note that the staffed circulation desk will remain open for students who need assistance with reserves, interlibrary loans, or any other special requests. If you have any questions about the new system, feel free to ask any library staff member for a quick demonstration.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) New library hours for the semester.
(B) A new self-checkout system in the library.
(C) Changes to the interlibrary loan policy.
(D) A renovation of the library entrance.
解説：Explanation: 司書は冒頭で「our library has just installed a new self-checkout system」と述べており、セルフチェックアウトシステムの導入がアナウンスの主旨。(A)は図書館の開館時間の変更は言及なし。(C)は図書館間貸出ポリシーの変更ではない。(D)は入口の改装は話題に出ていない。The librarian opens with 'our library has just installed a new self-checkout system' — the introduction of self-checkout is the main point. (A) library hours aren't mentioned. (C) not an interlibrary loan policy change. (D) entrance renovation isn't mentioned.
🔑 冒頭の「I'm pleased to announce that...」の直後に主旨が来る。「installed a new self-checkout system」= A new self-checkout system。Main point right after 'I'm pleased to announce that...' 'installed a new self-checkout system' = a new self-checkout system.
2
Why does the librarian mention the staffed circulation desk?
Purpose
(A) To reassure students that human assistance is still available for special services.
(B) To announce the closure of the circulation desk.
(C) To explain how the self-checkout kiosks work.
(D) To encourage students to volunteer at the desk.
解説：Explanation: 司書はセルフチェックアウトシステムの導入を説明した後、「the staffed circulation desk will remain open for students who need assistance with reserves, interlibrary loans, or any other special requests」と述べている。新システム導入後も有人カウンターが特別なサービスのために利用可能であることを安心させるため。(B)閉鎖ではなく「remain open」と述べている。(C)キオスクの使い方の説明ではない。(D)ボランティアは言及されていない。After explaining the self-checkout system, the librarian says, 'the staffed circulation desk will remain open for students who need assistance with reserves, interlibrary loans, or any other special requests.' This reassures students that the staffed counter remains available for special services. (B) it's 'remain open,' not closing. (C) not an explanation of how to use the kiosk. (D) volunteers aren't mentioned.
🔑 目的問題：「Please note that the staffed circulation desk will remain open」が手がかり。セルフシステム導入でも対面サポートが継続することを伝えるため。Purpose question: 'Please note that the staffed circulation desk will remain open' is the cue. Reassures that in-person support continues even with the self-system.

📢 Announcement 2 — 学内バスケットボールリーグ登録📢 Announcement 2 — Intramural basketball league registration

場面：キャンパス体育局 ｜ 話者：体育局長Setting: Campus athletics office | Speaker: Athletics director

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement from the campus athletics office.
[SPEAKER] Attention all students. The athletics department is excited to announce that registration for the intramural basketball season is now open. This year, we're offering both a competitive league and a recreational league, so there's something for everyone regardless of your skill level. Teams can register online through the campus recreation portal, and the deadline is Friday, April 18th. Each team must have a minimum of seven players and a maximum of twelve. Individual players who don't have a team can sign up as free agents, and we'll place you on a team. Games will be held every Tuesday and Thursday evening in the Fieldhouse gymnasium starting May 1st. There is a twenty-dollar registration fee per player, which covers referee costs and team jerseys. For more information, stop by the athletics office in the recreation center or visit our website.
3
What does the registration fee cover?
Detail
(A) Equipment rental and facility maintenance.
(B) Coach salaries and training materials.
(C) Trophies and end-of-season banquet.
(D) Referee costs and team jerseys.
解説：Explanation: 「There is a twenty-dollar registration fee per player, which covers referee costs and team jerseys」と明確に述べられている。(A)は設備レンタルや施設維持費は言及なし。(B)はコーチの給料やトレーニング資料は言及なし。(C)はトロフィーや打ち上げは言及なし。Clearly stated: 'There is a twenty-dollar registration fee per player, which covers referee costs and team jerseys.' (A) equipment rental and maintenance aren't mentioned. (B) coach salaries and training materials aren't mentioned. (C) trophies and parties aren't mentioned.
🔑 「which covers referee costs and team jerseys」が根拠。「covers」の直後に費用の内訳が続く。'which covers referee costs and team jerseys' is the basis. The cost breakdown follows 'covers.'
4
What can be inferred about the intramural basketball program?
Inference
(A) It is only available to experienced players.
(B) Teams must provide their own uniforms.
(C) The program is designed to be inclusive for players of all abilities.
(D) Games will be held on weekends only.
解説：Explanation: 「we're offering both a competitive league and a recreational league, so there's something for everyone regardless of your skill level」に加え、「Individual players who don't have a team can sign up as free agents」と述べている。競技リーグとレクリエーションリーグの両方を提供し、個人参加も可能であることから、あらゆるレベルの選手を受け入れる包括的なプログラムと推論できる。(A)「regardless of your skill level」と述べているので経験者限定ではない。(B)登録費にジャージが含まれている。(D)「every Tuesday and Thursday evening」と述べており、週末ではない。On top of 'we're offering both a competitive league and a recreational league, so there's something for everyone regardless of your skill level,' it adds 'Individual players who don't have a team can sign up as free agents.' Both leagues are offered and individual sign-up is allowed, so we infer it's an inclusive program for all skill levels. (A) 'regardless of your skill level' rules out experience-only. (B) jerseys are included in the fee. (D) it's 'every Tuesday and Thursday evening,' not weekends.
🔑 推論問題：「something for everyone regardless of your skill level」+「competitive league and recreational league」+「free agents」の3点から、包括的なプログラムと推論。Inference: from 'something for everyone regardless of your skill level' + 'competitive league and recreational league' + 'free agents,' infer it's an inclusive program.
