Announcement 1

Setting: University Library — Librarian
[Narrator] Listen to an announcement in a university library.

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm pleased to announce that our library has just installed a new self-checkout system. Starting next Monday, you'll be able to borrow and return books using the kiosks located near the entrance on the first floor. To use the system, simply scan your student ID, then scan the barcode on each book you wish to borrow. The screen will confirm your transaction and print a receipt with the due date. For returns, just place your books on the designated shelf next to the kiosk, and the system will automatically process them. Please note that the staffed circulation desk will remain open for students who need assistance with reserves, interlibrary loans, or any other special requests. If you have any questions about the new system, feel free to ask any library staff member for a quick demonstration.

Q1. What is the announcement mainly about?

A) New library hours for the semester.
B) A new self-checkout system in the library.
C) Changes to the interlibrary loan policy.
D) A renovation of the library entrance.

Q2. Why does the librarian mention the staffed circulation desk?

A) To reassure students that human assistance is still available for special services.
B) To announce the closure of the circulation desk.
C) To explain how the self-checkout kiosks work.
D) To encourage students to volunteer at the desk.

Announcement 2

Setting: Campus Athletics Office — Athletics Director
[Narrator] Listen to an announcement from the campus athletics office.

Attention all students. The athletics department is excited to announce that registration for the intramural basketball season is now open. This year, we're offering both a competitive league and a recreational league, so there's something for everyone regardless of your skill level. Teams can register online through the campus recreation portal, and the deadline is Friday, April 18th. Each team must have a minimum of seven players and a maximum of twelve. Individual players who don't have a team can sign up as free agents, and we'll place you on a team. Games will be held every Tuesday and Thursday evening in the Fieldhouse gymnasium starting May 1st. There is a twenty-dollar registration fee per player, which covers referee costs and team jerseys. For more information, stop by the athletics office in the recreation center or visit our website.

Q3. What does the registration fee cover?

A) Equipment rental and facility maintenance.
B) Coach salaries and training materials.
C) Trophies and end-of-season banquet.
D) Referee costs and team jerseys.

Q4. What can be inferred about the intramural basketball program?

A) It is only available to experienced players.
B) Teams must provide their own uniforms.
C) The program is designed to be inclusive for players of all abilities.
D) Games will be held on weekends only.
