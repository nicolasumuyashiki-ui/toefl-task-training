💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ

🎯

4つの焦点で聞く

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さない

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答え

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多い

📢 Announcement 1 — キャンパス北側工事と迂回ルート

場面：キャンパス運営局メール ｜ 話者：学部長

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to a campus-wide announcement.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon, everyone. This is Dean Harrison from the Office of Campus Operations. I'm writing to inform you that major construction work will begin on the north side of campus starting Monday, April 7th. The project involves replacing the underground water pipes along University Avenue, so the road between the Science Building and the Student Center will be completely closed to both vehicles and pedestrians for approximately four weeks. During this period, please use the detour route through Maple Lane, which runs behind the library. Shuttle buses will also be rerouted to stop at the temporary bus shelter near the east parking lot. We understand this may cause some inconvenience, and we appreciate your patience. Updated campus maps showing the detour routes are available on the facilities website.
1
What is the main purpose of this announcement?
Main Idea
(A) To announce a new building on campus.
(B) To inform students about construction and alternate routes.
(C) To close the campus library for renovations.
(D) To introduce a new shuttle bus schedule.
解説： 学部長は冒頭で「major construction work will begin on the north side of campus」と述べ、その後迂回ルートを案内している。アナウンス全体が工事と代替ルートの周知。(A)新しい建物の建設ではなく、地下水道管の交換工事。(C)図書館の改修は話題に出ていない。(D)シャトルバスの経路変更は一部に含まれるが、主旨ではない。
🔑 冒頭の「I'm writing to inform you that...」の直後に主旨。工事 + 迂回ルートの2つがアナウンスの柱。
2
Why does the dean mention Maple Lane?
Purpose
(A) To announce a new building on Maple Lane.
(B) To explain where the shuttle bus will stop.
(C) To direct pedestrians to an alternative walking route.
(D) To describe the location of the construction project.
解説： 学部長は「please use the detour route through Maple Lane, which runs behind the library」と述べている。道路が歩行者にも閉鎖されるため、Maple Laneを代替歩行ルートとして案内している。(A)新しい建物の発表ではない。(B)シャトルバスの停留所は東駐車場付近であり、Maple Laneではない。(D)工事の場所はUniversity Avenueであり、Maple Laneではない。
🔑 目的問題：「please use the detour route through Maple Lane」が手がかり。Maple Laneへの言及＝歩行者に代替ルートを案内するため。

📢 Announcement 2 — NASA研究者のゲスト講義

場面：教室 ｜ 話者：教授

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a classroom.
[SPEAKER] Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. Next Wednesday, Dr. Elena Vasquez from NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory will be visiting our department to give a special guest lecture. Dr. Vasquez leads the team working on the Mars sample return mission, and she'll be talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover. The lecture will be held in Auditorium B at three PM and is open to all students, not just those in the astronomy program. I strongly encourage you all to attend, as this is a rare opportunity to hear directly from someone involved in active space exploration. There will also be a Q&A session afterward, so come prepared with questions.
3
What will Dr. Vasquez talk about?
Detail
(A) Recent findings from the Mars Perseverance rover.
(B) The history of NASA's space shuttle program.
(C) Career opportunities at NASA.
(D) How to apply for the astronomy program.
解説： 教授は「she'll be talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover」と明確に述べている。(B)スペースシャトルの歴史は言及されていない。(C)NASAでのキャリアについては話題に出ていない。(D)天文学プログラムへの応募方法は言及されていない。
🔑 WHAT問題：「talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover」= Recent findings from the Mars Perseverance rover。
4
What can be inferred about the guest lecture?
Inference
(A) It is a required part of the astronomy course.
(B) Only a limited number of seats are available.
(C) Dr. Vasquez has visited the department before.
(D) It is an unusual opportunity that the professor values highly.
解説： 教授は「I strongly encourage you all to attend」「this is a rare opportunity to hear directly from someone involved in active space exploration」と述べている。「rare opportunity」「strongly encourage」から、教授がこの講義を非常に貴重な機会と捉えていると推論できる。(A)必修ではなく「encourage」しているだけ。(B)座席制限は言及されていない。(C)過去の訪問は言及されていない。
🔑 推論問題：「strongly encourage」+「rare opportunity」の2点から、教授がこの講義を非常に重要視していると推論。
