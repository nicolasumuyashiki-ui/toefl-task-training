💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ💡 Announcement strategy tips

🎯

4つの焦点で聞くListen for the four W's

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さないDon't miss WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE.

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答えPurpose answers come at the start

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！The answer to 'What is the announcement about?' is in the first one or two sentences. Focus on the opening!

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾Recommendations come at the end

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多いThe answer to 'What does the speaker recommend?' is often hidden in the last line.

📢 Announcement 1 — キャンパス北側工事と迂回ルート📢 Announcement 1 — North-campus construction & detour

場面：キャンパス運営局メール ｜ 話者：学部長Setting: Campus operations email | Speaker: Dean

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to a campus-wide announcement.
[SPEAKER] Good afternoon, everyone. This is Dean Harrison from the Office of Campus Operations. I'm writing to inform you that major construction work will begin on the north side of campus starting Monday, April 7th. The project involves replacing the underground water pipes along University Avenue, so the road between the Science Building and the Student Center will be completely closed to both vehicles and pedestrians for approximately four weeks. During this period, please use the detour route through Maple Lane, which runs behind the library. Shuttle buses will also be rerouted to stop at the temporary bus shelter near the east parking lot. We understand this may cause some inconvenience, and we appreciate your patience. Updated campus maps showing the detour routes are available on the facilities website.
1
What is the main purpose of this announcement?
Main Idea
(A) To announce a new building on campus.
(B) To inform students about construction and alternate routes.
(C) To close the campus library for renovations.
(D) To introduce a new shuttle bus schedule.
解説：Explanation: 学部長は冒頭で「major construction work will begin on the north side of campus」と述べ、その後迂回ルートを案内している。アナウンス全体が工事と代替ルートの周知。(A)新しい建物の建設ではなく、地下水道管の交換工事。(C)図書館の改修は話題に出ていない。(D)シャトルバスの経路変更は一部に含まれるが、主旨ではない。The dean opens with 'major construction work will begin on the north side of campus' and then explains the detour. The whole announcement is about the construction and the alternate route. (A) not a new building — it's underground water-pipe replacement. (C) library renovation isn't mentioned. (D) shuttle bus rerouting is partial, not the main point.
🔑 冒頭の「I'm writing to inform you that...」の直後に主旨。工事 + 迂回ルートの2つがアナウンスの柱。Main point right after 'I'm writing to inform you that...' Construction + detour are the two pillars.
2
Why does the dean mention Maple Lane?
Purpose
(A) To announce a new building on Maple Lane.
(B) To explain where the shuttle bus will stop.
(C) To direct pedestrians to an alternative walking route.
(D) To describe the location of the construction project.
解説：Explanation: 学部長は「please use the detour route through Maple Lane, which runs behind the library」と述べている。道路が歩行者にも閉鎖されるため、Maple Laneを代替歩行ルートとして案内している。(A)新しい建物の発表ではない。(B)シャトルバスの停留所は東駐車場付近であり、Maple Laneではない。(D)工事の場所はUniversity Avenueであり、Maple Laneではない。The dean says, 'please use the detour route through Maple Lane, which runs behind the library.' The road is closed to pedestrians too, so Maple Lane is presented as the pedestrian detour. (A) not a building announcement. (B) the shuttle stop is near the east lot, not Maple Lane. (D) the construction is on University Avenue, not Maple Lane.
🔑 目的問題：「please use the detour route through Maple Lane」が手がかり。Maple Laneへの言及＝歩行者に代替ルートを案内するため。Purpose question: 'please use the detour route through Maple Lane' is the cue. Mention of Maple Lane = guiding pedestrians to the alternate route.

📢 Announcement 2 — NASA研究者のゲスト講義📢 Announcement 2 — NASA researcher guest lecture

場面：教室 ｜ 話者：教授Setting: Classroom | Speaker: Professor

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a classroom.
[SPEAKER] Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. Next Wednesday, Dr. Elena Vasquez from NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory will be visiting our department to give a special guest lecture. Dr. Vasquez leads the team working on the Mars sample return mission, and she'll be talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover. The lecture will be held in Auditorium B at three PM and is open to all students, not just those in the astronomy program. I strongly encourage you all to attend, as this is a rare opportunity to hear directly from someone involved in active space exploration. There will also be a Q&A session afterward, so come prepared with questions.
3
What will Dr. Vasquez talk about?
Detail
(A) Recent findings from the Mars Perseverance rover.
(B) The history of NASA's space shuttle program.
(C) Career opportunities at NASA.
(D) How to apply for the astronomy program.
解説：Explanation: 教授は「she'll be talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover」と明確に述べている。(B)スペースシャトルの歴史は言及されていない。(C)NASAでのキャリアについては話題に出ていない。(D)天文学プログラムへの応募方法は言及されていない。The professor says clearly, 'she'll be talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover.' (B) space shuttle history isn't mentioned. (C) NASA careers aren't mentioned. (D) how to apply to the astronomy program isn't mentioned.
🔑 WHAT問題：「talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover」= Recent findings from the Mars Perseverance rover。WHAT question: 'talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover' = recent findings from the Mars Perseverance rover.
4
What can be inferred about the guest lecture?
Inference
(A) It is a required part of the astronomy course.
(B) Only a limited number of seats are available.
(C) Dr. Vasquez has visited the department before.
(D) It is an unusual opportunity that the professor values highly.
解説：Explanation: 教授は「I strongly encourage you all to attend」「this is a rare opportunity to hear directly from someone involved in active space exploration」と述べている。「rare opportunity」「strongly encourage」から、教授がこの講義を非常に貴重な機会と捉えていると推論できる。(A)必修ではなく「encourage」しているだけ。(B)座席制限は言及されていない。(C)過去の訪問は言及されていない。The professor says, 'I strongly encourage you all to attend,' 'this is a rare opportunity to hear directly from someone involved in active space exploration.' From 'rare opportunity' + 'strongly encourage,' we infer the professor sees this lecture as highly valuable. (A) it's encouragement, not required. (B) seat limits aren't mentioned. (C) past visits aren't mentioned.
🔑 推論問題：「strongly encourage」+「rare opportunity」の2点から、教授がこの講義を非常に重要視していると推論。Inference: from 'strongly encourage' + 'rare opportunity,' infer the professor places very high importance on this lecture.
