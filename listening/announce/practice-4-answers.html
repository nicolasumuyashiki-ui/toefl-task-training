💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ

🎯

4つの焦点で聞く

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さない

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答え

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多い

📢 Announcement 1 — インフルエンザ予防接種と対策

場面：キャンパスヘルスセンター ｜ 話者：管理者

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement from the campus health center.
[SPEAKER] Attention all students and faculty. As flu season approaches, the campus health center will be offering free flu vaccinations starting Monday, November 4th, through Friday, November 15th. Vaccinations will be available from nine AM to four PM on weekdays, and no appointment is necessary — just walk in with your student or faculty ID. In addition to vaccinations, the health center is distributing free hand sanitizer kits at the front desk. We strongly encourage everyone to wash their hands frequently, avoid touching their face, and stay home if they are feeling unwell. If you experience flu-like symptoms, please call the health center at extension 2200 before coming in, so we can take proper precautions.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) Flu season precautions and free vaccinations on campus.
(B) New health center operating hours.
(C) A new health insurance policy for students.
(D) A volunteer recruitment for a medical study.
解説： アナウンスの冒頭で「the campus health center will be offering free flu vaccinations」と述べ、その後に予防策を説明している。インフルエンザ対策と無料ワクチンの案内が主旨。(B)新しい営業時間の案内ではなく、ワクチン接種期間の案内。(C)健康保険ポリシーの変更は言及されていない。(D)医学研究のボランティア募集は話題に出ていない。
🔑 冒頭の「As flu season approaches...offering free flu vaccinations」が主旨を示している。
2
Why does the health center ask students to call before visiting if they have symptoms?
Purpose
(A) To verify the student's insurance coverage.
(B) To allow staff to take proper precautions.
(C) To schedule a mandatory appointment.
(D) To confirm the student's identity.
解説： アナウンスの末尾で「please call the health center...before coming in, so we can take proper precautions」と述べられている。事前電話の目的は適切な予防措置を取るため。(A)保険の確認は言及されていない。(C)予約の義務化ではなく予防措置のため。(D)身元確認は話題に出ていない。
🔑 目的問題：「so we can take proper precautions」が理由を明示。「so」の後に目的が来る。

📢 Announcement 2 — 授業キャンセルと課題

場面：教室 ｜ 話者：教授

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a classroom.
[SPEAKER] Before we start today, I have an important announcement. I will not be here next Wednesday because I'll be presenting a paper at a conference in Chicago. There will be no class that day. However, this does not mean you have a free day. I'm assigning a reading — chapters seven and eight — and I'd like you to write a one-page response summarizing the key arguments. This is due at the beginning of class the following Monday. Also, I've posted a recorded lecture on the course website that covers the same material. I strongly recommend watching it before doing the reading, as it will help you understand the more complex concepts. If you have any questions while I'm away, feel free to email me, though I may be slow to respond.
3
Why is the professor canceling class?
Detail
(A) The classroom is being renovated.
(B) The professor is sick.
(C) The professor is attending a conference.
(D) A university holiday is scheduled.
解説： 教授は「I'll be presenting a paper at a conference in Chicago」と明確に理由を述べている。学会での論文発表のため。(A)教室の改装は言及されていない。(B)病気ではなく学会出席が理由。(D)大学の休日は言及されていない。
🔑 WHY問題：「because I'll be presenting a paper at a conference」が根拠。presenting a paper = attending a conference。
4
What can be inferred about the professor's availability during the trip?
Inference
(A) The professor will be completely unreachable.
(B) The professor will hold virtual office hours.
(C) The professor will check email regularly.
(D) The professor may take longer than usual to reply to emails.
解説： 教授は「feel free to email me, though I may be slow to respond」と述べている。「slow to respond」= 返信に時間がかかる可能性がある = take longer than usual to reply。(A)完全に連絡不可ではなく、メールは受け付ける。(B)バーチャルオフィスアワーは言及されていない。(C)定期的にメールを確認するとは言っておらず、返信が遅れる可能性を示唆。
🔑 推論問題：「though I may be slow to respond」の意味を理解する。slow to respond = may take longer than usual to reply。
