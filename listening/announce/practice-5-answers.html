💡 Announcement 攻略のコツ💡 Announcement strategy tips

🎯

4つの焦点で聞くListen for the four W's

WHO（誰が）・WHAT（何を）・WHEN（いつ）・WHERE（どこで）を聞き逃さないDon't miss WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE.

📍

目的問題は冒頭に答えPurpose answers come at the start

「What is the announcement about?」の答えは最初の1〜2文にある。冒頭に全集中！The answer to 'What is the announcement about?' is in the first one or two sentences. Focus on the opening!

🔚

推奨・次のアクションは末尾Recommendations come at the end

「What does the speaker recommend?」の答えは最後の文に隠れていることが多いThe answer to 'What does the speaker recommend?' is often hidden in the last line.

📢 Announcement 1 — 水道システム検査と断水📢 Announcement 1 — Water system test and shutoff

場面：キャンパス施設管理局 ｜ 話者：メンテナンス担当者Setting: Campus Facilities Office | Speaker: Maintenance staff

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement from the campus facilities office.
[SPEAKER] Attention all residents of Oakwood Hall and Riverside Dormitory. The campus maintenance department will be conducting a scheduled test of the water system this Saturday, March 15th, from eight AM to two PM. During this time, water service will be temporarily interrupted in both buildings. We recommend that you store enough drinking water the night before and plan any laundry or showers accordingly. If the testing is completed ahead of schedule, service will be restored earlier. We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation.
1
What is the announcement mainly about?
Main Idea
(A) A water system test and temporary service interruption.
(B) A new water filtration system for dormitories.
(C) A broken water pipe in the dormitory.
(D) A reminder to conserve water on campus.
解説：Explanation: アナウンスは「conducting a scheduled test of the water system」と「water service will be temporarily interrupted」を伝えている。水道システムの検査と一時的な断水の告知。(B)新しい浄水システムの導入ではなく、既存システムの検査。(C)水道管の破損ではなく、予定された検査。(D)節水の呼びかけではない。The announcement covers 'conducting a scheduled test of the water system' and 'water service will be temporarily interrupted.' A water-system test plus a temporary water shutoff. (B) it's a test of the existing system, not a new purification system. (C) not a pipe break — a scheduled test. (D) not a call to conserve water.
🔑 冒頭の「Attention all residents」の直後に主旨。「scheduled test」と「temporarily interrupted」の2つがポイント。Main point right after 'Attention all residents.' 'scheduled test' + 'temporarily interrupted' are the two key items.
2
Why does the maintenance department recommend storing water?
Purpose
(A) Because the water quality will change after the test.
(B) Because water service will be interrupted during testing.
(C) Because the dormitory water supply is being permanently shut off.
(D) Because new plumbing will be installed in the buildings.
解説：Explanation: 「water service will be temporarily interrupted in both buildings」と述べた後に「We recommend that you store enough drinking water the night before」と述べている。断水するため水を備蓄するよう推奨。(A)水質の変化は言及されていない。(C)永久に停止するのではなく一時的な中断。(D)新しい配管工事は言及されていない。After saying 'water service will be temporarily interrupted in both buildings,' it adds, 'We recommend that you store enough drinking water the night before.' Storage is recommended because of the shutoff. (A) water quality changes aren't mentioned. (C) it's a temporary interruption, not permanent. (D) new pipe construction isn't mentioned.
🔑 目的問題：「water service will be temporarily interrupted」が水の備蓄を推奨する理由。前後の因果関係を聞き取る。Purpose question: the reason for storing water is 'water service will be temporarily interrupted.' Catch the cause-effect.

📢 Announcement 2 — ゲストスピーカーの来訪📢 Announcement 2 — Guest speaker visit

場面：講義室 ｜ 話者：教授Setting: Lecture hall | Speaker: Professor

📜 スクリプトを見る（クリックで展開）📜 View transcript (click to expand)
[NARRATOR] Listen to an announcement in a lecture hall.
[SPEAKER] Before we begin today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. Next Wednesday, we'll be welcoming Dr. Sarah Chen from the National Institute of Environmental Research as a guest speaker. Dr. Chen is one of the leading experts on ocean plastic pollution, and she will be presenting her latest findings on microplastics in marine ecosystems. The talk will be held in Auditorium B at three PM instead of our usual classroom. Attendance is required for this class, and I want you to prepare at least two questions to ask during the Q-and-A session. This is a wonderful opportunity to hear from someone working at the forefront of environmental science.
3
What is the professor announcing?
Detail
(A) A change in the course syllabus.
(B) A cancellation of next week's class.
(C) A guest speaker visiting the class.
(D) A new research project for students.
解説：Explanation: 教授は「we'll be welcoming Dr. Sarah Chen...as a guest speaker」と述べている。ゲストスピーカーの来訪がアナウンスの主旨。(A)シラバスの変更ではない。(B)授業のキャンセルではなく、場所が変わるだけ。(D)学生の新しい研究プロジェクトは提案されていない。The professor says, 'we'll be welcoming Dr. Sarah Chen...as a guest speaker.' A guest speaker is the main point. (A) not a syllabus change. (B) not a class cancellation — only the location changes. (D) no new student research project is proposed.
🔑 「I have an exciting announcement」の直後に「welcoming...as a guest speaker」が来る。'welcoming...as a guest speaker' comes right after 'I have an exciting announcement.'
4
What can be inferred about Dr. Chen's presentation?
Inference
(A) It is optional for students in the course.
(B) It will be recorded for students who cannot attend.
(C) It will focus on freshwater pollution.
(D) It will replace the regular class session for that day.
解説：Explanation: 教授は通常の教室ではなくAuditorium Bで開催すると述べ、「Attendance is required for this class」と言っている。通常の授業時間に、通常の教室の代わりにゲスト講演が行われることから、通常授業の代替であると推論できる。(A)「Attendance is required」と述べているため任意ではない。(B)録画については言及されていない。(C)海洋プラスチック汚染（ocean plastic pollution）であり、淡水汚染ではない。The professor says it'll be in Auditorium B instead of the regular classroom and 'Attendance is required for this class.' During regular class time, in place of the regular room, a guest lecture happens — so we infer it's a substitute for the usual class. (A) not optional ('Attendance is required'). (B) recording isn't mentioned. (C) it's ocean plastic pollution, not freshwater pollution.
🔑 推論問題：「instead of our usual classroom」+「Attendance is required for this class」から、ゲスト講演が通常授業の代わりであると推論。Inference: from 'instead of our usual classroom' + 'Attendance is required for this class,' infer the guest lecture replaces the regular class.
