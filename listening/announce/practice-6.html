Announcement 1

Setting: Library — Librarian
[Narrator] Listen to an announcement in a library.

Good afternoon, everyone. As you may know, final exams begin in two weeks, and we want to make sure you have plenty of time to prepare. Starting this Monday, the main library will extend its hours. We will be open from seven AM until midnight, Monday through Friday, and from nine AM to ten PM on weekends. Additionally, we are opening two extra study rooms on the third floor, which can be reserved in one-hour blocks through our online booking system. Please note that food and drinks are only permitted in the designated cafe area on the ground floor. If you have any questions about the extended hours or room reservations, please ask at the information desk.

Q1. What is the main purpose of the announcement?

A) To announce new library membership requirements.
B) To inform students about extended library hours during finals.
C) To introduce a new library catalog system.
D) To announce the closure of the library for repairs.

Q2. Why does the librarian mention the online booking system?

A) To explain how students can reserve the new study rooms.
B) To encourage students to visit the library website.
C) To announce a change in the library's return policy.
D) To remind students about overdue book fees.

Announcement 2

Setting: Athletic Center — Coach
[Narrator] Listen to an announcement at an athletic center.

Hey everyone, Coach Rivera here with an important reminder. The sign-up deadline for intramural sports this spring is this Friday at five PM. We have six sports available this season: basketball, soccer, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, and ultimate frisbee. Teams must have a minimum of eight members to register, and each student can sign up for a maximum of two sports. Registration forms are available at the athletic center front desk or on the campus recreation website. Also, I want to mention that all team captains are required to attend a mandatory orientation session next Monday at six PM in the gymnasium. If you miss the orientation, your team will not be eligible to compete. So make sure you sign up before Friday and captains, mark your calendars for Monday.

Q3. When is the deadline to sign up for intramural sports?

A) Next Monday at six PM.
B) This Wednesday at noon.
C) This Friday at five PM.
D) Next Friday at five PM.

Q4. What can be inferred about the orientation session for team captains?

A) It will cover the rules and schedule for the season.
B) It is optional for experienced captains.
C) It will be recorded for those who cannot attend.
D) Missing it has serious consequences for the entire team.
