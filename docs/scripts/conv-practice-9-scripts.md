CONVERSATION 1
========================================
Setting: Campus Health Center
Speakers: Student (male) + Receptionist (female)
Questions: Q1-Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:      WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:       MAN   — Voice: Eric (American)
  Receptionist:  WOMAN — Voice: Lily (Australian)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a receptionist at the campus health center.

========================================
CONVERSATION 1 SCRIPT (conv1.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Hi, I'd like to schedule a vaccination appointment. I need to get my flu shot before the deadline.

[WOMAN] Sure. The campus health center is offering flu vaccinations every weekday this month. Do you have a preference for a day?

[MAN] I'm free on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Are there any available slots?

[WOMAN] Let me check. Tuesday at two-thirty is open, and Thursday at three o'clock is available as well.

[MAN] I'll take Tuesday at two-thirty. Do I need to bring anything?

[WOMAN] Just your student ID and your health insurance card. If you don't have insurance, the vaccination is still covered by the student health fee.

[MAN] Great, so there's no extra charge either way?

[WOMAN] That's correct. We'll also update your immunization record in the university system automatically after your visit.

[MAN] Perfect. Thank you for your help!

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 1
========================================
Q1. Why does the student visit the health center? [Detail]
  A) To pick up a prescription from the pharmacy.
  B) To get a physical examination on campus.
  C) To register for the student health insurance plan.
  D) To schedule a flu vaccination appointment.
  Answer: D

Q2. What will happen after the student receives the vaccination? [Detail]
  A) He will need to pay a separate fee.
  B) He will need to submit paperwork to the registrar.
  C) His immunization record will be updated automatically.
  D) He will receive a follow-up phone call.
  Answer: C


========================================
CONVERSATION 2
========================================
Setting: Academic Dean's Office
Speakers: Student (female) + Academic Dean (male)
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)
  Dean:      MAN   — Voice: Daniel (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and an academic dean.

========================================
CONVERSATION 2 SCRIPT (conv2.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Thank you for seeing me, Dean Harris. I'd like to discuss the possibility of appealing my grade in Economics 201.

[MAN] Of course. Can you explain why you believe the grade should be reconsidered?

[WOMAN] I received a C-plus on my final paper, which brought my overall grade down to a B-minus. I think there may have been a calculation error because my midterm and participation grades were both A's.

[MAN] I see. Have you spoken with your professor about this first?

[WOMAN] Yes, I emailed Professor Wallace, but he said the grade stands because the final paper was weighted at forty percent.

[MAN] That's an important detail. For a formal appeal, you'll need to submit a written request explaining your case, along with copies of your graded assignments. The committee reviews appeals within two weeks.

[WOMAN] Is there any chance the grade could be changed?

[MAN] It depends on the evidence. If the committee finds a grading error, they can recommend a change. But if the professor followed the syllabus correctly, the grade will likely remain the same.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 2
========================================
Q3. What can be inferred about why the student visits the dean? [Inference]
  A) She believes there may be an error in her grade.
  B) She wants to transfer to a different economics class.
  C) She needs to drop a course before the deadline.
  D) She wants to request an incomplete for the semester.
  Answer: A

Q4. What can be inferred about the likely outcome of the appeal? [Inference]
  A) The committee will automatically change the grade.
  B) The grade will likely stay if the syllabus was followed.
  C) The student must rewrite the final paper first.
  D) The professor will be required to meet with her.
  Answer: B
