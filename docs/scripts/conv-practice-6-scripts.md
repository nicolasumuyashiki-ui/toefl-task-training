CONVERSATION 1
========================================
Setting: Campus Dining Hall Office
Speakers: Student (female) + Dining Hall Manager (male)
Questions: Q1-Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)
  Manager:   MAN   — Voice: Daniel (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a dining hall manager.

========================================
CONVERSATION 1 SCRIPT (conv1.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Excuse me, I wanted to talk to someone about the meal options in the dining hall. I have a severe peanut allergy, and I've been finding it really difficult to know which dishes are safe for me.

[MAN] I'm sorry to hear that. We do take food allergies very seriously. Have you noticed any specific dishes that were a concern?

[WOMAN] Well, last Tuesday the stir-fry was listed as vegetarian, but there was no mention of peanut oil, which was actually used in the sauce. I only found out when I asked the kitchen staff directly.

[MAN] That shouldn't happen. All allergen information should be displayed on the menu cards. I'll look into why it was missing that day.

[WOMAN] I appreciate that. Is there a way I could get advance notice of the weekly menu so I can plan ahead?

[MAN] Absolutely. We actually post the full weekly menu on our website every Sunday evening. I can also add you to our allergy notification list, which sends an email alert whenever a dish contains one of the major allergens.

[WOMAN] That would be perfect. Could I also suggest putting clearer labels directly on the serving stations?

[MAN] That's a great idea. We've been considering color-coded labels for the top eight allergens. I'll bring your suggestion to our next staff meeting and fast-track the implementation.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 1
========================================
Q1. Why does the student visit the dining hall office?
  A) To complain about the taste of the food
  B) To ask about the dining hall's operating hours
  C) To apply for a job in the kitchen
  D) To discuss food allergy accommodation
  Answer: D

Q2. What does the manager offer to do for the student?
  A) Prepare special meals exclusively for her
  B) Add her to an allergy notification email list
  C) Refund her meal plan fees
  D) Transfer her to a different dining hall
  Answer: B


========================================
CONVERSATION 2
========================================
Setting: Writing Center
Speakers: Student (male) + Writing Tutor (female)
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   MAN   — Voice: Liam (Australian)
  Tutor:     WOMAN — Voice: Lily (Australian)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a writing tutor.

========================================
CONVERSATION 2 SCRIPT (conv2.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Hi, I signed up for a tutoring session because I'm struggling with the structure of my research paper. My professor said my thesis is too broad.

[WOMAN] That's actually one of the most common issues we see here. Can you tell me what your paper is about?

[MAN] It's for my sociology class. I'm writing about how social media affects young people. But I'm not sure how to narrow it down.

[WOMAN] Okay, that's definitely a very broad topic. One approach is to focus on a specific aspect, like the impact of social media on academic performance among college students. That gives you a clear population and a measurable outcome.

[MAN] That makes sense. But how do I restructure my thesis statement once I narrow it down?

[WOMAN] A strong thesis should include your topic, your position, and a preview of your main supporting points. For example, instead of saying "Social media affects young people," you could write something like "Excessive social media use negatively impacts college students' academic performance by reducing study time, increasing distraction, and lowering sleep quality."

[MAN] That's really helpful. Should I rewrite my introduction first, or adjust the body paragraphs?

[WOMAN] I'd recommend starting with the thesis, then revising the body paragraphs to align with your new focus. Once those are solid, rewrite the introduction last so it accurately previews your argument.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 2
========================================
Q3. What is the student's main problem?
  A) He cannot find enough sources for his paper
  B) He missed the deadline for his assignment
  C) His thesis statement is too broad
  D) He does not understand the grading rubric
  Answer: C

Q4. What does the tutor recommend the student do first?
  A) Start with revising the thesis statement
  B) Rewrite the introduction paragraph
  C) Add more references to the bibliography
  D) Change the topic entirely
  Answer: A
