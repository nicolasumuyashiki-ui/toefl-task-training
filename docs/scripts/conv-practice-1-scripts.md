Practice 1 — Campus Life Conversations

========================================
CONVERSATION 1
========================================
Setting: University IT Help Desk
Speakers: Student (female) + IT Staff (male)
Questions: Q1–Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)
  Staff:     MAN   — Voice: George (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and an IT help desk staff member.

========================================
CONVERSATION 1 SCRIPT (conv1.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Hi, I'm having trouble connecting to the campus Wi-Fi on my laptop. It was working fine yesterday.

[MAN] I see. Have you tried restarting your laptop? Sometimes that clears up connection issues.

[WOMAN] Yeah, I did that already, but it still won't connect. It keeps saying "authentication failed."

[MAN] That usually means your network password has expired. We reset all student passwords at the beginning of each month for security purposes.

[WOMAN] Oh, I didn't know that. How do I set a new one?

[MAN] You can reset it through the student portal, or I can do it for you right here. You'll just need your student ID.

[WOMAN] That would be great. Here's my ID. I have a class in twenty minutes, so I really need this fixed quickly.

[MAN] No problem. It'll only take a minute. I'll also send you an email reminder before next month's reset so you won't have this issue again.

[WOMAN] That's really helpful, thank you so much!

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 1
========================================
Q1. Why does the student visit the IT help desk? [Detail]
  A) To report a stolen laptop on campus.
  B) To install new software on her computer.
  C) To request a new student ID card.
  D) To get help connecting to the campus Wi-Fi.
  Answer: D

Q2. What will the staff member do for the student? [Detail]
  A) Reset her network password and send a monthly reminder.
  B) Replace her laptop battery right away.
  C) Give her a temporary Wi-Fi hotspot device.
  D) Schedule a follow-up appointment for tomorrow.
  Answer: A


========================================
CONVERSATION 2
========================================
Setting: Campus Bookstore
Speakers: Student (male) + Bookstore Staff (female)
Questions: Q3–Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   MAN   — Voice: Daniel (American)
  Staff:     WOMAN — Voice: Lily (Australian)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a bookstore staff member.

========================================
CONVERSATION 2 SCRIPT (conv2.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Excuse me, I ordered a textbook online for pickup, but I received the wrong edition.

[WOMAN] Oh, I'm sorry about that. Do you have your order confirmation?

[MAN] Yes, here it is. I ordered the third edition of Introduction to Psychology, but I got the second edition instead.

[WOMAN] Let me check our inventory. Unfortunately, we're currently out of stock on the third edition.

[MAN] That's a problem. My professor specifically said we need the third edition because some chapters were reorganized.

[WOMAN] I understand. We have a new shipment arriving on Wednesday. I can reserve a copy for you and give you a ten percent discount for the inconvenience.

[MAN] Wednesday works. Can I keep the second edition until then, just so I can start reading the parts that didn't change?

[WOMAN] Of course. Just bring it back when you pick up the new one, and we'll process the exchange then.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 2
========================================
Q3. What can be inferred about why the student needs the third edition? [Inference]
  A) The second edition contains too many errors.
  B) His professor requires it because the chapters were reorganized.
  C) The third edition is cheaper than the second.
  D) The bookstore no longer carries the second edition.
  Answer: B

Q4. What will the student most likely do next? [Inference]
  A) Return the book and wait for a full refund.
  B) Buy the second edition for the whole semester.
  C) Read the second edition until Wednesday and then exchange it.
  D) Order the book from a different online bookstore.
  Answer: C

RECORDING NOTES
========================================
- Record narrator lines as separate files
- Record each conversation as one continuous audio file
- Natural conversational pace with brief pauses between turns
- Slight overlap/naturalness OK but keep clarity
- 2-second pause after narrator intro before conversation starts
- Save as:
    conv1-narrator.mp3, conv1.mp3
    conv2-narrator.mp3, conv2.mp3
