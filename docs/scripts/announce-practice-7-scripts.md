NARRATOR (ann1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement in a university classroom.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1 SCRIPT (ann1.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. The biology department is looking for two undergraduate research assistants to join Professor Chen's lab starting next month. The position involves helping with data collection for a study on freshwater ecosystems. You'll work about ten hours per week, and it counts as an independent study credit. To apply, you'll need to submit a short statement of interest and your transcript to the department office by next Friday. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on research experience, especially for those of you considering graduate school.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 1
========================================
Q1. What is the announcement mainly about? [Main Idea]
  A) A change in the lecture schedule.
  B) A research assistant position opening.
  C) A new course being offered next semester.
  D) A scholarship opportunity for biology students.
  Answer: B

Q2. Why does the professor mention graduate school? [Purpose]
  A) To announce a new graduate program in biology.
  B) To require students to apply for graduate school.
  C) To highlight the value of the research experience for future studies.
  D) To explain the prerequisites for the position.
  Answer: C


========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2
========================================
Setting: Campus Safety Bulletin — Campus Police
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  MAN   — Voice: George (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a campus safety announcement.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2 SCRIPT (ann2.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Good afternoon. This is a message from the campus police department regarding bicycle safety. Over the past month, we've received twelve reports of stolen bicycles on campus. Most of these thefts occurred near the east parking lot and the dormitory bike racks. To protect your bicycle, we strongly recommend using a U-lock rather than a cable lock, as cable locks can be easily cut. Always lock your bike to a designated rack, not to trees or handrails. We are also offering free bike registration at the campus police station. Registering your bike makes it much easier for us to recover it if it's stolen. Drop by our office Monday through Friday between nine and five to register yours.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 2
========================================
Q3. What type of lock does the campus police recommend? [Detail]
  A) A cable lock with a combination.
  B) A chain lock with a padlock.
  C) An electronic smart lock.
  D) A U-lock.
  Answer: D

Q4. What can be inferred about the bicycle theft problem on campus? [Inference]
  A) It is a growing concern that requires preventive action.
  B) The thefts only occur during nighttime hours.
  C) The campus police have identified the suspects.
  D) Students are not interested in registering their bicycles.
  Answer: A
