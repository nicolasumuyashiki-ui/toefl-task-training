NARRATOR (ann1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement in a university library.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1 SCRIPT (ann1.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Good afternoon, everyone. As you may know, final exams begin in two weeks, and we want to make sure you have plenty of time to prepare. Starting this Monday, the main library will extend its hours. We will be open from seven AM until midnight, Monday through Friday, and from nine AM to ten PM on weekends. Additionally, we are opening two extra study rooms on the third floor, which can be reserved in one-hour blocks through our online booking system. Please note that food and drinks are only permitted in the designated cafe area on the ground floor. If you have any questions about the extended hours or room reservations, please ask at the information desk.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 1
========================================
Q1. What is the main purpose of the announcement?
  A) To announce new library membership requirements
  B) To inform students about extended library hours during finals
  C) To introduce a new library catalog system
  D) To announce the closure of the library for repairs
  Answer: B

Q2. What additional resource is being made available?
  A) Two extra study rooms on the third floor
  B) Free tutoring sessions in the library
  C) A new computer lab on the second floor
  D) Extended printing services after midnight
  Answer: A

ANNOUNCEMENT 2

Setting: Athletic Center — Coach

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  MAN   — Voice: Daniel (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement at a university athletic center.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2 SCRIPT (ann2.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Hey everyone, Coach Rivera here with an important reminder. The sign-up deadline for intramural sports this spring is this Friday at five PM. We have six sports available this season: basketball, soccer, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, and ultimate frisbee. Teams must have a minimum of eight members to register, and each student can sign up for a maximum of two sports. Registration forms are available at the athletic center front desk or on the campus recreation website. Also, I want to mention that all team captains are required to attend a mandatory orientation session next Monday at six PM in the gymnasium. If you miss the orientation, your team will not be eligible to compete. So make sure you sign up before Friday and captains, mark your calendars for Monday.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 2
========================================
Q3. When is the deadline to sign up for intramural sports?
  A) Next Monday at six PM
  B) This Wednesday at noon
  C) This Friday at five PM
  D) Next Friday at five PM
  Answer: C

Q4. What happens if a team captain misses the orientation session?
  A) The captain will be replaced by another team member
  B) The team must pay an additional registration fee
  C) The captain can attend a make-up session the following week
  D) The team will not be eligible to compete
  Answer: D
