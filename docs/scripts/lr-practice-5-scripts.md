========================================

========================================
You are a new front desk receptionist at a luxury hotel. Today is your first day of guest service training. Listen to the trainer and repeat each sentence.

========================================

Time Limit: 8s
Difficulty: 1/5

========================================
RECORDING NOTES
========================================
- Each sentence is read once by the trainer voice
- Clear, slightly slower-than-natural pace for learner repetition
- Brief pause (0.5s) between chunks to aid comprehension
- 1-second silence before and after each sentence
- Save as: s1.mp3 through s7.mp3 + narrator.mp3
