ANNOUNCEMENT 1
========================================
Setting: Campus-wide Email / Dean's Office
Questions: Q1-Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  MAN   — Voice: Daniel (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a campus-wide announcement from the dean's office.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1 SCRIPT (ann1.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Good afternoon, everyone. This is Dean Harrison from the Office of Campus Operations. I'm writing to inform you that major construction work will begin on the north side of campus starting Monday, April 7th. The project involves replacing the underground water pipes along University Avenue, so the road between the Science Building and the Student Center will be completely closed to both vehicles and pedestrians for approximately four weeks. During this period, please use the detour route through Maple Lane, which runs behind the library. Shuttle buses will also be rerouted to stop at the temporary bus shelter near the east parking lot. We understand this may cause some inconvenience, and we appreciate your patience. Updated campus maps showing the detour routes are available on the facilities website.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 1
========================================
Q1. What is the main purpose of this announcement? [Main Idea]
  A) To announce a new building on campus.
  B) To inform students about construction and alternate routes.
  C) To close the campus library for renovations.
  D) To introduce a new shuttle bus schedule.
  Answer: B

Q2. Why does the dean mention Maple Lane? [Purpose]
  A) To announce a new building on Maple Lane.
  B) To explain where the shuttle bus will stop.
  C) To direct pedestrians to an alternative walking route.
  D) To describe the location of the construction project.
  Answer: C

ANNOUNCEMENT 2
========================================
Setting: Classroom — Professor
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  WOMAN — Voice: Lily (Australian)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement in a university classroom.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2 SCRIPT (ann2.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Before we start today's lecture, I have an exciting announcement. Next Wednesday, Dr. Elena Vasquez from NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory will be visiting our department to give a special guest lecture. Dr. Vasquez leads the team working on the Mars sample return mission, and she'll be talking about the latest discoveries from the Perseverance rover. The lecture will be held in Auditorium B at three PM and is open to all students, not just those in the astronomy program. I strongly encourage you all to attend, as this is a rare opportunity to hear directly from someone involved in active space exploration. There will also be a Q&A session afterward, so come prepared with questions.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 2
========================================
Q3. What will Dr. Vasquez talk about? [Detail]
  A) Recent findings from the Mars Perseverance rover.
  B) The history of NASA's space shuttle program.
  C) Career opportunities at NASA.
  D) How to apply for the astronomy program.
  Answer: A

Q4. What can be inferred about the guest lecture? [Inference]
  A) It is a required part of the astronomy course.
  B) Only a limited number of seats are available.
  C) Dr. Vasquez has visited the department before.
  D) It is an unusual opportunity that the professor values highly.
  Answer: D
