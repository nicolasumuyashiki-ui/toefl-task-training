NARRATOR (narrator.mp3)
========================================
You have agreed to take part in a research study about technology and daily life. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.

========================================
Question 1 (q1.mp3)
========================================
Thank you for participating in this study. I'm going to ask you a few questions about technology and daily life.
[2-SECOND PAUSE]
How often do you use technology in your daily life?

========================================
Question 2 (q2.mp3)
========================================
That's interesting. Now, can you describe a time when technology helped you solve a problem?

========================================
Question 3 (q3.mp3)
========================================
I see. Some people believe that smartphones are essential tools that improve productivity. What do you think — do people spend too much time on their smartphones? Why or why not?

========================================
Question 4 (q4.mp3)
========================================
That's a great point. Some people argue that technology actually brings people closer together through social media and video calls. Do you agree or disagree that technology is making us less social?
