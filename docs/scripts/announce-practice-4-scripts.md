Practice 4 — Campus Announcements

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1
========================================
Setting: Campus Health Center — Email / Bulletin Board Notice
Questions: Q1–Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement from the campus health center.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1 SCRIPT (ann1.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Attention all students and faculty. As flu season approaches, the campus health center will be offering free flu vaccinations starting Monday, November 4th, through Friday, November 15th. Vaccinations will be available from nine AM to four PM on weekdays, and no appointment is necessary — just walk in with your student or faculty ID. In addition to vaccinations, the health center is distributing free hand sanitizer kits at the front desk. We strongly encourage everyone to wash their hands frequently, avoid touching their face, and stay home if they are feeling unwell. If you experience flu-like symptoms, please call the health center at extension 2200 before coming in, so we can take proper precautions.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 1
========================================
Q1. What is the announcement mainly about? [Main Idea]
  A) Flu season precautions and free vaccinations on campus.
  B) New health center operating hours.
  C) A new health insurance policy for students.
  D) A volunteer recruitment for a medical study.
  Answer: A

Q2. Why does the health center ask students to call before visiting if they have symptoms? [Purpose]
  A) To verify the student's insurance coverage.
  B) To allow staff to take proper precautions.
  C) To schedule a mandatory appointment.
  D) To confirm the student's identity.
  Answer: B


========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2
========================================
Setting: Classroom — Professor
Questions: Q3–Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  MAN   — Voice: George (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement in a classroom.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2 SCRIPT (ann2.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Before we start today, I have an important announcement. I will not be here next Wednesday because I'll be presenting a paper at a conference in Chicago. There will be no class that day. However, this does not mean you have a free day. I'm assigning a reading — chapters seven and eight — and I'd like you to write a one-page response summarizing the key arguments. This is due at the beginning of class the following Monday. Also, I've posted a recorded lecture on the course website that covers the same material. I strongly recommend watching it before doing the reading, as it will help you understand the more complex concepts. If you have any questions while I'm away, feel free to email me, though I may be slow to respond.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 2
========================================
Q3. Why is the professor canceling class? [Detail]
  A) The classroom is being renovated.
  B) The professor is sick.
  C) The professor is attending a conference.
  D) A university holiday is scheduled.
  Answer: C

Q4. What can be inferred about the professor's availability during the trip? [Inference]
  A) The professor will be completely unreachable.
  B) The professor will hold virtual office hours.
  C) The professor will check email regularly.
  D) The professor may take longer than usual to reply to emails.
  Answer: D
