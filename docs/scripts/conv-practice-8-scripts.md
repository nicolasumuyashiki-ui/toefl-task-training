CONVERSATION 1
========================================
Setting: Financial Aid Office
Speakers: Student (male) + Financial Aid Advisor (female)
Questions: Q1-Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   MAN   — Voice: Daniel (American)
  Advisor:   WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a financial aid advisor.

========================================
CONVERSATION 1 SCRIPT (conv1.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Hi, I was wondering if I'm eligible for the work-study program this semester. I applied last month but haven't heard back.

[WOMAN] Let me pull up your file. Could I have your student ID number?

[MAN] Sure, it's 4-7-2-9-0-1.

[WOMAN] Okay, I see your application here. It looks like you haven't submitted your most recent tax documents. We need those to verify your financial need.

[MAN] Oh, I thought the documents I submitted with my FAFSA would be enough.

[WOMAN] Unfortunately, the work-study program requires additional verification. You'll need to upload your most recent tax return through the student portal by the end of this week.

[MAN] Got it. And if everything checks out, when could I start working?

[WOMAN] Once your documents are approved, usually within three business days, you can browse available positions on the employment page. Most on-campus jobs offer between ten and fifteen hours per week.

[MAN] That sounds perfect. I'll upload those documents today. Thanks for your help.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 1
========================================
Q1. Why hasn't the student's work-study application been processed?
  A) He missed the application deadline
  B) He is missing required tax documents
  C) His GPA does not meet the minimum requirement
  D) The program is already full for this semester
  Answer: B

Q2. What will the student do next?
  A) Schedule another appointment with the advisor
  B) Submit a new FAFSA application
  C) Upload tax documents through the student portal
  D) Apply directly to on-campus employers
  Answer: C


========================================
CONVERSATION 2
========================================
Setting: Chemistry Lab
Speakers: Student A (female) + Student B / Lab Partner (male)
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student A: WOMAN — Voice: Lily (Australian)
  Student B: MAN   — Voice: Liam (Australian)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between two students in a chemistry lab.

========================================
CONVERSATION 2 SCRIPT (conv2.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Hey, did you look at our experiment results from last Thursday? My numbers are totally different from yours.

[MAN] Really? I got a concentration of 0.45 moles per liter. What did you get?

[WOMAN] I got 0.32. That's a pretty big difference. Do you think one of us made a measurement error?

[MAN] It's possible. I remember the solution looked a bit cloudy when I was measuring it. Maybe some of the solid hadn't fully dissolved yet.

[WOMAN] That could explain why your reading was higher. The undissolved particles might have affected the spectrophotometer reading.

[MAN] Good point. Should we ask Professor Tanaka if we can redo the experiment before the lab report is due?

[WOMAN] I think that's a good idea. The report is due on Monday, so we'd need to do it by Friday at the latest.

[MAN] I'll email the professor right now and ask if the lab is available this Friday afternoon.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 2
========================================
Q3. What is the likely cause of the discrepancy in results?
  A) They used different chemicals for the experiment
  B) The solution may not have been fully dissolved during measurement
  C) The spectrophotometer was broken
  D) They conducted the experiment on different days
  Answer: B

Q4. What do the students plan to do?
  A) Submit the report with both sets of data
  B) Ask the professor to extend the deadline
  C) Use the average of their two results
  D) Request permission to redo the experiment before Friday
  Answer: D
