Response Type: Request / Direction

Script:
Excuse me, is there a campus mailroom where I can pick up packages?

Choices:
A) Yes, it's in the basement of the student center. You'll need your ID to collect them.
B) I usually order things online because it's more convenient.
C) The post office downtown is open until five o'clock.
D) I think packages are delivered on weekdays only.

Answer: A
