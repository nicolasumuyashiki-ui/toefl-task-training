Audio Scripts for Recording
Practice 9 — Theme: Bookstore Customer Service Training

========================================
VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
========================================
Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
Trainer:   MAN   — Voice: George (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (narrator.mp3)
========================================
You are a new employee at the campus bookstore. Today is your first day of customer service training. Listen to the trainer and repeat each sentence.

========================================
Sentence 1 (s1.mp3)
========================================
Words: 5
Time Limit: 8s
Difficulty: 1/5

Script:
Welcome to the campus bookstore.

Chunks:
  [Welcome] [to the campus bookstore.]

========================================
Sentence 2 (s2.mp3)
========================================
Words: 9
Time Limit: 8s
Difficulty: 2/5

Script:
How can I help you find what you need today?

Chunks:
  [How can I help you] [find what you need today?]

========================================
Sentence 3 (s3.mp3)
========================================
Words: 10
Time Limit: 9s
Difficulty: 2/5

Script:
The textbook section is organized by department and course number.

Chunks:
  [The textbook section] [is organized] [by department and course number.]

========================================
Sentence 4 (s4.mp3)
========================================
Words: 12
Time Limit: 10s
Difficulty: 3/5

Script:
We offer a ten percent discount on all used textbooks this week.

Chunks:
  [We offer] [a ten percent discount] [on all used textbooks] [this week.]

========================================
Sentence 5 (s5.mp3)
========================================
Words: 16
Time Limit: 10s
Difficulty: 3/5

Script:
If the book you're looking for is out of stock, I can place a special order for you.

Chunks:
  [If the book you're looking for] [is out of stock,] [I can place a special order] [for you.]

========================================
Sentence 6 (s6.mp3)
========================================
Words: 16
Time Limit: 11s
Difficulty: 4/5

Script:
Please keep your receipt in case you need to return or exchange any items within thirty days.

Chunks:
  [Please keep your receipt] [in case you need] [to return or exchange any items] [within thirty days.]

========================================
Sentence 7 (s7.mp3)
========================================
Words: 18
Time Limit: 12s
Difficulty: 5/5

Script:

Chunks:
  [We also carry] [a wide selection of] [university merchandise, school supplies,] [and graduation gifts] [on the second floor.]

========================================
RECORDING NOTES
========================================
- Each sentence is read once by the trainer voice
- Clear, slightly slower-than-natural pace for learner repetition
- Brief pause (0.5s) between chunks to aid comprehension
- 1-second silence before and after each sentence
- Save as: s1.mp3 through s7.mp3 + narrator.mp3
- Directory: speaking/lr/audio/practice-9/
