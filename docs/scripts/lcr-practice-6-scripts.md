Question 1 (q1.mp3) — WOMAN
========================================
CEFR: A2
Speaker: WOMAN
Voice: Charlotte (American)
Response Type: Request / Problem-solving

Script:
I bought this notebook at the campus bookstore, but it has some pages missing. Can I get a replacement?

Choices:
A) Sure, bring your receipt and we'll swap it.
B) The bookstore sells lots of school supplies.
C) I buy my school supplies every semester.
D) Notebooks are on sale this week here.

Answer: A
