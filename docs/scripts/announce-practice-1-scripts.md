========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1
========================================
Setting: Classroom — Professor
Questions: Q1–Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  MAN   — Voice: George (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement in a classroom.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 1 SCRIPT (ann1.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Good morning, everyone. I wanted to let you know that our midterm exam has been rescheduled. Instead of next Thursday, it will now take place on the following Monday, October 14th, in this same room. The exam will cover chapters one through six, so please make sure to review all the material. Also, I will be holding extra office hours this Friday from two to four PM if anyone needs help.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 1
========================================
Q1. What is the announcement mainly about?
  A) A new textbook requirement
  B) A change in the midterm exam date
  C) A guest speaker visiting the class
  D) A field trip scheduled for next week
  Answer: B

Q2. What does the professor offer to students?
  A) An extension on the homework
  B) A practice exam
  C) A study guide for the exam
  D) Extra office hours on Friday
  Answer: D


========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2
========================================
Setting: Campus Radio / Library Notice
Questions: Q3–Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:   WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Announcer:  WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (ann2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to an announcement on the campus radio.

========================================
ANNOUNCEMENT 2 SCRIPT (ann2.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Attention all students. The main campus library will be undergoing renovations starting November 1st. During the renovation period, which is expected to last approximately three weeks, the second and third floors will be closed. However, the ground floor reading room and the computer lab will remain open as usual. Temporary study spaces have been set up in the student union building for those who need quiet areas. Please visit the library website for updated hours and available services.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Announcement 2
========================================
Q3. What is happening to the library?
  A) It is undergoing renovations
  B) It is being permanently closed
  C) It is moving to a new building
  D) It is extending its hours
  Answer: A

Q4. Where can students find temporary study spaces?
  A) In the campus gymnasium
  B) At a nearby coffee shop
  C) In the student union building
  D) In the science building
  Answer: C
