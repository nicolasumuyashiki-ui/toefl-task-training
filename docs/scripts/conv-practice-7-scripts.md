CONVERSATION 1
========================================
Setting: Housing Office
Speakers: Student (male) + Housing Coordinator (female)
Questions: Q1-Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   MAN   — Voice: Eric (American)
  Coordinator: WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a housing coordinator.

========================================
CONVERSATION 1 SCRIPT (conv1.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Hi, I'd like to request a room change. I'm currently in Baker Hall, but I was wondering if there are any openings in Greenfield Residence.

[WOMAN] I see. May I ask why you'd like to switch?

[MAN] Well, my roommate has a completely different schedule from mine. He stays up until three in the morning, and I have an eight AM class every day. It's really affecting my sleep.

[WOMAN] I understand. That's a common issue. Have you tried discussing quiet hours with your roommate first?

[MAN] I have, but it hasn't worked out. He agreed to use headphones, but the light from his desk lamp still keeps me awake.

[WOMAN] Okay. Greenfield does have a few single rooms available, but they cost about two hundred dollars more per semester. Would that be all right?

[MAN] That's fine. At this point, getting enough sleep is worth the extra cost.

[WOMAN] Great. I'll need you to fill out a transfer request form. If everything is approved, you could move in as early as next Monday.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 1
========================================
Q1. Why does the student want to change rooms? [Detail]
  A) His current room is too small for his belongings.
  B) His roommate's schedule is disrupting his sleep.
  C) He wants to live closer to the science building.
  D) His room has a maintenance problem.
  Answer: B

Q2. What does the coordinator tell the student about Greenfield Residence? [Detail]
  A) It is fully occupied this semester.
  B) It requires a minimum GPA to apply.
  C) Single rooms are available but cost more.
  D) It is being renovated until next month.
  Answer: C


========================================
CONVERSATION 2
========================================
Setting: Professor's Office
Speakers: Student (female) + Professor (male)
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   WOMAN — Voice: Alice (British)
  Professor: MAN   — Voice: George (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a professor.

========================================
CONVERSATION 2 SCRIPT (conv2.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Professor Davis, do you have a moment? I wanted to ask about extra credit opportunities for your sociology class.

[MAN] Sure, come in. What's on your mind?

[WOMAN] Well, I didn't do as well as I expected on the midterm, and I'm worried about my final grade. I was hoping there might be some way to earn additional points.

[MAN] I see. I don't typically offer extra credit assignments, but there is something you could do. The department is hosting a guest lecture next Thursday by Dr. Yamamoto on urban sociology. If you attend and write a one-page summary connecting her talk to our course material, I can add five points to your midterm score.

[WOMAN] That sounds great. What time is the lecture?

[MAN] It starts at four PM in the auditorium. I'd also suggest visiting the writing center before submitting your summary. They can help you polish your work.

[WOMAN] I'll definitely do that. Thank you so much, Professor Davis.

[MAN] You're welcome. And remember, the final exam is worth forty percent, so strong performance there can really turn things around.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 2
========================================
Q3. What can be inferred about why the student is worried? [Inference]
  A) She wants to change her major to sociology.
  B) She needs a deadline extension for an assignment.
  C) She is struggling to understand the course material.
  D) Her midterm score was lower than she expected.
  Answer: D

Q4. What will the student most likely do next? [Inference]
  A) Attend a guest lecture and write a summary.
  B) Retake the midterm exam the following week.
  C) Drop the course and register again next semester.
  D) Form a study group with her classmates.
  Answer: A
