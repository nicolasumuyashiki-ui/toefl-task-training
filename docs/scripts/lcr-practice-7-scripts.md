Question 1 (q1.mp3) — WOMAN
========================================
CEFR: A2
Speaker: WOMAN
Voice: Alice (British)
Response Type: Request / Information

Script:
Excuse me, do you know what time the campus shuttle stops running on weekdays?

Choices:
A) It goes to the train station.
B) The last one leaves at eleven.
C) I usually take the bus instead.
D) The service started three years ago.

Answer: B
