CONVERSATION 1
========================================
Setting: Registrar's Office
Speakers: Student (male) + Registrar Staff (female)
Questions: Q1-Q2

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   MAN   — Voice: Eric (American)
  Staff:     WOMAN — Voice: Alice (British)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv1-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a registrar's office staff member.

========================================
CONVERSATION 1 SCRIPT (conv1.mp3)
========================================
[MAN] Hi, I transferred here from a community college, and I'm trying to figure out which of my credits will count toward my degree here.

[WOMAN] Welcome! We get a lot of transfer students. Do you have your unofficial transcript from your previous school?

[MAN] Yes, I brought a printed copy. I completed sixty credits there, mostly general education courses.

[WOMAN] Great. Generally, most general education credits transfer without any issues. The tricky part is usually with major-specific courses. Which major are you pursuing here?

[MAN] Business administration. I took an introductory accounting course and a statistics course at the community college.

[WOMAN] Let me pull up the equivalency chart. The statistics course should transfer directly, but for the accounting course, we'll need to verify the syllabus matches our curriculum. Can you provide the course syllabus?

[MAN] I think I have it saved in my email. How long does the evaluation process usually take?

[WOMAN] Once we have all the documents, it typically takes about two weeks. I'll also set you up with an academic advisor who specializes in transfer students to make sure you're on the right track for graduation.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 1
========================================
Q1. Why does the student visit the registrar's office? [Detail]
  A) To find out which community college credits will transfer.
  B) To register for classes for the upcoming semester.
  C) To request a copy of his official transcript.
  D) To change his major to business administration.
  Answer: A

Q2. What does the staff member ask the student to provide? [Detail]
  A) A letter of recommendation from his previous professor.
  B) The course syllabus for his accounting class.
  C) His official high school diploma for verification.
  D) A completed application form for credit transfer.
  Answer: B


========================================
CONVERSATION 2
========================================
Setting: Professor's Office
Speakers: Student (female) + Professor (male)
Questions: Q3-Q4

VOICE ASSIGNMENTS
  Narrator:  WOMAN — Voice: Rachel (American)
  Student:   WOMAN — Voice: Charlotte (American)
  Professor: MAN   — Voice: Liam (Australian)

========================================
NARRATOR (conv2-narrator.mp3)
========================================
Listen to a conversation between a student and a professor.

========================================
CONVERSATION 2 SCRIPT (conv2.mp3)
========================================
[WOMAN] Professor Nakamura, thank you for meeting with me. I wanted to discuss my research paper topic for the final project.

[MAN] Of course. What topic are you considering?

[WOMAN] I was thinking about writing on the impact of social media on political campaigns. I find it really interesting how candidates use platforms like Instagram and TikTok now.

[MAN] That's a relevant topic, but it's also quite broad. For a fifteen-page paper, you'd need to narrow it down significantly. Have you thought about focusing on a specific aspect?

[WOMAN] Hmm, maybe I could focus on how social media affected voter turnout among young adults in the last election?

[MAN] Now that's much more manageable. There's also a good amount of recent research on that specific topic, so you'll have solid sources to work with. I'd suggest starting with the Journal of Political Communication for peer-reviewed articles.

[WOMAN] That's really helpful. When is the topic proposal due again?

[MAN] The written proposal is due next Friday. It should be about one page, including your thesis statement and a preliminary list of at least five sources.

========================================
QUESTIONS — Conversation 2
========================================
Q3. What does the professor imply about the student's original topic? [Inference]
  A) It has already been covered by too many students.
  B) It is not related to the course material at all.
  C) It would be too broad to cover in a fifteen-page paper.
  D) It lacks sufficient academic sources for research.
  Answer: C

Q4. What can be inferred about what the student will do next? [Inference]
  A) She will submit the complete research paper immediately.
  B) She will write a one-page summary of the project.
  C) She will compile a list of ten peer-reviewed sources.
  D) She will write a proposal with a thesis and five sources.
  Answer: D
