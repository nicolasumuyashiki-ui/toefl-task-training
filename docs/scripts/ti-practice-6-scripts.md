You have agreed to take part in a research study about the environment and nature. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.

========================================
Question 1 (q1.mp3)
========================================
Thank you for joining this study. I'd like to ask you about the environment and the natural world.
[2-SECOND PAUSE]
What is the biggest environmental problem in your country?

========================================
Question 2 (q2.mp3)
========================================
That's interesting. Thank you for sharing that. Can you describe a time when you did something to help the environment?

========================================
Question 3 (q3.mp3)
========================================
Thank you for that example. Some people believe that environmental protection is mainly the responsibility of governments and large corporations, not individuals. Do you think individuals can make a real difference in protecting the environment, or is it mainly the responsibility of governments and companies?

========================================
Question 4 (q4.mp3)
========================================
I see. That's a thoughtful perspective. Some people argue that economic growth and environmental protection cannot go hand in hand — that one must be sacrificed for the other. What is your view on this? Can a country pursue economic development while also protecting the environment?
