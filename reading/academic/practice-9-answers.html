Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "fast tempos and complex harmonies that challenged both performers and listeners" → Answer: "Rapid tempos and intricate harmonic structures that demanded skill from musicians and listeners"
Passage: "gaps created by dying host trees allow sunlight to reach the forest floor, promoting new growth" → Answer: "benefit the ecosystem by creating opportunities for new plant growth"
2 Understand passage structure
First sentence → Topic / Main claim
Middle → Details, examples, data
Last sentence → Conclusion / Summary

Main Idea questions can often be solved by reading just the first and last sentences of each paragraph.
Q1: Paragraph 1 introduces jazz's origins in New Orleans. Paragraph 2 describes its spread in the 1920s. Paragraph 3 covers later subgenres. The thread: how jazz originated and developed = A.
3 Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check
Replace the target word with each option and see which one reads most naturally in the sentence.
Q5: "the fig's roots [squeeze] the host tree's trunk" reads naturally and matches the context of roots wrapping around and killing the tree. "Support" is the opposite; "decorate" and "touch" do not convey the destructive nature described.
4 Rhetorical Purpose: ask "what point is the author making?"
When asked why the author mentions something, look at the sentence before the example. That sentence usually states the point the example is meant to illustrate.
罠のパターン:Trap pattern: An option that accurately describes the example itself but misidentifies the author's purpose for including it.

設問 5 — 推論Question 5 — Inference
第3段落から、1920年代以降に登場したジャズの様々なサブジャンルについて何が推論できるか。What can be inferred from paragraph 3 about the various subgenres of jazz that emerged after the 1920s?
正解: DCorrect: D あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 大衆の好みにおいて古いジャズスタイルを意図的に置き換えるために設計された。They were intentionally designed to replace older jazz styles in the public's taste.
B 米国のミュージシャンに影響を与える前に、主に米国外で出現した。They emerged primarily outside the United States before influencing American musicians.
C 初期のニューオーリンズジャズを定義した個人的表現を放棄した。They abandoned the personal expression that defined early New Orleans jazz.
D 高度に実験的なサブジャンルでさえ、ジャンルのニューオーリンズ起源との結びつきを少なくとも一つ保っていた。 ✓Even highly experimental subgenres maintained at least one link to the genre's New Orleans origins. ✓
本文に基づく推論:Text-based inference: The inference combines two facts from the passage: (1) Bebop is described as pioneering "fast tempos and complex harmonies that challenged both performers and listeners" — i.e., highly experimental. (2) "Although each new style departed in some ways from traditional jazz, they all preserved the spirit of improvisation that had defined the genre from its earliest days" — with paragraph 1 placing the genre's roots in New Orleans. Combining these, even an experimental subgenre such as bebop kept improvisation, which is itself the link to the genre's New Orleans origins = D.

A is unsupported — no intention to "replace" is mentioned. B contradicts the passage, which places jazz development in the U.S. C is the opposite of what the passage states ("preserved the spirit of improvisation").
推論ステップ:Inference step: "bebop... fast tempos and complex harmonies that challenged listeners" + "all preserved the spirit of improvisation that had defined the genre from its earliest days" + jazz roots in New Orleans → D: even experimental subgenres kept a link to New Orleans origins (via improvisation).

罠のパターン:Trap pattern: 本文の事実に近いが、著者が言及する「目的」を取り違える選択肢 (A) や、本文を超えた主張をする選択肢 (D)。Options that stay close to passage facts but misidentify the author's purpose (A), or that go beyond what the passage claims (D).
重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 推論問題では、根拠はパッセージ内のみ。 自分の知識や意見を持ち込まないこと。
3. 主旨 = 最初の文 + 最後の文。 段落中央の細部に引き込まれないように。
4. 文脈中の語彙：代入して確認。 各選択肢で語を置き換え、最も自然に読めるものを選ぶ。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For inference questions, only the passage counts as evidence. Do not mix in your own knowledge or opinions.
3. Main Idea = first sentence + last sentence. Do not get pulled into middle-paragraph details.
4. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check. Replace the word with each option and see which one reads most naturally.
