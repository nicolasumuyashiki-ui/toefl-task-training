Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases (パラフレーズを見抜く)
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "seeps into the ground, replenishing underground aquifers" → Answer: "soaks into the ground to refill aquifers"
Passage: "grooming may also serve as a strategic tool for gaining social advantages" → Answer: "serves social and strategic purposes beyond basic hygiene"
2 Vocabulary: identify the part of speech first (品詞特定)
Before looking at options, determine if the target word is a noun, verb, adjective, or adverb. Then substitute each option into the sentence to check which one fits both grammatically and logically.
Q2: "it cools and condenses into tiny droplets" — "condenses" is a verb. Substitute: "changes from gas to liquid into tiny droplets" reads naturally. "Disappears completely" and "expands rapidly" contradict the context of forming droplets.
3 Rhetorical Purpose: read the sentence before and after (前後の文を読む)
When asked "Why does the author mention X?", the answer is usually found in the sentence immediately before the mention. The author states a claim, then provides X as an example or evidence.
Q4: The sentence before says "the range of social structures found in primates is remarkably diverse." The mention of marmosets (small groups) and baboons (large hierarchies) illustrates this diversity = A.
4 Use elimination for difficult questions (消去法)
When unsure, eliminate options that contain extreme words (always, never, only, all, none) or information not found in the passage.
Red-flag words to watch for:
✗ "always," "never," "only," "all," "none," "completely," "the most"
✓ Correct answers tend to use moderate language: "some," "often," "may," "tend to"

重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 語彙問題では、まず品詞を特定してから各選択肢を代入する。 文脈に最も自然に収まるものを確認する。
3. 修辞目的問題では、言及より前の文を読む。 主張が先に来て、その後に例が続く。
4. 極端な語（always, never, only, all）を含む選択肢は除外する。 TOEFL の正解は穏やかな表現を使う。
5. 文挿入問題では、ディスコースマーカーを手がかりに。 「As a result」や「Furthermore」のような語は、文がどう前後と接続するかを示す。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For vocabulary questions, identify the part of speech first, then substitute each option. Check which one reads most naturally in context.
3. For rhetorical purpose, read the sentence before the mention. The claim comes first, then the example.
4. Eliminate options with extreme words (always, never, only, all). TOEFL correct answers use moderate language.
5. For Sentence Insertion, use discourse markers as clues. Words like "As a result" and "Furthermore" signal how the sentence connects to its neighbors.
