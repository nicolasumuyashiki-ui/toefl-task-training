Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "heavily influenced by cognitive biases" → Answer: "How cognitive biases affect the way people make decisions"
Passage: "providing a unified framework that explains" → Answer: "connects many different geological phenomena under one explanation"
2 Rhetorical Purpose: ask "Why did the author include this?"
When you see a specific example in the passage, ask yourself: What general point was the author trying to support? The answer to a purpose question is never about the example itself — it is about the larger claim.
Q4: The Himalayas are mentioned right after the sentence about plate collision causing mountain formation. The example illustrates this process = D.
3 Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check
Replace the highlighted word with each option and read the sentence. The option that fits most naturally is the answer.
Common trap: An option that is a valid dictionary definition of the word but does not fit the specific context of the passage.
4 Sentence Insertion: follow the referents
When there are no discourse markers (like "Furthermore" or "In contrast"), look for pronoun references and vocabulary echoes that connect the new sentence to a specific preceding sentence.
Q8 example: "this ongoing collision" and "the two plates" in the insertion sentence can only refer to the Himalayas and the Indian/Eurasian Plates mentioned in sentence 3. This tells you the sentence must go at position C.
Common mistake: Choosing a position where the sentence "sounds okay" without checking whether the pronoun references and vocabulary actually match. Always ask: What does "the range" / "these findings" / "such behavior" refer to? The referent must appear before the insertion point.

設問 8 — 文挿入Question 8 — Sentence Insertion
Where would the following sentence best fit in the passage?
"Indeed, precise satellite measurements have confirmed that this ongoing collision continues to push the two plates toward each other at a measurable rate."
正解: CCorrect: C あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 文1の後（プレート衝突と山脈形成について）After sentence 1 (about plates colliding and creating mountain ranges)
B 文2の後（造山運動と顕著な山脈について）After sentence 2 (about orogeny and prominent mountain chains)
C 文3の後（ヒマラヤとインド/ユーラシアプレート衝突について） ✓After sentence 3 (about the Himalayas and the Indian/Eurasian Plate collision) ✓
D 文4の後（ヒマラヤが年5mm上昇することについて）After sentence 4 (about the Himalayas rising 5 mm per year)
なぜ C が正解か：Why C is correct: 挿入文には2つの手がかりがある：ディスコースマーカー「Indeed」（直前の主張を補強する）と、指示句「this ongoing collision」（直前に「進行中の衝突」への言及が必要）。両方が一致して指し示すのは位置 C：直前の文が「…the ongoing collision between the Indian Plate and the Eurasian Plate, a process that began approximately 50 million years ago and continues to this day.」で終わっている。挿入文が観測データでこの主張を裏付け、続く「As a result, the Himalayas are still rising...」が結果として続く。

A・B が不正解の理由： まだ「進行中の衝突」が紹介されていないため、「this ongoing collision」の指示対象がない。
D が不正解の理由： 「Indeed」は直前の主張を補強するが、D の直前の文（「ヒマラヤが年5mm上昇している」）はそれ自体が結果であり、「進行中の衝突」という主張ではない。結果を飛び越して衝突の主張に戻って補強するのは不自然で、原因→証拠→結果の流れを崩す。
挿入問題の戦略： ディスコースマーカー（「Indeed」「Furthermore」）と指示参照（「this ongoing collision」）を組み合わせて使う。マーカーは「直前の主張を補強する」と教えてくれ、指示句は「どの主張か」を特定する。両方が同じ位置を指す必要がある。
The insertion sentence has two anchors: the discourse marker "Indeed" (which reinforces a just-stated claim) and the deictic phrase "this ongoing collision" (which requires an immediately preceding mention of an ongoing collision). Both anchors point uniquely to position C: the prior sentence ends with "...the ongoing collision between the Indian Plate and the Eurasian Plate, a process that began approximately 50 million years ago and continues to this day." The insertion confirms that claim with measurement evidence, and "As a result, the Himalayas are still rising..." then follows as the consequence.

Why not A or B? No ongoing collision has been introduced yet, so "this ongoing collision" has no referent.
Why not D? "Indeed" reinforces a preceding claim, but the sentence preceding D ("Himalayas are still rising by about five millimeters per year") is itself a stated consequence, not the claim about ongoing collision. Reaching back over the consequence to reinforce the collision is unnatural and breaks the cause→evidence→consequence flow.
Insertion strategy: Use discourse markers ("Indeed," "Furthermore") and deictic references ("this ongoing collision") together. The marker tells you the sentence reinforces a recent claim; the deictic tells you exactly which claim. Both must point to the same position.

重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 修辞目的 = "what" ではなく "why"。 答えは例そのものではなく、著者がその例を含めた目的を説明する。
3. 文脈中の語彙：代入して確認。 各選択肢で語を置き換え、最も自然に読めるものを選ぶ。
4. シグナル語のない文挿入問題： 代名詞参照と語彙のエコー（言い換え）を追跡し、明確な指示対象が成立する唯一の位置を見つける。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. Rhetorical Purpose = why, not what. The answer explains the author's purpose for including an example, not the example itself.
3. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check. Replace the word with each option and see which one reads most naturally.
4. Sentence Insertion without signal words: Track pronoun references and vocabulary echoes to find the only position where they have clear referents.
