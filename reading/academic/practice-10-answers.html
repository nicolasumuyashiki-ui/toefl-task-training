Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "fundamentally changed how goods were made and how people lived and worked" → Answer: "transformed society through urbanization, new social classes, and eventual improvements in living standards"
Passage: "the enzyme temporarily changes shape to fit around it more tightly" → Answer: "temporarily adjusts its shape to fit more closely around the substrate"
2 Understand passage structure
First sentence → Topic / Main claim
Middle → Details, examples, data
Last sentence → Conclusion / Summary

Main Idea questions can often be solved by reading just the first and last sentences of each paragraph.
Q1: Paragraph 1 introduces the Industrial Revolution. Paragraph 2 describes urbanization and new social classes. Paragraph 3 covers long-term improvements. The thread: how industrialization transformed society = A.
3 Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check
Replace the target word with each option and see which one reads most naturally in the sentence.
Q2: "One of the most [significant] effects of industrialization was the rapid growth of cities" reads naturally and matches the context of massive societal change. "Temporary" contradicts the lasting impact described; "slight" is the opposite.
4 Rhetorical Purpose: ask "what point is the author making?"
When asked why the author mentions something, look at the sentence before the example. That sentence usually states the point the example is meant to illustrate.
罠のパターン:Trap pattern: An option that accurately describes the example itself but misidentifies the author's purpose for including it.

設問 4 — 推論Question 4 — Inference
農村から工業都市に移住した労働者について何が推論できるか。What can be inferred about the workers who migrated from the countryside to industrial cities?
正解: CCorrect: C あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 彼らはすぐに工場主や専門職の新中流階級に加わった。They quickly joined the new middle class of factory owners and professionals.
B 大半は最終的に農村の農業労働に戻った。Most of them eventually returned to agricultural work in the countryside.
C 収入の改善が、必ずしも健康的な生活水準につながったわけではない。 ✓Their improvement in income did not necessarily translate into healthier living standards. ✓
D 他の工場労働より炭鉱の仕事を好んだ。They preferred coal-mining jobs to other forms of factory labor.
本文に基づく推論:Text-based inference: 推論は 2 つの事実を組み合わせる必要がある。① 第2段落「workers migrated... in search of employment（労働者は仕事を求めて移動した）」 → 雇用 = 収入の改善が動機。② 同じ第2段落「This mass movement of people led to overcrowded living conditions, poor sanitation, and the spread of disease（過密な生活環境、不衛生、疾病の蔓延をもたらした）」 → 健康・衛生面では悪化。両者を統合すると、収入は得たが健康な生活には結びついていない、と推論できる = C。

A は誤り。中流階級は「工場主、商人、専門職」であり、移住労働者ではない。B は本文に記述なし。D も本文に根拠なし。炭鉱と他の労働の比較はパッセージでされていない。 The inference combines two facts: (1) workers migrated "in search of employment" → income improved relative to rural unemployment, and (2) the mass migration "led to overcrowded living conditions, poor sanitation, and the spread of disease." Together these imply that getting paid work did not automatically improve their health or living standards = C.

A is wrong because the middle class consisted of "factory owners, merchants, and professionals," not the migrating workers. B is unsupported — nothing in the passage describes a return migration. D is unsupported — the passage never compares coal mining with other factory work.
言い換え:Paraphrase: Passage: "the harsh conditions faced by many early factory workers" → C: "often faced difficult living conditions even after finding factory employment"

なぜ高熱が人間の健康に深刻なリスクをもたらしうるかについて、パッセージから何が推論できるか。What can be inferred from the passage about why a high fever can pose a serious risk to human health?

A 体が一度に新しい酵素を作りすぎる原因となる。It causes the body to produce too many new enzymes at once.

重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 推論問題では、根拠はパッセージ内のみ。 自分の知識や意見を持ち込まないこと。
3. 主旨 = 最初の文 + 最後の文。 段落中央の細部に引き込まれないように。
4. 文脈中の語彙：代入して確認。 各選択肢で語を置き換え、最も自然に読めるものを選ぶ。
5. シグナル語のない文挿入問題： ディスコースマーカー（Furthermore, However など）が無い場合は、代名詞参照と指示詞（"this," "these," "such"）に注目する。直前の文に明確な指示対象が成立する唯一の位置を見つける。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For inference questions, only the passage counts as evidence. Do not mix in your own knowledge or opinions.
3. Main Idea = first sentence + last sentence. Do not get pulled into middle-paragraph details.
4. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check. Replace the word with each option and see which one reads most naturally.
5. Sentence Insertion without signal words: When there is no discourse marker (Furthermore, However, etc.), focus on pronoun references and demonstratives ("this," "these," "such"). Find the only position where they have a clear referent in the preceding sentence.
