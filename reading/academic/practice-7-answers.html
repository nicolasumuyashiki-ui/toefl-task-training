Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "prevent any single individual from gaining too much control" → Answer: "prevent one person from holding too much power"
Passage: "mount a much faster and stronger response" → Answer: "respond more quickly"
2 Understand passage structure
First sentence → Topic / Main claim
Middle → Details, examples, data
Last sentence → Conclusion / Summary

Main Idea questions can often be solved by reading just the first and last sentences.
Q1: Passage 1 opens with the Republic being "established around 509 BCE" and ends with "the end of a political experiment that had lasted nearly five hundred years." This arc — establishment to collapse — gives the main idea = A.
3 Rhetorical purpose: ask "why did the author include this?"
Purpose questions ask about the function of a detail, not the detail itself. Look at the sentence before the mentioned detail — it usually states the point the example supports.
Q4: Before mentioning neutrophils and macrophages, the passage says "general-purpose immune cells that respond quickly to any foreign substance." The examples illustrate cells involved in the body's immediate defense = A.
4 Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check
Replace the highlighted word with each answer choice and see which one reads most naturally in context. The correct answer should preserve the original meaning of the sentence.
Common trap: An answer that matches the word's dictionary definition but does not fit the specific context of the passage.

設問 4 — 文脈語彙Question 4 — Vocabulary in Context
第3段落の「concentrated」に最も近い意味は何か。The word "concentrated" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to
正解: DCorrect: D あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 分けたdivided
B 弱めたweakened
C 手放したsurrendered
D 集めた ✓gathered ✓
文脈確認:Context check: 本文は「Powerful leaders such as Julius Caesar challenged the authority of the Senate and concentrated power in their own hands（カエサルのような強力な指導者が元老院の権威に挑戦し、権力を自らの手に集中させた）」と述べる。「concentrate power in one's hands」とは権力を一箇所に集めて支配することを意味し、＝gathered（集めた）。代入確認：「gathered power in their own hands」は自然に読める = D。

A（divided 分けた）は逆。権力を分散させる意味で、集中の反対。B（weakened 弱めた）も逆。指導者が権力を弱めるのではなく、強化している。C（surrendered 手放した）も逆。挑戦して権力を獲得する文脈と矛盾。 The passage reads "challenged the authority of the Senate and concentrated power in their own hands." To concentrate power in one's hands means to gather it together into a single location of control = gathered. Substitution check: "gathered power in their own hands" reads naturally = D.

A (divided) is the opposite — concentrating means gathering, not splitting. B (weakened) contradicts the context of leaders acquiring power. C (surrendered) is also the opposite — they were seizing, not giving up, power.

A 個人的野心が共和国への忠誠を上回ることが多かった。 ✓Their personal ambitions often outweighed their commitment to the Republic. ✓

B（イタリア国外での作戦を拒否）はパッセージと矛盾。第2段落で地中海全域を征服したと明記。C（元老院に忠実に従った）も逆。パッセージは元老院への挑戦と権力集中を明示する。D（外国政府からの資金）は本文に根拠なし。 Paragraph 2 says "Wealthy generals began to build personal armies loyal to them rather than to the Republic." Paragraph 3 continues that "Powerful leaders such as Julius Caesar challenged the authority of the Senate." Together these statements show generals prioritizing personal power over loyalty to the state = A.

B (refusing campaigns abroad) contradicts paragraph 2, which explicitly says Rome conquered much of the Mediterranean. C (consistently following the Senate) is the opposite of the passage, which describes generals challenging Senate authority. D (foreign funding) has no support in the text.
言い換え:Paraphrase: Passage: "personal armies loyal to them rather than to the Republic" → A: "personal ambitions often outweighed their commitment to the Republic"

設問 6 — 修辞目的Question 6 — Rhetorical Purpose
なぜ著者は第2段落で好中球とマクロファージに言及するのか。Why does the author mention neutrophils and macrophages in paragraph 2?
正解: ACorrect: A あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 体の即時防衛に関わる細胞の例を示すため ✓To give examples of cells involved in the body's immediate defense ✓
B ワクチンがどう免疫系を刺激するかを説明するためTo explain how vaccines stimulate the immune system
C Bリンパ球とTリンパ球の機能を対比するためTo contrast the functions of B lymphocytes and T lymphocytes
D 自然免疫が獲得免疫より重要だと論じるためTo argue that innate immunity is more important than adaptive immunity
解説:Explanation: 第2段落は自然免疫を「the body's first line of defense（体の第一防衛線）」と論じ、「general-purpose immune cells that respond quickly to any foreign substance（あらゆる異物に素早く反応する汎用免疫細胞）」と言及する。好中球とマクロファージはこれらの細胞の例として導入されている = A。

B（ワクチンの説明）は第4段落の内容で、第2段落ではない。C（BとTリンパ球の対比）は第3段落の内容。D（重要性の順位付け）はパッセージが支持しない比較主張で、自然免疫と獲得免疫のランキングは与えられていない。 Paragraph 2 discusses innate immunity as "the body's first line of defense" and mentions "general-purpose immune cells that respond quickly." Neutrophils and macrophages are given as examples of these cells = A.

B discusses vaccines, which appear in paragraph 4, not paragraph 2. C mentions B and T lymphocytes, which are in paragraph 3. D makes a comparison claim that the passage never supports — no ranking of importance is given.

パッセージに基づくと、特定のウイルス感染から回復した人について何が推論できるか。Based on the passage, what can be inferred about a person who has recovered from a particular viral infection?
正解: BCorrect: B あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 彼らの免疫系は、初回感染時よりもそのウイルスを認識するのが遅くなる。Their immune system will recognize the virus more slowly than during the first infection.
B 既に回復した病気のワクチン接種を受けても追加効果は限定的だろう。 ✓Receiving a vaccine against a disease they have already recovered from would provide limited additional benefit. ✓
C 彼らの自然免疫系が永久に強くなる。Their innate immune system will become permanently stronger.
D 彼らはもはや皮膚のような物理的バリアを必要としない。They no longer need physical barriers such as skin to protect them.
本文に基づく推論:Text-based inference: 第4段落は、メモリー細胞が同じ病原体が再侵入したときに免疫系が「mount a much faster and stronger response（はるかに速く強い反応を起こす）」ことを可能にし、「often eliminating the threat before any symptoms appear（症状が現れる前に脅威を排除することが多い）」と述べる。回復した人はすでにこれらのメモリー細胞を持っており、ワクチンが提供する保護は冗長になる = B。

A は本文と矛盾。本文ははっきりと、メモリー細胞によって再感染時には「faster」な反応をすると述べている。「より遅い認識」とは正反対。C は自然免疫と獲得免疫を混同。メモリー細胞は獲得免疫の一部で、自然免疫ではない。D は不合理。物理的バリアは過去の感染とは無関係に必要。 Paragraph 4 states that memory cells allow the immune system to "mount a much faster and stronger response" when the same pathogen returns, "often eliminating the threat before any symptoms appear." A recovered person already has these memory cells, so vaccination would add little = B.

A directly contradicts the passage, which states that memory cells produce a "faster" response on re-exposure — slower recognition is the opposite. C confuses innate and adaptive immunity — memory cells are part of adaptive immunity, not innate. D is absurd — physical barriers remain necessary regardless of past infections.
言い換え:Paraphrase: Passage: "mount a much faster and stronger response" → B (combined with the fact that vaccines train the immune system) implies a prior infection already provides what the vaccine would.

パッセージによれば、獲得免疫反応においてBリンパ球はどんな役割を果たすか。According to the passage, what role do B lymphocytes play in the adaptive immune response?
正解: BCorrect: B あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 皮膚や粘膜と同様の物理的バリアを形成する。They form physical barriers similar to the skin and mucous membranes.
B 病原体に結合してそれを破壊の対象として印を付ける抗体を産生する。 ✓They produce antibodies that attach to pathogens and mark them for destruction. ✓
C 体内の感染した細胞を直接攻撃して破壊する。They directly attack and destroy infected cells in the body.
D 将来の免疫反応を刺激するため弱められた病原体を放出する。They release weakened pathogens to stimulate future immune responses.

A（物理的バリア）は第2段落の自然免疫（皮膚・粘膜）の役割で、Bリンパ球の機能ではない。C（感染細胞を直接攻撃）はTリンパ球の役割であり、同じ段落に「T lymphocytes directly attack infected cells」と書かれている。D（弱められた病原体を放出）はワクチンの概念（第4段落）と混同したもの。Bリンパ球はそうした働きをしない。 Paragraph 3 explicitly states: "B lymphocytes produce antibodies that bind to the pathogen and mark it for destruction" = B.

A (physical barriers) refers to innate immunity in paragraph 2 (skin, mucous membranes), not to B lymphocytes. C (directly attacking infected cells) is the role of T lymphocytes, stated in the same paragraph as "T lymphocytes directly attack infected cells." D (releasing weakened pathogens) confuses B lymphocytes with the concept of vaccines (paragraph 4); B lymphocytes do not do this.

設問 10 — 推論Question 10 — Inference
ワクチンが疾病予防の有効な方法である理由について、何が推論できるか。What can be inferred about why vaccines are an effective method of disease prevention?
正解: DCorrect: D あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 感染に対する体の物理的バリアを永久に強化するから。They permanently strengthen the body's physical barriers against infection.
B 自然免疫をより強く速い防衛システムで置き換えるから。They replace innate immunity with a stronger, faster defense system.
C 病原体に接触した瞬間に戦う、出来合いの抗体を体に供給するから。They supply the body with ready-made antibodies that fight the pathogen on contact.
D 病気を引き起こさずに、より速い反応を可能にするメモリー細胞を作るから。 ✓They create memory cells that allow a faster response without causing illness. ✓
本文に基づく推論:Text-based inference: 第4段落は二つの主要な情報を組み合わせる：（1）メモリー細胞は同じ病原体が再侵入したときに「much faster and stronger response」を可能にする、（2）ワクチンは「introduces a harmless form of a pathogen to train the immune system without causing disease（病気を引き起こさずに免疫系を訓練するため病原体の無害な形を導入する）」。これらを組み合わせると、ワクチンが有効なのは病気なしでメモリー細胞を作るから = D。

A（物理的バリアを強化）は誤り。物理的バリアは自然免疫の一部で、ワクチンが影響を与えるものではない。B（自然免疫を置き換える）も誤り。ワクチンは獲得免疫を訓練するが、自然免疫を置き換えるわけではない。C（出来合いの抗体を供給）は誤り。本文はワクチンが免疫系を「訓練する（train）」＝体が自らメモリー細胞や抗体を作ると述べており、出来合いの抗体を与えるわけではない（それは受動免疫の説明）。 Paragraph 4 combines two key pieces of information: (1) memory cells allow a "much faster and stronger response" when the same pathogen returns, and (2) vaccines "introduce a harmless form of a pathogen to train the immune system without causing disease." Combining these, vaccines work by creating memory cells without causing illness = D.

A (strengthening physical barriers) is wrong — physical barriers are part of innate immunity and are not affected by vaccines. B (replacing innate immunity) is wrong — vaccines train adaptive immunity but do not replace innate immunity. C (supplying ready-made antibodies) is wrong — the passage says vaccines "train the immune system," so the body makes its own response and memory cells; ready-made antibodies would describe passive immunity.
言い換え:Paraphrase: Passage: "train the immune system without causing disease" + "memory cells" → D: "create memory cells that allow a faster response without causing illness"
重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 推論問題では、根拠はパッセージ内のみ。 自分の知識や意見を持ち込まないこと。
3. 主旨 = 最初の文 + 最後の文。 段落中央の細部に引き込まれないように。
4. 文脈中の語彙：代入して確認。 各選択肢で語を置き換え、最も自然に読めるものを選ぶ。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For inference questions, only the passage counts as evidence. Do not mix in your own knowledge or opinions.
3. Main Idea = first sentence + last sentence. Do not get pulled into middle-paragraph details.
4. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check. Replace the word with each option and see which one reads most naturally.
