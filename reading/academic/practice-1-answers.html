Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "track goods, debts, and transactions" → Answer: "keep track of goods and commercial exchanges"
Passage: "play a crucial role in regulating host populations" → Answer: "help maintain balance by controlling the size of host populations"
2 Understand passage structure
First sentence → Topic / Main claim
Middle → Details, examples, data
Last sentence → Conclusion / Summary

Main Idea questions can often be solved by reading just the first and last sentences.
Q1: Passage 1 opens with "The invention of writing is widely regarded as one of the most significant achievements" and ends with "they all followed a comparable path: from pictures, to symbols for words, to symbols for sounds." Together these give the main idea: how writing systems originated and evolved = A.
3 Inference must come from the text only
Do not mix in your own knowledge or opinions. Only information stated in the passage counts as evidence.
最もよくある間違い:Most common mistake:
✓ Passage says "parasites regulate host populations" → "they help maintain balance" (valid inference from the text)
✗ Passage says "parasites regulate host populations" → "they are beneficial to the hosts" (unsupported — the passage says they cause damage)
4 Main Idea: first + last sentences are key
For Main Idea questions, do not get distracted by specific details in the middle paragraphs. The correct answer covers the entire passage, not just one section.
罠のパターン:Trap pattern: An option that accurately describes one paragraph but ignores the rest of the passage. This is a detail, not the main idea.

A 異なる地域の文字体系が同様の経路で発展したことを示すため ✓To show that writing systems in different regions developed along similar paths ✓

設問 5 — 推論Question 5 — Inference

A 文字体系が一般の人にとって学習困難になったIt made the writing system too difficult for ordinary people to learn

C 文字が話し言葉との結びつきを失う結果になったIt caused writing to lose its connection to the spoken language

本文に基づく推論:Text-based inference: 第2段落に「Writers started using wedge-shaped marks pressed into soft clay tablets, which were faster to produce than detailed drawings」とある。「速く作れる」 → 効率性を求めた移行と推論できる = B。

A introduces "difficulty for ordinary people," never mentioned. C is the opposite — the passage says symbols later "came to represent sounds," strengthening the connection to spoken language. D is contradicted by paragraph 3, which describes similar shifts in Egypt and China.

解説:Explanation: 第2段落は「Mutualism is a relationship in which both species benefit（相利共生は両種が利益を得る関係）」で始まり、最もよく知られた例として「One of the most well-known examples」とクマノミとイソギンチャクを導入する。この例は相利共生を例示する目的で使われている = C。

A は刺胞という細部に注目しすぎており、目的を捉えていない。B は「最も重要」という過剰表現で本文に根拠なし。D は誤り。寄生は別の段落で扱われており、この段落は相利共生のみが話題。 Paragraph 2 opens with "Mutualism is a relationship in which both species benefit" and then introduces the clownfish-anemone example with "One of the most well-known examples." The example serves to illustrate mutualism = C.

A focuses on only one detail (stinging tentacles) and misses the purpose. B uses extreme language ("most important") with no support. D is wrong because parasitism is discussed in a separate paragraph; this paragraph is solely about mutualism.

設問 10 — 推論Question 10 — Inference
科学者が片利共生（commensalism）と相利共生（mutualism）の区別をどう見ているか、何が推論できるか。What can be inferred about how scientists view the distinction between commensalism and mutualism?
正解: ACorrect: A あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 現在「片利共生」と分類されている関係の一部は、科学者がまだ検出できていない利益を含んでいる可能性がある ✓Some commensalism cases may actually involve benefits scientists have not yet detected ✓
B 両者は本質的に同じ関係であると考えているThey consider the two types to be fundamentally the same relationship
C 海では片利共生のほうが相利共生よりはるかに多いと信じているThey believe commensalism is far more common than mutualism in oceans
D 真に一方的な関係は実際には存在しないと結論づけているThey have concluded that truly one-sided relationships do not actually exist
本文に基づく推論:Text-based inference: 本文は「Scientists note, however, that the boundary between commensalism and mutualism is not always clear」と「subtle benefits may be difficult to detect」の 2 文を組み合わせる必要がある。境界が不明瞭な理由は、片利共生と分類された関係に未検出の利益が潜んでいる可能性があるから。よって「現在片利共生と呼ばれている一部の関係は、実は相利共生かもしれない」と推論できる = A。

Together they imply that some relationships currently called commensalism might actually be undetected mutualism = A.

B is wrong — the passage never claims the categories are identical. C introduces a frequency claim not in the text. D is too strong — the passage only says one-sided relationships "may" involve subtle benefits, not that they "do not exist."
推論ステップ:Inference step: "boundary not always clear" + "subtle benefits difficult to detect" → A: some commensalism cases may include unrecognized benefits.
重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 推論問題では、根拠はパッセージ内のみ。 自分の知識や意見を持ち込まないこと。
3. 主旨 = 最初の文 + 最後の文。 段落中央の細部に引き込まれないように。
4. 文脈中の語彙：代入して確認。 各選択肢で語を置き換え、最も自然に読めるものを選ぶ。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For inference questions, only the passage counts as evidence. Do not mix in your own knowledge or opinions.
3. Main Idea = first sentence + last sentence. Do not get pulled into middle-paragraph details.
4. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check. Replace the word with each option and see which one reads most naturally.
