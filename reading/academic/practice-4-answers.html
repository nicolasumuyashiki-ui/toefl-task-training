Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "carried not only goods but also sacred texts, philosophies, and cultural practices" → Answer: "carried goods as well as religious texts and cultural practices"
2 Rhetorical Purpose: ask "why," not "what"
Purpose questions ask why the author mentions something, not what it means. Look at the sentence before the mentioned detail — it usually contains the claim the example is meant to support.
Q4: Before mentioning cacti and rainforest plants, paragraph 3 states that "different species have developed remarkable adaptations." The examples illustrate this claim = D.
3 Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check
Replace the highlighted word with each answer choice and see which one reads most naturally and preserves the original meaning.
Q2: "One of the most deep and far-reaching effects of Silk Road contact..." reads naturally and matches the context of major religious and cultural transmission = C.
4 Sentence Insertion: use discourse markers as clues
Words like "Furthermore," "However," "For example," "In contrast," at the beginning of the inserted sentence tell you how it connects to the surrounding text. Match the discourse marker to the logical relationship between the sentences.
Q6: "Furthermore" signals additional information. The sentence about improved crops in tropical regions adds to the idea of developing resilient crop varieties in the previous sentence = B.

設問 4 — 修辞目的Question 4 — Rhetorical Purpose
なぜ著者は第3段落でサボテンと熱帯雨林の植物に言及するのか。Why does the author mention cacti and tropical rainforest plants in paragraph 3?
正解: DCorrect: D あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 光合成が極端な環境でのみ起きることを示すためTo show that photosynthesis occurs only in extreme environments
B クロロフィルが緑の光を反射する理由を説明するためTo explain why chlorophyll reflects green light
C 砂漠植物が熱帯雨林植物より光合成効率が高いと論じるためTo argue that desert plants are more efficient at photosynthesis than rainforest plants
D 異なる植物がどう環境に合わせて光合成戦略を適応させてきたかを示すため ✓To illustrate how different plants have adapted their photosynthetic strategies to suit their environments ✓
解説:Explanation: 文には「One of the most profound effects of Silk Road contact was the transmission of religious beliefs」とあり、仏教が大陸を越えて広がった例が続く。これらは明らかに深く広範囲な影響 = C。代入：「最も深く広範囲な効果の一つ」自然に読める。

A（予期しない）はパッセージは効果が驚きとは示唆しない。B（一時的）は逆。宗教変化は持続的だった。D（十分に文書化された）は歴史記録を表し、効果の性質ではない。 Paragraph 3 opens by stating that "different species have developed remarkable adaptations to survive in challenging environments." Cacti (CAM photosynthesis in deserts) and rainforest plants (broad leaves for light capture) are then presented as specific examples of this claim = D.

A uses the extreme word "only" — the passage does not say photosynthesis occurs only in extreme environments. B refers to content from paragraph 2, not paragraph 3. C makes a comparison of efficiency that the passage never makes.

A ありふれたordinary
B 危険なrisky
C 注目すべき ✓notable ✓

文脈確認:Context check: 第2段落には「Merchants, monks, and diplomats carried not only goods but also sacred texts, philosophies, and cultural practices that transformed the societies they reached」とある。これは直接 B を支持。

A（大学）は言及されていない。C（疫病防止）は議論されていない。D は商人と外交官の競争を述べるが、パッセージは協力する運搬者として並列している。 The sentence reads "different species have developed remarkable adaptations to survive in challenging environments." 設問 6 — 文挿入Question 6 — Sentence Insertion
Look at the four squares in paragraph 4 that indicate where the following sentence could be added:

"Furthermore, these improved crops would be especially valuable in tropical regions, where rising temperatures already threaten staple food production."
正解: BCorrect: B あなたの回答: —Your answer: —
A 文1の後（「…in the context of climate change.」の後）After sentence 1 (after "...in the context of climate change.")
B 文2の後（「…more resilient to environmental stress.」の後） ✓After sentence 2 (after "...more resilient to environmental stress.") ✓
C 文3の後（「…agriculture and food security.」の後）After sentence 3 (after "...agriculture and food security.")
Discourse marker clue: 第3段落は「different species have developed remarkable adaptations to survive in challenging environments」で始まる。サボテン（砂漠でのCAM光合成）と熱帯雨林植物（光捕捉のための広い葉）が、この主張の具体例として提示される = D。

A は極端な「only」を使う。パッセージは光合成が極端な環境でのみ起きるとは言わない。B は第2段落の内容を指す。C はパッセージにない効率比較を行う。 The word "Furthermore" signals that the sentence adds information to a closely related preceding idea. Sentence 2 discusses developing "crop varieties that are more resilient to environmental stress," and the insertion sentence elaborates on where those crops would be most useful. "These improved crops" directly refers back to the resilient crop varieties mentioned in sentence 2 = B.

A is incorrect because sentence 1 introduces general interest in climate change but does not mention crops, so "these improved crops" would have no clear referent. C is incorrect because sentence 3 is a concluding statement about the broader implications of photosynthesis research; inserting a specific detail about tropical regions after the conclusion would disrupt the paragraph's logical flow.
重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 修辞目的問題では、その詳細の前の文を見る。 例が支える主張は通常その直前にある。
3. 文脈中の語彙：代入して確認。 各選択肢で語を置き換え、最も自然に読めるものを選ぶ。
4. 文挿入問題では、ディスコースマーカーを手がかりに。 「Furthermore」や「However」のような語は、文がどう前後と接続するかを示す。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For Rhetorical Purpose questions, look at the sentence before the detail. The claim the example supports is usually right before it.
3. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check. Replace the word with each option and see which one reads most naturally.
4. For Sentence Insertion, use discourse markers as clues. Words like "Furthermore" and "However" signal how the sentence connects to its neighbors.
