Academic Reading 攻略 TipsAcademic Reading Strategy Tips
1 Correct answers are always paraphrases
The correct answer restates the passage in different words. Be suspicious of options that copy exact words from the passage — they are often traps.
この練習からの言い換え例:Paraphrase examples from this practice:
Passage: "a substance that inhibited bacterial growth" → Answer: "suppressed" bacterial growth
Passage: "providing strong evidence that not everything revolved around the Earth" → Answer: "provided evidence against the Earth-centered model"
2 Rhetorical Purpose: ask "why did the author include this?"
Purpose questions ask about the function of a detail, not its content. Ask yourself: "What point is the author trying to make by mentioning this?" The answer usually connects the detail back to the paragraph's main claim.
Q6: Galileo's discovery of Jupiter's moons is mentioned right after the topic sentence about telescopes transforming astronomy. The discovery serves as evidence that telescopes revealed facts challenging the geocentric model = A.
3 Vocabulary in Context: substitute and check
Replace the highlighted word with each answer choice. The correct answer must make sense in the specific sentence, not just as a dictionary definition.
Common mistake: Choosing a meaning that is technically correct for the word but does not fit the context. Always re-read the sentence with your chosen answer plugged in.
4 Inference: each advance = new capability
When a passage describes a series of developments, look for the pattern. If each new technology is described as revealing something new, you can infer that progress means expanded capability.
Q10: The passage describes optical telescopes revealing moons, reflecting telescopes observing fainter objects, radio telescopes detecting invisible radiation, and space telescopes imaging distant galaxies. The pattern: each advance revealed previously undetectable phenomena = C.

Note: Inference questions never require outside knowledge. The correct answer must be a logical conclusion supported directly by specific sentences in the passage.

重要ポイントKey Takeaways
1. 正解は常にパッセージの言い換えである。 パッセージと同じ単語を使った選択肢は罠であることが多い。
2. 修辞目的問題では、その詳細を段落の主張と結びつける。 詳細は証拠や例として機能する。
3. 文脈中の語彙：代入して再読する。 正解は当該文に自然に収まらなければならない。
4. 推論問題では、パッセージ全体のパターンを探す。 一連の進歩を述べているなら、推論は全体の傾向に関するもの。
5. 文挿入問題では、ディスコースマーカーを手がかりに。 「Furthermore」や「As a result」のような語は、文がどう前後と接続するかを示す。代名詞参照（例: "these moons"）も同様に重要 — 挿入位置の直前に明確な指示対象が必要。 1. Correct answers are always paraphrases of the passage. Options using the same words as the passage are often traps.
2. For rhetorical purpose questions, connect the detail to the paragraph's main claim. The detail serves as evidence or illustration.
3. Vocabulary in Context: substitute and re-read. The correct answer must fit the specific sentence naturally.
4. For inference questions, look for patterns across the passage. If the passage describes a series of advances, the inference is about the overall trend.
5. For Sentence Insertion, use discourse markers as clues. Words like "Furthermore" and "As a result" signal how the sentence connects to its neighbors. Also check pronoun references like "these moons" — they must have a clear referent before the insertion point.
