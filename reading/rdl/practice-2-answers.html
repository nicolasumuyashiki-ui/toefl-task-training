Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords (目的特定)
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
Reminder: This is a reminder that... / Please be advised...
Instruction: Please bring... / You must submit...
Invitation: You are invited to... / We are hosting...
Q1: The email opens with "The Campus Health Center will be hosting free flu vaccination clinics" and then describes what to bring and who should consult a doctor. This is an informational notification about a clinic = A.
2 NOT/EXCEPT: Use elimination (NOT問題消去法)
Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned in the passage. The remaining one is the answer.
Common traps:
- A word from the passage appears in the option, but the meaning is different
- The option sounds reasonable but is never stated in the text
- Partially correct but adds information not in the passage
3 Inference questions: stick to the text (推論は本文のみ)
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.
Q4: The notice says "Students who received the scholarship in a previous year must reapply; renewal is not automatic." This directly supports C — they must go through the same process as new applicants.
Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the main purpose of this email?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A To inform students about an upcoming flu vaccination clinic and how to prepare ✓
B To warn students about a flu outbreak on campus
C To explain the side effects of the flu vaccine in detail
D To require all students to get vaccinated before the semester ends
Explanation: The email announces the dates and location of the flu clinic, explains what students need to bring, and provides guidance for students with certain medical conditions. This is clearly an informational message about an upcoming clinic = A.

B is wrong because no outbreak is mentioned. C is wrong because side effects are not discussed at all. D is wrong because the vaccine is described as optional (walk-ins are "welcome," not required).
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, what advantage do students gain by scheduling an appointment in advance?
Correct: D Your answer: —
A They will receive a free health checkup along with the vaccination.
B They will be given a discount on future health services.
C They can choose a specific type of vaccine.
D They will be served before walk-in students and wait less. ✓
Explanation: The email states: "students who schedule an appointment through the student health portal will receive priority service and shorter wait times" = D.

A, B, and C are not mentioned anywhere in the email. This is a straightforward detail question — the key phrase "priority service and shorter wait times" maps directly to D.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — NOT/EXCEPT
According to the notice, all of the following are required application documents EXCEPT
Correct: B Your answer: —
A an unofficial academic transcript
B a proof of financial need from the student's family ✓
C a personal statement about academic goals
D a letter of recommendation from a faculty member
Elimination method:
A — "An unofficial academic transcript" ✓ mentioned
C — "A personal statement of no more than 500 words describing your academic goals" ✓ mentioned
D — "One letter of recommendation from a current faculty member" ✓ mentioned
B — Financial need is never mentioned in the notice. This is an Academic Merit Scholarship based on GPA, not financial need. B is the correct answer.

Trap alert: Because it is a "Financial Aid Office" notice, you might assume financial need is relevant. But the passage only mentions GPA and credit hours as eligibility criteria.
Question 4 — Inference
What can be inferred about students who received the scholarship last year?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A They are guaranteed to receive the scholarship again.
B They are no longer eligible to apply for the scholarship.
C They must go through the same application process as new applicants. ✓
D They will receive a larger award amount than first-time applicants.
Explanation: The notice states: "Students who received the scholarship in a previous year must reapply; renewal is not automatic." This means past recipients have no special guarantee and must submit a full application just like everyone else = C.

A directly contradicts the passage ("renewal is not automatic"). B is wrong because the notice says they "must reapply," meaning they ARE eligible. D has no basis in the text — no different award amounts are mentioned.
