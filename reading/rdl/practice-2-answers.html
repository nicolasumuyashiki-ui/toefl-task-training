Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords (目的特定)
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
Reminder: This is a reminder that... / Please be advised...
Promotion: Sign up before... / Limited time offer...
Invitation: You are invited to... / We are hosting...
Q4: The ad opens with a question to the reader and then introduces a "spring deal" with a discounted rate for students. The whole text is built around promoting this offer = A.
2 NOT/EXCEPT: Use elimination (NOT問題消去法)
Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned in the passage. The remaining one is the answer.
Common traps:
- A word from the passage appears in the option, but the meaning is different
- The option sounds reasonable but is never stated in the text
- Partially correct but adds information not in the passage
3 Inference questions: stick to the text (推論は本文のみ)
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.
Q6: The ad says "All instruments except drums are available to borrow during your lesson." From the word "except," we can infer that drum students must bring or buy their own instrument = C.
Passage 1 Menu
Question 1 — Factual Information
According to the menu, what is included with the price of a sandwich?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A A specialty drink of the customer's choice
B A small side salad at no additional charge ✓
C A freshly baked almond croissant from the oven
D A bottle of water and a piece of fruit
Explanation: The menu intro states: "All sandwiches are served with a small side salad" = B.

A is a classic trap — a specialty drink is available, but only as a $2 paid add-on, not included. C and D are not mentioned with the sandwiches at all.
Question 2 — Inference
What can be inferred about a student who arrives at the cafe at 11:30 AM on a Tuesday?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A The student can still receive the 10% student discount.
B The student will not qualify for the morning discount. ✓
C The student must order a full meal to get any discount.
D The student can only buy pastries at that time of day.
Explanation: The note states the 10% student discount is only valid "before 10:00 AM, Monday through Friday." 11:30 AM is after the cutoff, so the student cannot receive the discount = B.

A directly contradicts the time cutoff. C and D are never stated in the menu.
Question 3 — NOT/EXCEPT (Negative Factual)
According to the menu, all of the following are mentioned as ingredients EXCEPT
Correct: C Your answer: —
A feta cheese in the roasted vegetable wrap
B real maple syrup in one of the coffee drinks
C dark chocolate chips used in the almond croissant ✓
D bacon included in the smoked turkey sandwich
Elimination method:
A — "Zucchini, red peppers, feta cheese on a spinach tortilla" ✓ mentioned
B — "Espresso, steamed milk, real maple syrup" ✓ mentioned
D — "House-smoked turkey, bacon, tomato on sourdough" ✓ mentioned
C — Chocolate chips are never mentioned. The almond croissant description only says "Baked fresh daily before 8:00 AM." C is the answer.
Passage 2 Advertisement
Question 4 — Text Purpose
What is the main purpose of this advertisement?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A To promote a special student rate for music lessons ✓
B To announce the grand opening of a new music studio
C To recruit instructors for a university music program
D To advertise used instruments being sold to students
Explanation: The ad's central message is the "Spring Semester Promotion" offering the first month of lessons at half price for university students. Everything else supports this offer = A.

B is wrong because the ad never calls this a grand opening — it is an existing studio running a promotion. C is wrong because the ad targets students as customers, not instructors. D has no basis in the text.
Question 5 — Factual Information
According to the advertisement, what will students receive if they sign up for a three-month package?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A A free instrument to keep after the course ends
B A complimentary half-hour recording session at the studio ✓
C A discounted rate for additional group theory workshops
D A certificate of completion signed by the instructor
Explanation: The ad states: "Students who commit to a three-month package will also receive a free 30-minute recording session in our home studio at the end of the term." "Complimentary half-hour" paraphrases "free 30-minute" = B.

A confuses the "borrow" policy with a giveaway — students can borrow instruments during lessons, not keep them. C and D are not mentioned.
Question 6 — Inference
What can be inferred about students who want to learn the drums?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A They are not allowed to take lessons at the studio.
B They must attend only group lessons, not private ones.
C They will need to provide their own instrument for lessons. ✓
D They must pay a higher rate than other instrument students.
Explanation: The ad says: "All instruments except drums are available to borrow during your lesson." The word "except" implies that drums are not lendable, so drum students must bring or buy their own = C.

A contradicts the list of offered lessons, which includes drums. B is wrong because drums are offered as one-on-one. D has no basis in the text — no pricing differences by instrument are mentioned.
