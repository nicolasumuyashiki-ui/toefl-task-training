Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
Request: Could you... / I would appreciate if...
Reminder: This is a reminder that... / Please be advised...
Q1: The email opens with "The Campus Safety Office will conduct mandatory fire drills" and then describes when, where, and how to evacuate. This is a notification about upcoming fire drills and procedures = C.
2 For detail questions, locate the key phrase first
Steps:
1. Identify the key phrase in the question (e.g., "specific drill date")
2. Scan the passage for that exact phrase or a synonym
3. Read the sentence containing it carefully
4. Match it to an answer choice
Q2: The question asks how occupants will learn the specific date. Scanning for "specific date" leads to: "Building coordinators will post the specific date and assembly location on the bulletin board near each main entrance" = B.
3 Inference questions: stick to the text
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.
Common trap: An option that sounds reasonable based on real-world knowledge but is NOT supported by the passage text.
Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the main purpose of this email?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A To report the results of a recent fire safety inspection
B To request volunteers for the campus fire department
C To inform building occupants about scheduled fire drills and evacuation procedures ✓
D To announce the installation of new fire alarm systems across campus
Explanation: The email announces upcoming fire drills (October 13-17), provides evacuation instructions (use emergency exits, gather at assembly points), and tells occupants how to find their building's specific schedule. This is clearly an informational message about fire drill procedures = C.

A is wrong because the email discusses upcoming drills, not past inspection results. B is unrelated to the content. D is wrong because the email never mentions installing new alarm systems.
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, how will occupants learn the specific drill date for their building?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A By checking the campus website for a master schedule
B By reading a notice posted on the bulletin board near the main entrance ✓
C By attending a mandatory safety meeting the week before
D By receiving a personal email from the building coordinator
Explanation: The email states: "Building coordinators will post the specific date and assembly location on the bulletin board near each main entrance by October 8." This directly matches B.

A mentions a campus website, which is never referenced. C and D describe methods not mentioned in the email. This is a straightforward detail-matching question.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — Factual Detail
What happens if a student withdraws from a course after November 7?
Correct: D Your answer: —
A The course is removed from the transcript entirely.
B The student receives a "W" that does not affect the GPA.
C The student must wait until the following semester to re-enroll.
D The student receives a "WF" grade that counts as a failing grade in the GPA. ✓
Explanation: The notice states: "Students who withdraw after November 7 will receive a 'WF' (Withdrawal with Failure), which is calculated as a failing grade in your GPA" = D.

A describes what happens before September 19 (add/drop period), not after November 7. B describes what happens before November 7. C is not mentioned anywhere in the notice. Be careful to match the correct deadline to the correct consequence.
Question 4 — Inference
Why does the notice advise students receiving financial aid to consult the Financial Aid Office before withdrawing?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A Because withdrawing from a course could reduce their course load and put their financial aid at risk ✓
B Because the Financial Aid Office must approve all withdrawal requests
C Because financial aid students are not allowed to withdraw from any courses
D Because the Financial Aid Office offers free tutoring to struggling students
Explanation: The notice states: "dropping below full-time status may affect your aid eligibility." From this, we can infer that withdrawing from a course could reduce a student's course load below full-time, which may put their financial aid at risk = A.

B is wrong because the notice says withdrawal requires advisor approval, not Financial Aid Office approval. C uses extreme language ("not allowed") which is not supported. D introduces tutoring, which is never mentioned.
