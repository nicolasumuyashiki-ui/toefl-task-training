Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 For NOT/EXCEPT, verify each option
In "all of the following EXCEPT" questions, three options are in the passage and one is not. Check each option against the text — the odd one out is your answer.
Q2: The notice mentions the outdoor track (A), online fitness classes (B), and Hartley Hall fitness room (D). A public community pool (C) is never discussed, so C is the answer.
2 Inference from consequences
Notices often include deadlines. If a deadline is stated, think about what happens after it passes — that is usually what an inference question targets.
Trap: Do not invent extra consequences (fees, penalties) that the notice never mentions.
3 SMS: follow the conversation flow
Text messages move fast. Read each bubble in order and note who agrees, who disagrees, and what plan is made at the end. The final bubbles usually settle the plan.
Q5: The last messages settle on a Zoom video call at 4:00 PM to review the essay draft, not an in-person meeting.
Passage 1 Notice
Question 1 — Factual Detail
According to the notice, why will the Recreation Center be closed?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A To install new exercise equipment in the main gymnasium
B To replace the gym floor and fix a leak above the pool ✓
C To host a series of athletic tournaments for visiting schools
D To clean the building after damage caused by a recent storm
Explanation: The notice says contractors will "replace the wooden flooring in the main gymnasium and repair the roof above the swimming pool" = B.

A is wrong because no new equipment is mentioned. C is never stated. D is a trap: storms caused the leak, but the closure is for repairs, not cleaning.
Question 2 — Negative Factual (EXCEPT)
All of the following are mentioned as alternatives during the closure EXCEPT
Correct: C Your answer: —
A using the outdoor track during normal hours
B joining group fitness classes through an online platform
C swimming at a nearby public community pool ✓
D exercising at the Hartley Residence Hall fitness room
Explanation: The notice lists the outdoor track (A), online group fitness classes via Zoom (B), and the Hartley Residence Hall fitness room (D). A public community pool is never mentioned, so C is the EXCEPT answer.
Question 3 — Inference
What can be inferred about items kept in the locker rooms?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A Belongings not collected by February 6 may be inaccessible during repairs. ✓
B Users will be charged an additional fee for extending their locker rental.
C All personal items will automatically be moved to Hartley Residence Hall.
D Staff will photograph the contents before the contractors begin work.
Explanation: The notice says items should be removed before February 6 and the building is closed from February 9-15. From this we can infer that anything left behind will be locked inside the closed building = A.

B is contradicted by "at no extra cost." C and D are never mentioned.

Question 5 — Inference
What will the two students most likely do at 4:00 PM?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A Meet at Cafe Luna once the rain has stopped
B Review the essay draft together during a video call ✓
C Submit their finished essays to their professor online
D Walk to the library to study together in person
Explanation: Priya agrees to a video call and says she will send a Zoom link at 4, adding "Bring the draft of the essay so we can go over it together." So they will review the essay on a video call = B.

A contradicts the plan to skip the cafe. C and D are not discussed.
