1 Schedules: read the headers and the footnotes
A schedule has two layers of information: the table itself (days, hours, names) and the notes above or below (rules and exceptions). Questions often target the notes.
Q2: The schedule's intro says sessions are "limited to 25 minutes per student when others are waiting." A busy day therefore means a shorter session = B.
2 Menus: scan the descriptions for optional phrases
Common option signals on a menu:
Add X for $Y / available on request / choose from ...

If an EXCEPT question asks which option is not offered, an answer that sounds reasonable but is never written on the menu is the trap you are looking for.
Trap: common real-world substitutions (e.g. almond milk for buttermilk) that the menu simply does not mention.
3 Inference = what must be true, not what might be true
A correct inference is directly supported by the text. If you have to add outside knowledge or make a new assumption, it is probably a distractor.
Q5: The menu says "students save 15% with a valid campus ID." A 15% discount must mean the student pays less than the printed price = B.
Passage 1 Schedule
Question 1 — Factual Information
According to the schedule, who staffs the Writing Center on Wednesdays?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A An undergraduate peer tutor from the English department
B A faculty mentor assigned to that weekday shift ✓
C A graduate student tutor from the writing program
D A library staff member trained in academic writing
Explanation: Read the "Tutor on Duty" column of the table. The Wednesday row says "Faculty mentor," which matches B.

A is never listed — no undergraduate tutors appear in the table. C describes the Monday and Friday shifts, not Wednesday. D introduces a "library staff" role that the schedule does not mention at all.
Question 2 — Inference
What can be inferred about a student who arrives when several others are already waiting?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A The student will be asked to reserve a later appointment.
B The student may receive a shorter tutoring session than usual. ✓
C The student will be sent to a different campus library.
D The student must pay a small fee to keep their place.
Explanation: The intro note says sessions are "limited to 25 minutes per student when others are waiting." This means the standard session could be longer, but gets capped on busy days — so the student receives a shorter-than-usual session = B.

A contradicts the "no appointment needed, first come, first served" policy. C and D introduce ideas (different library, fees) that the schedule never mentions.
Passage 2 Menu
Question 3 — Factual Information
According to the menu, what is served with the Classic Buttermilk Pancakes?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A Whipped cream and a side of bacon strips
B Maple syrup along with fruit of the season ✓
C Powdered sugar and a small glass of juice
D Honey butter and locally sourced berries
Explanation: The pancake description says "a stack of three pancakes with maple syrup and seasonal fruit." Answer B paraphrases this as "maple syrup along with fruit of the season" = B.

A, C, and D all mention toppings (whipped cream, powdered sugar, honey butter, berries) that sound like breakfast items but are never listed in the menu.
Question 4 — NOT/EXCEPT
All of the following are mentioned as optional additions or choices EXCEPT
Correct: D Your answer: —
A adding a poached egg to the avocado toast
B requesting a vegan version of the pancake batter
C choosing between decaf and regular house coffee
D substituting almond milk in place of buttermilk ✓
Elimination method:
A — "Add a poached egg for $1.50" ✓ mentioned
B — "Vegan batter available on request" ✓ mentioned
C — "Choose from house blend, decaf, or loose-leaf tea" ✓ mentioned
D — Almond milk substitution is never mentioned anywhere on the menu. This is the correct answer.
Question 5 — Inference
What can be inferred about a student who presents a valid campus ID at brunch?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A The student can stack the campus discount with other promotions.
B The student will pay less than the listed price on the menu. ✓
C The student is served brunch items outside normal weekend hours.
D The student receives complimentary refills of fresh orange juice.
Explanation: The note says "students save 15% with a valid campus ID." A 15% saving directly implies the student pays less than the listed price = B.

A directly contradicts "not combinable with other discounts." C contradicts the fixed brunch hours (9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on weekends only). D confuses the "bottomless coffee or tea" offer with orange juice, which is a separately priced item.
