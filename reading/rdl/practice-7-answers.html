Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
Request: Could you... / I would appreciate if...
Confirmation: I'm writing to confirm...
Reminder: We would like to remind...
Q1: The email describes election dates, voting method, and login requirements. This is an informational notification about voting procedures = B.
2 NOT/EXCEPT: Use elimination
Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned in the passage. The remaining one is the answer.
Common traps:
- A word from the passage appears in the option, but the meaning is different
- Partially correct but inaccurate as a whole
- States the opposite of what the passage says
3 Inference questions: stick to the text
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.

Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the main purpose of this email?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A To announce the results of the spring election
B To provide students with information about how and when to vote ✓
C To recruit candidates for the student government positions
D To explain why the election date has been changed
Explanation: The email tells students when the election is (March 24-25), how to vote (online through the student portal), and what they need to log in. This is clearly an informational message about voting procedures = B.

A is wrong because results have not been announced yet (they will be on March 28). C is about recruiting candidates, but the email is addressed to voters. D mentions a date change that is never discussed in the email.
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, what do students need to log in to the voting system?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A A physical student ID card and a password
B A campus email address and a phone number
C A student ID number and a campus email address ✓
D A registration form and an invitation code
Explanation: The email states: "You will need your student ID number and campus email address to log in" = C.

A says "physical student ID card" but the passage says "student ID number" — this is a classic detail trap. B includes a phone number that is never mentioned. D has no basis in the text.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — NOT/EXCEPT
According to the notice, which of the following is NOT listed as a required document for the renewal appointment?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A A valid passport
B A letter of recommendation from a professor ✓
C Proof of enrollment for the upcoming semester
D Evidence of financial support
Elimination method:
A — "A valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity" ✓ mentioned
C — "Proof of enrollment for the upcoming semester" ✓ mentioned
D — "Evidence of financial support, such as a bank statement or scholarship letter" ✓ mentioned
B — A letter of recommendation from a professor is never mentioned in the notice. This is the correct answer.
Question 4 — Inference
What can be inferred about students who do not renew their I-20 before it expires?
Correct: D Your answer: —
A They will automatically receive an extension from the university.
B They may need to pay a small late fee to the registrar's office.
C They will be transferred to a different visa category.
D They could risk losing their ability to study at the university. ✓
Explanation: The notice states: "Students who fail to renew on time may face serious consequences, including the loss of their student status." Loss of student status means they could lose their ability to study = D.

A directly contradicts the urgency of the notice — there is no mention of automatic extensions. B mentions a late fee that is never discussed. C has no basis in the text — no mention of visa category transfers.
