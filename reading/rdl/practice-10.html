TEMPORARY CLOSURE: Campus Recreation Center

Department of Athletics — Bellmont University

Dear Students and Staff,

Please be advised that the Campus Recreation Center will be closed to all users from Monday, February 9 through Sunday, February 15. During this week, contractors will replace the wooden flooring in the main gymnasium and repair the roof above the swimming pool, which has developed a minor leak during recent storms.

While the building is closed, the following alternatives are available:

  • The outdoor track and tennis courts will remain open during regular hours.
  • Students may use the fitness room at Hartley Residence Hall free of charge by showing a student ID at the front desk.
  • All group fitness classes scheduled for this week have been moved online; a Zoom link will be sent to registered participants.

Locker rentals will be extended by one week at no extra cost, and any personal items left inside the locker rooms should be removed before Friday, February 6. The Recreation Center will reopen at 6:00 AM on Monday, February 16.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

Department of Athletics · athletics@bellmont.edu
