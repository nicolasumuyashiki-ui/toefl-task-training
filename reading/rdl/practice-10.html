Subject: Study Abroad Program — Application Deadline Approaching
From: Office of International Programs <abroad@westfield.edu> To: All Undergraduate Students Date: November 12, 2025

Dear Students,

The Office of International Programs is pleased to remind you that the application deadline for the Spring 2026 Study Abroad Program is Friday, December 6. We currently offer exchange partnerships with universities in twelve countries, including the United Kingdom, Japan, Australia, and Brazil.

To apply, you must have completed at least 30 credit hours and have a cumulative GPA of 2.75 or higher. Applications must be submitted through the student portal and must include a personal statement, two letters of recommendation, and an unofficial transcript. Students who are selected will be notified by email no later than January 10.

An information session will be held on Thursday, November 20, at 4:00 PM in the Global Learning Center, Room 105. Advisors from the International Programs office will be available to answer questions and help you choose a program that fits your academic goals.

We encourage all eligible students to apply. Studying abroad is a valuable opportunity to develop language skills, gain international experience, and broaden your perspective.

Best regards,
Office of International Programs
Westfield University

NOTICE: New Campus Recycling Program — Effective January 6

Facilities Management Office — Westfield University

Dear Campus Community,

Beginning January 6, 2026, Westfield University will implement an updated recycling program across all campus buildings. The new system replaces the current single-bin collection with a three-bin sorting system designed to increase recycling rates and reduce waste sent to landfills.

Under the new program, each common area and hallway will be equipped with three clearly labeled bins:

  • Blue bin: Paper and cardboard only
  • Green bin: Plastic bottles, glass, and aluminum cans
  • Black bin: Non-recyclable waste

Please note that food waste and liquids should not be placed in any recycling bin. Items contaminated with food residue must go in the black bin. The Facilities Management Office will conduct training sessions during the first week of January to help staff and students learn the new sorting guidelines.

Students and staff who notice incorrectly sorted bins may report the issue through the campus facilities app or by emailing facilities@westfield.edu. The university's goal is to increase its recycling rate from 35 percent to 60 percent by the end of 2026.

Thank you for your cooperation in making our campus more sustainable.

Facilities Management Office
Westfield University
