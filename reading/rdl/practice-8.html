Volunteers Plant 500 Trees Along Maplewood River in Weekend Event

By Clara Jennings · April 14, 2026

More than 300 residents gathered along the Maplewood River last Saturday to take part in the town’s largest community tree-planting event to date. Organized by the nonprofit group Green Roots Maplewood, the event successfully placed 500 native saplings along a two-kilometer stretch of riverbank.

The planting is part of a wider plan to reduce soil erosion and restore habitats for local birds and insects. Organizer Daniel Ortega explained that the chosen species—red maple, river birch, and swamp oak—were selected because they thrive in wet soil and attract pollinators.

Funding for the saplings came from a grant awarded by the state environment office in January, though tools and refreshments were donated by local businesses. Green Roots plans to hold a second planting session in October, with a goal of reaching 1,500 trees by next spring.
