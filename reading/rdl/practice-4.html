The Hartwell Gazette
New Riverside Innovation Hub Opens Doors to Students and Local Startups
By Daniel Park, Staff Reporter April 18, 2026

Hartwell University officially opened the Riverside Innovation Hub on Monday, a 4,200-square-meter facility designed to bring together engineering students, local entrepreneurs, and visiting researchers under one roof. The project, which was funded primarily by a grant from the Hartwell Foundation, took nearly three years to complete.

Unlike traditional campus buildings, the Hub is open to members of the public who apply for a community workspace pass. University officials say this decision was made to strengthen ties between the campus and nearby businesses, which have sometimes complained that university resources remain out of reach for small firms.

The facility houses a prototyping lab with 3D printers and laser cutters, four soundproof meeting rooms, and a small cafe operated by a student cooperative. However, access to the prototyping lab will initially be limited to certified users who have completed a two-hour safety course. A full schedule of courses will be released next month.

Dean of Engineering Helena Ortiz said the Hub's most unusual feature is its mentorship program, which pairs student teams with retired professionals from the area rather than with current faculty. "Our graduates tell us they want feedback from people who have actually built businesses," Ortiz said. "This is not something a lecture can provide."
