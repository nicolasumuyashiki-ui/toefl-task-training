Subject: New Meal Plan Options — Spring Semester
From: Greenfield University Dining Services <dining@greenfield.edu> To: All Students — Spring 2026 Date: January 8, 2026

Dear Students,

Dining Services is pleased to announce two new meal plan options for the spring semester. In addition to the existing Standard Plan (14 meals per week), students may now choose the Flex Plan (10 meals per week plus $150 in dining dollars) or the Light Plan (7 meals per week plus $250 in dining dollars). Dining dollars can be used at any campus cafe or food truck.

To switch your meal plan, please log in to the student portal and submit a change request by January 20. Changes made after this date will not take effect until the following month. Students who do not submit a request will remain on their current plan.

If you have questions about which plan is right for you, please visit the Dining Services office in Baker Hall, Room 105, or email us at dining@greenfield.edu.

Best regards,
Greenfield University Dining Services

NOTICE: Campus Parking Permit Changes — 2026-2027

Office of Transportation Services

Dear Students and Staff,

The Office of Transportation Services would like to inform all campus community members that significant changes to the parking permit system will take effect beginning August 1, 2026. These changes are intended to reduce traffic congestion on campus and encourage the use of public transportation and carpooling.

The key changes are as follows:

  • Freshman students living in campus housing will no longer be eligible for individual parking permits. A limited number of shared-vehicle permits will be available through the Housing Office.
  • All other students must apply for permits online through the new Transportation Portal by July 15. Paper applications will no longer be accepted.
  • Permit fees will increase by 10 percent for the 2026-2027 academic year. A monthly payment option is now available for students who prefer not to pay the full amount at once.
  • A new carpool discount of 20 percent will be offered to groups of three or more students who register a shared vehicle.

Students who currently hold a valid permit for the 2025-2026 year do not need to take any action until the renewal period opens on June 1. Detailed instructions will be sent by email at that time.

For questions, please contact Transportation Services at transport@campus.edu or visit Room 210 in the Administration Building during business hours (Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM).

Office of Transportation Services
