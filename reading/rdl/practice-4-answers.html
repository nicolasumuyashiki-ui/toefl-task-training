1 Text messages: track who says what
In SMS threads, causes, conditions, and promises are spread across speakers. Before answering, identify which bubble contains the specific fact — do not confuse your own message with the other person's.
Q1: Maya writes "Final inspection is Saturday at 10am." The cleaning date is set by the inspection, not by the shampooer or work schedules = B.
2 EXCEPT questions: eliminate what IS stated
For EXCEPT or NOT questions, find each option in the passage and cross it off if it appears. The one you cannot find is the answer.
Q4: 3D printers (paragraph 3), soundproof rooms (paragraph 3), and the student cafe (paragraph 3) are all listed. Sleeping pods are never mentioned = D.
3 Quoted speech signals inference clues
When a news article quotes someone directly, the quote usually carries the implied argument. Paraphrase the speaker's point in your own words before scanning options.
Common trap: Options that exaggerate ("will replace all," "charge less") go beyond what the quote supports. Choose the answer that restates the speaker's idea without adding new claims.

Correct: B Your answer: —
A The carpet shampooer is only available that day
B The landlord scheduled the final inspection for that morning ✓
C Both roommates are off work for the entire day
D The hardware store offers a weekend discount on spackle
Explanation: Maya's first message says, "Final inspection is Saturday at 10am." All the cleaning plans follow from that inspection deadline = B.

A twists the shampooer rental (24-hour rental, not Saturday-only). C contradicts the sent message "I have a shift till 2pm." D is never mentioned in the thread.
Question 2 — Inference
What can be inferred from Maya's warning about the deposit?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A The landlord has threatened them with extra charges before
B Any overlooked task could cost them their refundable money ✓
C Maya is planning to move out before the final inspection
D The apartment is in unusually poor condition overall
Explanation: "We lose the deposit if anything is missed" implies the deposit (refundable money) is at risk for any overlooked task = B.

A adds a past threat that the thread does not mention. C contradicts the plans for the deep clean marathon. D over-reads "anything is missed" — Maya is describing a rule, not judging the apartment's overall condition.

Question 4 — Negative Factual Information
According to the article, all of the following are features of the Innovation Hub EXCEPT
Correct: D Your answer: —
A a prototyping lab equipped with 3D printers
B several soundproof meeting rooms for team use
C a cafe managed by a student-run cooperative
D overnight sleeping pods for visiting researchers ✓
Explanation: The facility list mentions 3D printers (A), four soundproof meeting rooms (B), and a cafe run by a student cooperative (C). Sleeping pods are never described = D.

Remember: visiting researchers are mentioned only as users of the Hub, not as residents. The article gives no information about overnight accommodation.
Question 5 — Inference
What does Dean Ortiz suggest about the mentorship program?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A Students prefer guidance from those with real-world experience ✓
B The program will eventually replace all traditional classes
C Current faculty members lacked interest in advising teams
D Retired professionals charge less than university lecturers
Explanation: Ortiz quotes graduates as wanting "feedback from people who have actually built businesses" — in other words, mentors with real-world experience = A.
