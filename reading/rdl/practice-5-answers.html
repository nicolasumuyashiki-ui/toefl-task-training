Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We would like to inform you... / We are writing to let you know...
Reminder: We would like to remind... / Please be aware that...
Policy update: The following policy applies to...
Q1: The email opens with "would like to inform you that the indoor swimming pool will be temporarily closed" and then provides maintenance details and an alternative. This is a notification about a closure = A.
2 Combine multiple details for inference
Inference questions often require you to combine two or more pieces of information from different parts of the passage.
Q4: October 20 falls after October 15 (so a "W" appears on the transcript) AND between October 15 and October 31 (so a 50% refund applies). Combining these two facts gives C.
3 Watch for date-based traps
Notices with multiple deadlines are common in TOEFL. Draw a quick timeline to keep track of which rules apply when.
Common mistake: Confusing which deadline applies. In Q4, a student might pick A (full refund, no notation) because they confuse the October 15 and October 31 deadlines.
Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the main purpose of this email?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A To notify the campus community about a temporary pool closure and alternative arrangements ✓
B To encourage students to join the university swim team
C To announce a permanent change to pool operating hours
D To request feedback on the recreation center's facilities
Explanation: The email informs students that the pool will be closed from October 10–17, explains what maintenance will occur, and offers the outdoor pool as an alternative. This is clearly a notification about a temporary closure with alternatives = A.

B is unrelated to the email content. C is wrong because the change is temporary, not permanent. D is not mentioned anywhere in the email.
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, what option is available for lap swimmers during the closure?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A They can use a heated pool at a nearby community center.
B They can use the outdoor pool at the West Athletic Complex with extended hours. ✓
C They will receive a temporary membership at an off-campus gym.
D They can schedule private sessions in the pool before 6:00 AM.
Explanation: The email states that lap swimmers "may use the outdoor pool at the West Athletic Complex, which will extend its hours" = B.

A is a trap — the email specifically says the outdoor pool is unheated, not heated. C is not mentioned. D inverts the information — 6:00 AM is the opening time of the alternative pool, not a time for private sessions.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — Factual Detail
According to the notice, which of the following is true about withdrawing from a course between October 15 and November 14?
Correct: D Your answer: —
A The course will be completely removed from the student's transcript.
B The student will receive a failing grade for the course.
C The student must obtain approval from the Dean of Students.
D A "W" notation will appear on the transcript but will not affect the GPA. ✓
Explanation: The notice states that after October 15 and until November 14, "a 'W' (Withdrawn) notation will appear on their academic record" and "a 'W' does not affect your GPA" = D.

A applies to withdrawals before October 15 (no record on transcript). B is not mentioned — a "W" is not a failing grade. C applies only after November 14, not during the October 15–November 14 window.
Question 4 — Inference
What can be inferred about a student who withdraws from a course on October 20?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A The student will receive a full tuition refund and no transcript notation.
B The student will need to submit a formal petition to the Dean.
C The student will have a "W" on the transcript and receive a 50% tuition refund. ✓
D The student will not receive any refund but will have no transcript notation.
Inference from two details:
1. October 20 is after October 15 → a "W" appears on the transcript.
2. October 20 is between October 15 and October 31 → a 50% tuition refund applies.
Combining these two facts = C.

A is wrong because the full refund and no-notation window ends on October 15. B applies only after November 14. D incorrectly states no refund (50% still applies) and no notation (a "W" does appear).
