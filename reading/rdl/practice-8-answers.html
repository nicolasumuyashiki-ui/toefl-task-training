Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Announcement: We are pleased to announce... / We are hosting...
Notification: We would like to inform you...
Invitation: You are invited to... / Registration is now open...
Instructions: To participate, you must... / Please follow these steps...
Q1: The email opens with "The campus bookstore will be hosting its end-of-semester textbook buyback program." The word "hosting" and the details about dates and procedures indicate this is an announcement about a program = A.
2 Detail questions: match each option to the passage
Steps:
1. Read the question carefully and identify what specific detail is being asked about
2. Locate the relevant sentence in the passage
3. Match the passage wording to the correct option (look for paraphrases)
Q2: The passage states "Payment will be issued as campus bookstore credit." This directly matches option C: "As bookstore credit for future purchases."
3 Inference questions: stick to the text
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.
Q4: The notice says registration is "open to all currently enrolled students, regardless of major or year of study." This directly supports D: any currently enrolled student can attend.
Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the main purpose of this email?
Correct: A Your answer: —
A To announce a program where students can sell back their used textbooks ✓
B To inform students about new textbook prices for next semester
C To warn students that the bookstore will be closing temporarily
D To encourage students to donate their textbooks to the library
Explanation: The email announces the "end-of-semester textbook buyback program," explains the dates (May 5–16), location (second floor of the Student Center), and conditions for selling back books. This is clearly an announcement about a buyback program = A.

B is wrong because the email is about selling back used books, not new textbook prices. C is wrong because the bookstore is not closing; it is hosting a special program. D is wrong because students are selling books for credit, not donating them.
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, how will students receive payment for their textbooks?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A By direct deposit into their bank accounts
B As a cash refund at the checkout counter
C As bookstore credit for future purchases ✓
D Through a check mailed to their home address
Explanation: The email clearly states: "Payment will be issued as campus bookstore credit, which can be applied toward future textbook purchases" = C.

A (direct deposit), B (cash refund), and D (check by mail) are all payment methods that are not mentioned anywhere in the email. This is a straightforward detail question where the answer directly matches the passage.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — Factual Detail
According to the notice, which of the following is a recommended preparation step for the career fair?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A Submitting a cover letter to each employer before the event
B Researching the companies that will be attending the fair ✓
C Paying a registration fee at the Career Services office
D Scheduling an interview with employers before the fair begins
Explanation: The notice lists three recommended preparation steps. One of them is: "Research the list of attending companies, which will be posted on the portal by May 1." This matches B.

A is wrong — submitting cover letters is not mentioned. C is wrong — the notice explicitly states "Registration is free," so there is no fee. D is wrong — scheduling interviews is not mentioned as a preparation step.
Question 4 — Inference
What can be inferred about who is eligible to attend the career fair?
Correct: D Your answer: —
A Only students in their final year of study may attend.
B Students must have previous work experience to register.
C Only business and engineering majors are invited to the fair.
D Any currently enrolled student can attend, no matter what they are studying. ✓
Explanation: The notice states: "Registration is free and open to all currently enrolled students, regardless of major or year of study." From this we can infer that any enrolled student is eligible, regardless of what they study = D.

A directly contradicts the passage — the notice says "regardless of... year of study." B has no basis in the text — work experience is not mentioned. C directly contradicts "regardless of major."
