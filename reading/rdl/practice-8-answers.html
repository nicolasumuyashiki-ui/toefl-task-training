1 ニュース記事は事実と背景を分けるNews articles: separate facts from background
ニュース記事は中心となる出来事（「誰が、何を、いつ、どこで」）から始まる。後続の段落では理由、引用、今後の予定が追加される。事実問題では、記事全体を読み直すのではなく、そのサブトピックを扱う特定の段落を特定すること。 News articles open with the core event ("who did what, when, where"). Later paragraphs add reasons, quotes, and future plans. For factual questions, locate the specific paragraph that deals with that sub-topic rather than re-reading the whole article.
Q2: 資金についての設問は第3段落でのみ答えられる：「Funding for the saplings came from a grant... tools and refreshments were donated by local businesses.」 = C。The funding question is answered only in paragraph 3: "Funding for the saplings came from a grant... tools and refreshments were donated by local businesses." = C.
2 推論問題は未来形の文を確認するInference: check the future-tense sentences
記事の最後が「plans to」「will hold」「goal of」のような文で締められている場合、その一文が推論問題の根拠になることが多い。書き手は次に何が起こるかを伝えており、それを使って組織やイベントについての結論を導くことができる。 When an article ends with a sentence about "plans to," "will hold," or "goal of," that line is often the basis for inference questions. The writer is telling you what comes next — use that to draw conclusions about the organization or the event.
Q3: 「Green Roots plans to hold a second planting session in October, with a goal of reaching 1,500 trees.」これは「グループは植樹活動を続ける意向である」を直接支持する = B。"Green Roots plans to hold a second planting session in October, with a goal of reaching 1,500 trees." This directly supports "The group intends to continue its planting work" = B.
3 SNS投稿はカジュアルな口調を慎重に読むSocial media posts: read the informal tone carefully
SNSの投稿は短くカジュアルだが、すべての表現が情報を含んでいる。「Honestly, my Portuguese is still rough, but the staff keep switching to English to help me out」は困難と肯定的な反応の両方を示している。推論問題では「書き手の言葉遣いはどんな態度や状況を明らかにしているか？」と問うこと。 Social posts are short and casual, but every phrase carries information. "Honestly, my Portuguese is still rough, but the staff keep switching to English to help me out" shows both a difficulty and a positive response. For inference, ask: "What attitude or situation does the writer's word choice reveal?"
Q5: 同僚が "switching to English to help me out"（私を助けるために英語に切り替えてくれる）ということから、言語ギャップに対して柔軟で協力的だと分かる = A。The coworkers "switching to English to help me out" signals they are being flexible and supportive about the language gap = A.
