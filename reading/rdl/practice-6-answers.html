Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Track who says what in a group chat
In a group conversation, each message is tied to a specific sender. Questions often ask what a particular person will do. Read the sender's name first, then locate their statement — don't mix up tasks between members.
Q2: Daniel says "I'll draft the survey and send it out to classmates tomorrow." That is his task, not Raj's (literature review) or Sofia's (video) = A.
2 Inference: stay close to what is actually said
An inference must be logically supported by the text. Sofia says "I'm swamped with my chem lab this week." This directly supports the idea that she is busy with another subject. Don't jump to bigger conclusions (like "she doesn't want to help") that go beyond what the text warrants.
Common traps:
- Options that exaggerate (e.g., "doesn't want to help" vs. "is busy this week")
- Options that introduce new facts not in the passage
3 Advertisements: watch conditions on discounts
Ad passages usually include multiple offers with different conditions (early bird, student discount, membership, etc.). Questions often test whether you can match the offer to its exact condition. Underline the deadline or requirement for each discount on your first read.
Q4: The Early Bird Special requires registering "before April 25." The 10% discount for having a student ID is a different offer. = C.
4 NOT/EXCEPT: eliminate what is mentioned
Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned. The remaining one is the answer.
Note: The answer often introduces a plausible-sounding detail (like "online version") that was never actually stated in the passage.

Question 4 — Factual Detail
According to the advertisement, what does a student need to do to receive the Early Bird discount?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A Attend a free conversation cafe on a Friday
B Show a valid university student ID card
C Sign up for the course before April 25 ✓
D Complete a short placement test in advance
Explanation: The ad states: "Register before April 25 and save 20% off the regular tuition." This is the Early Bird condition = C.

A is a separate benefit for current students, unrelated to the discount. B is the condition for the additional 10% student discount, not the Early Bird. D is never mentioned.
Question 5 — NOT/EXCEPT

Correct: D Your answer: —
A a class size limited to eight students
B instructors who are native Spanish speakers
C a weekly optional conversation gathering
D an online version for remote learners ✓
Elimination method:
A — "no more than eight students per instructor" ✓ mentioned
B — "all teachers are native speakers" ✓ mentioned
C — "free weekly conversation cafe on Fridays" ✓ mentioned
D — An online or remote option is never mentioned. The ad refers only to the downtown campus. This is the correct answer.
