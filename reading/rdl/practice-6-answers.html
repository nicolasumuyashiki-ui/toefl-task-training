Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Distinguish temporary vs. permanent changes
Pay close attention to time-limiting phrases like for that day only, from... through..., or during this period. These signal that a change is temporary, not permanent. Answer choices that say "permanent" or omit the time limit are traps.
Q1: The email says office hours are canceled "for that day only" and offers "make-up office hours" on Thursday. This is clearly a temporary change = C, not a permanent schedule change (A).
2 NOT/EXCEPT: Check each option against the passage
Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned. The remaining one is the answer.
Common traps:
- A word from the passage appears in the option, but the meaning is different
- Partially correct but inaccurate as a whole
- States the opposite of what the passage says
3 Inference: look for what is logically supported
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. If the passage says classes are "moved to" a new location with an "updated schedule," you can infer they will continue at a different place and possibly different times.
Q4: The notice says aqua fitness classes "will be moved to the multipurpose studio" and students should "check the updated schedule." This supports B: they continue but in a different location and possibly at different times.
Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the main purpose of this email?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A To announce a permanent change to the professor's office hours schedule
B To ask students to attend a faculty meeting on Wednesday
C To inform students about a temporary office hours change and alternative contact options ✓
D To require all students to schedule video calls for the upcoming week
Explanation: The email explains that Wednesday office hours are canceled "for that day only," provides a make-up time on Thursday, and offers email and video call as alternatives. This is a temporary change notification with alternative options = C.

A is wrong because the change is explicitly temporary ("for that day only"), not permanent. B confuses the reason for the cancellation with the purpose of the email. D overstates the email; video calls are offered as an option, not required.
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, what should a student do if they cannot attend the Thursday make-up office hours?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A Wait until the following Wednesday to visit office hours
B Email questions or book a video call through the course website ✓
C Go directly to the professor's office without an appointment
D Ask a teaching assistant for help instead
Explanation: The email states: "please feel free to email me your questions or schedule a brief video call through the appointment link on the course website" = B.

A is not mentioned as advice. C contradicts the email's tone of scheduling alternatives. D is not mentioned anywhere in the email.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — NOT/EXCEPT
According to the notice, all of the following are part of the pool maintenance work EXCEPT
Correct: D Your answer: —
A replacing the filtration system
B resurfacing the pool deck
C repairing damaged tiles
D installing new diving boards ✓
Elimination method:
A — "replace the filtration system" ✓ mentioned
B — "resurface the pool deck" ✓ mentioned
C — "repair damaged tiles along the edges" ✓ mentioned
D — Diving boards are never mentioned in the notice. This is the correct answer.

This is a straightforward EXCEPT question. All three distractors use exact or near-exact wording from the passage.
Question 4 — Inference
What can be inferred about the aqua fitness classes during the maintenance period?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A They will be canceled entirely until the pool reopens.
B They will continue but in a different location and possibly at different times. ✓
C They will be held at Riverside Community Center along with swim team practices.
D They will only be available to swim team members during the closure.
Explanation: The notice states that aqua fitness classes "will be moved to the multipurpose studio on the second floor" and asks students to "check the updated schedule." From this we can infer the classes continue in a new location and possibly at different times = B.

A directly contradicts the passage (they are moved, not canceled). C confuses aqua fitness with swim team practice; only the swim team goes to Riverside. D has no basis in the text.
