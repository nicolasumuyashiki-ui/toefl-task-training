1 冒頭のキーワードから目的を特定するIdentify the purpose from opening keywords
最初の1〜2文に文章の目的が示されている。次のパターンを覚えておこう。

通知: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
依頼: Could you... / I would appreciate if...
確認: I'm writing to confirm...
謝罪: I apologize for... / Unfortunately... The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
Request: Could you... / I would appreciate if...
Confirmation: I'm writing to confirm...
Apology: I apologize for... / Unfortunately...
Q1: メールは「Your pre-ordered textbooks are now available for pickup」で始まり、受け取りのルールと返品規定について述べている。これは情報通知 = B。The email opens with "Your pre-ordered textbooks are now available for pickup" and then describes pickup rules and exchange policies. This is an informational notification = B.
2 NOT/EXCEPT問題は消去法でNOT/EXCEPT: Use elimination
手順:
1. 設問内の NOT/EXCEPT に印をつける
2. 各選択肢をパッセージと一つずつ照らし合わせる
3. パッセージに書かれている3つの選択肢を消す。残った1つが正解。 Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned in the passage. The remaining one is the answer.
よくある罠:
- パッセージの単語が選択肢に登場するが、意味が違う
- 部分的には正しいが、全体として不正確
- パッセージの内容と反対のことを言っている Common traps:
- A word from the passage appears in the option, but the meaning is different
- Partially correct but inaccurate as a whole
- States the opposite of what the passage says
3 推論問題は本文から離れないInference questions: stick to the text
推論問題では、答えはパッセージの情報から論理的に導かれるものでなければならない。自分の知識や思い込みを混ぜないこと。 For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.

メールによれば、学生が8月25日より前に事前予約をキャンセルした場合、何が起きるか。According to the email, what will happen if a student cancels a pre-order before August 25?

A は典型的な罠。メールは「Refunds are not available for pre-orders」と明言しており、現金返金は除外される。「refund」という単語はパッセージに出てくるが、答えは返金では「ない」。C は交換規定（別版用）とキャンセル規定を混同している。D はどこにも記載がない。 The passage states: "store credit will be issued for any cancellations made before August 25" = B.

A is a classic trap — the email explicitly says "Refunds are not available for pre-orders," so a cash refund is ruled out. The word "refund" appears in the passage but the answer is NOT a refund. C confuses the exchange policy (for different editions) with the cancellation policy. D is not mentioned anywhere.

A 1週間まるごと完全に閉鎖される。It will be completely closed for the entire week.

A はパッセージと直接矛盾（「will remain open」）。C は本文に根拠なし（階による制限の記載なし）。D は記載されているメンテナンス作業に含まれていない。 The notice states: "The basement laundry room will remain open, but access through the south entrance will be restricted. Please use the north entrance instead." From this we can infer that residents can still use it, but need to enter through a different door = B.
