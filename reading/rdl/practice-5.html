Subject: Pool Maintenance Schedule — Temporary Closures
From: Greenfield University Campus Recreation Center <recreation@greenfield.edu> To: All Students, Faculty & Staff Date: October 2, 2025

Dear Campus Community,

The Campus Recreation Center would like to inform you that the indoor swimming pool will be temporarily closed for scheduled maintenance from October 10 through October 17. During this period, the pool filtration system will be inspected, tiles along the pool deck will be repaired, and the water heating unit will be serviced.

Lap swimmers who wish to continue training during the closure may use the outdoor pool at the West Athletic Complex, which will extend its hours to 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM for the duration of the maintenance period. Please note that the outdoor pool is unheated, so appropriate gear is recommended.

Regular pool hours will resume on October 18. We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.

Best regards,
Campus Recreation Center
Greenfield University

IMPORTANT: Course Withdrawal Policy and Deadlines

Office of Academic Affairs — Greenfield University

Dear Students,

The Office of Academic Affairs would like to remind all currently enrolled students of the university's course withdrawal policy for the Fall 2025 semester. Understanding these deadlines is essential, as missing them may have significant academic and financial consequences.

Students who wish to drop a course without any record on their transcript must do so by October 15. After this date and until November 14, students may still withdraw from a course, but a "W" (Withdrawn) notation will appear on their academic record. Please note that a "W" does not affect your GPA, but it is visible to graduate schools and future employers who review your transcript.

After November 14, withdrawal is not permitted except in cases of documented medical emergency or other extraordinary circumstances, which must be approved by the Dean of Students. In such cases, students should submit a formal petition along with supporting documentation to the Academic Affairs office in Room 210 of the Administration Building.

Important financial information:

  • Full tuition refund is available only for courses dropped before October 15.
  • A 50% tuition refund applies to withdrawals made between October 15 and October 31.
  • No refund is available for withdrawals after October 31.

If you have questions about how a withdrawal might affect your academic plan, we strongly encourage you to meet with your academic advisor before making a decision. The Academic Advising Center is open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

Office of Academic Affairs
Greenfield University
