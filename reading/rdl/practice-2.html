Subject: Campus Flu Vaccination Clinic — Schedule & Requirements
From: Greenfield University Health Center <health@greenfield.edu> To: All Students — Spring 2026 Date: March 10, 2026

Dear Students,

The Campus Health Center will be hosting free flu vaccination clinics during the week of March 23–27 in Room 105 of the Wellness Building. Walk-ins are welcome, but students who schedule an appointment through the student health portal will receive priority service and shorter wait times.

Please bring your student ID and completed immunization consent form, which can be downloaded from the health portal. Students with egg allergies or those currently taking blood-thinning medication should consult a physician before receiving the vaccine. For questions, contact the Health Center at (555) 312-7400.

Stay healthy,
Greenfield University Health Center

IMPORTANT: Scholarship Application Deadline Approaching

Office of Financial Aid — Greenfield University

Dear Students,

The Office of Financial Aid reminds all eligible students that the deadline to submit applications for the 2026–2027 Academic Merit Scholarship is April 15, 2026. This scholarship is awarded to full-time undergraduate students who have maintained a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or above and have completed at least 30 credit hours at Greenfield University.

To complete your application, you must submit the following documents through the Financial Aid Portal:

  • A completed scholarship application form
  • An unofficial academic transcript
  • A personal statement of no more than 500 words describing your academic goals
  • One letter of recommendation from a current faculty member

Please note that incomplete applications will not be reviewed. Late submissions will only be accepted if accompanied by a documented reason approved by the Dean of Students. Award decisions will be announced by May 20, 2026.

Students who received the scholarship in a previous year must reapply; renewal is not automatic. If you have questions about eligibility or the application process, visit the Financial Aid Office in Hamilton Hall, Room 210, or email finaid@greenfield.edu.

Office of Financial Aid
Greenfield University
