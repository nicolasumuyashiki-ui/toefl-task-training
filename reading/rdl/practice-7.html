IMPORTANT: Visa Renewal Requirements

International Student Office — Lakewood University

Dear International Students,

The International Student Office would like to remind all students on F-1 visas that maintaining valid immigration status requires timely action. If your current I-20 document is set to expire within the next 60 days, you must schedule an appointment with an international student advisor to begin the renewal process.

To complete the renewal, you will need to bring the following documents to your appointment:

  • A valid passport with at least six months of remaining validity
  • Your current I-20 form
  • Proof of enrollment for the upcoming semester (available through the registrar's office)
  • Evidence of financial support, such as a bank statement or scholarship letter

Please note that processing times may vary, and we strongly recommend submitting your renewal application at least 30 days before your I-20 expires. Students who fail to renew on time may face serious consequences, including the loss of their student status.

For questions or to book an appointment, please contact us at iso@lakewood.edu or visit our office in Wilson Hall, Room 215.
