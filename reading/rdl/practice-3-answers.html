Read in Daily Life Strategy Tips
1 Identify the purpose from opening keywords
The first 1-2 sentences reveal the purpose of the text. Learn the patterns:

Notification: We are pleased to inform... / We would like to inform you...
Request: Could you... / I would appreciate if...
Instruction: All accounts must... / You are required to...
Announcement: We are now accepting... / Applications are open...
Q1: The email states that "all university accounts must undergo a mandatory password reset by October 15." The word "mandatory" and the deadline indicate this is an instruction to take action = C.
2 NOT/EXCEPT: Use elimination
Steps:
1. Circle NOT/EXCEPT in the question
2. Check each option against the passage one by one
3. Cross out the 3 options that ARE mentioned in the passage. The remaining one is the answer.
Common traps:
- A word from the passage appears in the option, but the meaning is different
- Partially correct but inaccurate as a whole
- States the opposite of what the passage says
3 Inference questions: stick to the text
For inference questions, the answer must be logically supported by information in the passage. Do not mix in your own knowledge or assumptions.
Q4: The passage says clubs that received funding previously "must submit a spending report before applying again." This directly supports that they must account for how they spent their previous funds = D.
Passage 1 Email
Question 1 — Main Purpose
What is the primary purpose of this email?
Correct: C Your answer: —
A To notify users that their accounts have been compromised
B To announce the launch of a new university portal
C To instruct users to reset their passwords before a deadline ✓
D To explain how to create a new university account
Explanation: The email clearly states that "all university accounts must undergo a mandatory password reset by October 15" and provides step-by-step instructions for doing so. The main purpose is to instruct users to reset their passwords = C.

A is wrong because the email mentions a routine "annual security update," not a security breach. B is wrong because the portal already exists; the email simply tells users to log in to it. D is wrong because the email is about resetting an existing password, not creating a new account.
Question 2 — Factual Detail
According to the email, what will happen to accounts that are not updated by October 15?
Correct: B Your answer: —
A They will be permanently deleted from the system.
B They will be temporarily locked until the password is reset. ✓
C They will be automatically assigned a new password.
D They will lose access to email but retain portal access.
Explanation: The email explicitly states: "Accounts that have not been updated by the deadline will be temporarily locked until the password is reset" = B.

A uses extreme language ("permanently deleted") that contradicts "temporarily locked." C is not mentioned anywhere in the email. D invents a partial restriction that has no basis in the text.
Passage 2 Notice
Question 3 — NOT/EXCEPT
According to the notice, funding may be used for all of the following EXCEPT
Correct: A Your answer: —
A purchasing food for club members ✓
B inviting guest speakers to campus
C buying supplies for club activities
D covering travel expenses for club events
Elimination method:
B — "guest speakers" ✓ mentioned as an approved use
C — "supplies" ✓ mentioned as an approved use
D — "travel related to club purposes" ✓ mentioned as an approved use
A — The notice explicitly states "Funding cannot be used for food." This is the correct answer because food is excluded from approved uses.

This is a straightforward EXCEPT question. The key is to check the restrictions list carefully.
Question 4 — Inference
What can be inferred about clubs that received funding in the previous semester?
Correct: D Your answer: —
A They are automatically approved for funding in the next semester.
B They are no longer eligible to apply for additional funding.
C They will receive a larger amount of funding than new applicants.
D They must account for how they spent their previous funds before reapplying. ✓
Explanation: The notice states: "Clubs that received funding in the previous semester must submit a spending report before applying again." A spending report is a way to account for how money was spent. Therefore, we can infer that these clubs must demonstrate how they used their previous funds = D.

A directly contradicts the passage — they must still apply and submit a spending report. B is wrong because they CAN apply again, they just need to submit a report first. C has no support in the text — no preferential amounts are mentioned.
