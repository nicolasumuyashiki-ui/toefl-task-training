Writing

Tip
Writing攻略の鉄則：Build a Sentenceは「主語→動詞→骨格→残り配置」。Emailは3ポイントを絶対に見落とさない。Discussionは「主張→理由→具体例→言及」のテンプレートで。 Writing strategy: For Build a Sentence, follow subject → verb → frame → rest. For Email, cover all three prompts. For Discussion, use claim → reason → example → citation.
