💡 Build a Sentence 攻略 3ステップBuild a Sentence — 3-step strategy

1

主語と動詞を見つけるFind the subject and verb

「誰が（主語）」「どうする（動詞）」を最初に探す。この2つが文の骨格。Look first for who (the subject) and what they do (the verb). These two form the backbone of the sentence.

2

骨格を作るBuild the skeleton

S+V を組み合わせて基本形を作る。文型（SV, SVC, SVO）を意識。Combine S + V into a basic shape. Stay aware of the sentence pattern (SV, SVC, SVO).

3

残りを配置Place the rest

修飾語、前置詞句、接続詞を適切な位置に。最後に音読チェック（5秒）。Slot modifiers, prepositional phrases, and conjunctions into the right spots. Finish with a 5-second read-aloud check.

📐 今回の語順パターンWord-order patterns in this set

🔹 過去完了と時制の前後Past perfect & sequence of tenses

Q4「I had expected」Q7「the deadline had passed」— 過去のある時点より前の動作を示す had + Vpp。Q4 'I had expected', Q7 'the deadline had passed' — had + past participle marks an action prior to a past reference point.

🔹 間接疑問文Indirect questions

Q2「how people think」Q10「what I could do」のように、wh節内は平叙文語順（S+V）になる。As in Q2 'how people think' and Q10 'what I could do', the wh-clause uses statement word order (S + V).

🔹 目的・理由の接続詞Conjunctions of purpose & reason

Q6「because I was looking forward to it」Q9「so that I do not fall behind」— 理由と目的を明確に分ける。Q6 'because I was looking forward to it', Q9 'so that I do not fall behind' — clearly distinguish reason from purpose.

🔹 仮定法 would + VSubjunctive 'would + V'

Q10「I would talk to the professor」— 仮定の状況への回答に使う丁寧・仮定表現。Q10 'I would talk to the professor' — a polite, hypothetical response to an imagined situation.
