💡 Build a Sentence 攻略 3ステップ

1

主語と動詞を見つける

「誰が（主語）」「どうする（動詞）」を最初に探す。この2つが文の骨格。

2

骨格を作る

S+V を組み合わせて基本形を作る。文型（SV, SVC, SVO）を意識。

3

残りを配置

修飾語、前置詞句、接続詞を適切な位置に。最後に音読チェック（5秒）。

📐 今回の語順パターン

🔹 used to / be used to

Q1「I used to eat fast food almost every day.」, Q9「She used to skip breakfast, but now she eats a balanced meal every morning.」

🔹 Relative clause with whose

Q2「she is the doctor whose clinic is next to the park.」, Q10「a small Italian restaurant whose owner used to be a professional chef.」

🔹 too / enough + to-infinitive

Q4「I was too tired to wake up early every day.」, Q5「it is big enough for three people to share.」

🔹 Embedded wh- questions

Q6「I am not sure what time it closes on weekdays.」, Q7「Could you tell me where the nearest farmers market is?」
