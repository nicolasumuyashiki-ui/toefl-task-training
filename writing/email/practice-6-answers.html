📋 問題の確認
Scenario: You live in an apartment building. Your neighbor's dog (Ms. Garcia's dog) has been barking loudly late at night for the past week. The noise usually starts around 11 PM and continues until after midnight. You have an important work presentation early in the morning, and the lack of sleep is affecting your performance.

Write an email to Ms. Garcia. In your email:

  • Point 1 Describe the problem with the late-night barking and how long it has been happening.
  • Point 2 Explain how the noise is affecting your daily life and work.
  • Point 3 Suggest a possible solution to the situation.
✅ 模範回答（約95語）
Dear Ms. Garcia,

1 I am writing to let you know that your dog has been barking very loudly late at night for the past week. The barking usually starts around 11 PM and continues until after midnight.

2 I have an important presentation at work early each morning, and the noise has been making it very difficult for me to get enough sleep. As a result, my performance at work has been suffering.

3 Would it be possible to keep your dog inside after 10 PM, or perhaps consider using a calming product to help reduce the barking at night?

Thank you for your understanding.
Best regards
✅ 自己チェックリスト
☑️ 3つのポイントがすべて含まれている
☑️ 各ポイントに2〜3文の説明がある
☑️ Dear [Name] で始まり、Best regards で終わっている
☑️ 80〜120語の範囲に収まっている
☑️ スペルミス・文法ミスがない
☑️ 丁寧だが率直なトーンを保っている（苦情でも攻撃的にならない）
⚠️ よくあるミス
✗ ポイント見落とし

3つのうち1つを書き忘れる → 大減点。書き終わったら必ず再確認

✗ 攻撃的なトーン

"Your dog is so annoying!" → "I would appreciate your help with..." 丁寧に依頼する

✗ 具体性の不足

"The dog barks at night" だけでなく、いつから・何時頃かを具体的に述べる

✗ 解決策が曖昧

"Please do something" ではなく、具体的な提案をする（室内に入れる、等）
