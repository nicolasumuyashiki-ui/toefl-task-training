📋 問題の確認Prompt recap
Scenario: Last month you volunteered to help organize a charity event on campus next Saturday, but you have just realized that it falls on the same day as an important exam review session for your economics final. You will not be able to attend both.

Write an email to the event coordinator, Ms. Rivera. In your email:

  • Point 1 Apologize for the scheduling conflict and explain why you cannot attend the charity event.
  • Point 2 Suggest a specific classmate or friend who could take your place and explain why they would be a good replacement.
  • Point 3 Offer to help with the event in another way, either before or after the event day.
✅ 模範回答（約95語）Model response (~95 words)
Dear Ms. Rivera,

1 I am writing to sincerely apologize for a scheduling conflict. I have just realized that the charity event next Saturday falls on the same day as my economics final exam review session, which I cannot miss. Unfortunately, I will not be able to help at the event as I originally planned.

2 However, I would like to recommend my classmate, David Kim. He has experience organizing school events and is very reliable. I believe he would be a great replacement.

3 I would also be happy to help prepare materials or set up decorations the evening before the event.

Thank you for your understanding.
Best regards

✗ ポイント見落とし✗ Missing a required point

3つのうち1つを書き忘れる → 大減点。書き終わったら必ず再確認Forgetting one of the three points = a big score hit. Always re-check after writing.

✗ 冗長な前置き✗ Long-winded openings

"I hope this email finds you well..." は不要。すぐに目的を述べる"I hope this email finds you well..." is unnecessary — get to the point immediately.

✗ カジュアルすぎる表現✗ Wording too casual

"Hey Prof!" → "Dear Professor" に。メールの適切なトーンを意識"Hey Prof!" → "Dear Professor". Keep the email tone appropriate.

✗ 書きすぎ✗ Writing too much

120語を超えても加点なし。簡潔に3ポイントを網羅することが重要Going over 120 words earns no extra credit. Cover the 3 points concisely.
