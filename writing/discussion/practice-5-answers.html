📋 ディスカッション内容の確認Discussion recap
Dr. Nakamura
Should public transportation be free for all citizens? Why or why not?
Sofia（無料化賛成派）Sofia (pro-free transit)
低所得者の負担軽減、車利用の減少による渋滞・大気汚染の改善。ルクセンブルクの成功例を挙げている。Reduces the burden on low-income riders; cuts congestion and air pollution by lowering car use. She cites Luxembourg as a successful example.
James（無料化反対派）James (against free transit)
運営コストが巨額。税金で賄うと教育・医療など他のサービスに影響。運賃を手頃にする方が現実的。Operating costs are huge. Funding it through taxes would squeeze other services like education and healthcare. Keeping fares affordable is more realistic.
✅ 模範回答（約115語）Model response (~115 words)
主張Claim I believe that public transportation should be free, but only if governments can secure sustainable funding to maintain service quality.

言及Reference I agree with Sofia that removing fares would encourage more people to use public transit, which would help reduce traffic and pollution. At the same time, I understand James's concern about the financial burden on taxpayers.

理由Reason However, the long-term savings from reduced road maintenance, lower healthcare costs due to cleaner air, and increased worker productivity could offset the initial investment.

具体例Example For example, in my hometown, a free shuttle service between the university and downtown has significantly reduced car usage among students and has been funded through a small parking fee increase.

Therefore, with proper planning and creative funding, free public transit can benefit both citizens and the environment.

※ この模範回答は「条件付き賛成」の立場を取っています。完全に賛成・反対どちらの立場でもOKです。This model response takes a 'conditional yes' position. A fully pro or fully con stance is also acceptable.

✅ 自己チェックリストSelf-check list
☑️ 最初の1文で立場を明確にしている（I believe that...）The first sentence clearly states your position (I believe that...)
☑️ 他の学生の意見に言及している（I agree with... / While I understand...）References another student's view (I agree with... / While I understand...)
☑️ 明確な理由が1つ以上あるIncludes at least one clear reason
☑️ 具体例が1つある（自分の経験でOK）Includes one specific example (a personal one is fine)
☑️ 100語以上あるLength is at least 100 words
☑️ まとめの文で締めくくっている（Therefore...）Ends with a concluding sentence (Therefore...)
⚠️ よくあるミスCommon mistakes
✗ 立場が不明確✗ Position is unclear

"This is a very interesting question..." で始めて立場を述べない → I believe that... ですぐ始めるOpening with "This is a very interesting question..." and never stating a stance — open with "I believe that..." right away.

✗ 理由が2つで浅い✗ Two reasons, both shallow

2つの理由を浅く並べるより、1つの理由を深く掘り下げる方が高得点Developing one reason in depth scores higher than listing two shallow reasons.

✗ 他の学生への言及なし✗ No reference to other students

ディスカッションなので他の投稿に触れることが期待されているIt is a discussion, so referring to other posts is expected.

✗ 具体例が長すぎる✗ Example too long

具体例は2-3文で十分。長すぎると時間切れのリスク2–3 sentences is plenty for the example — going longer risks running out of time.
