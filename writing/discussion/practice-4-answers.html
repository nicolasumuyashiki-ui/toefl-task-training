ディスカッション内容の確認Discussion Recap
Dr. Chen
Should schools replace textbooks with tablets and laptops? Why or why not?
Priya（テクノロジー賛成派）Priya (pro-technology)
タブレットやノートPCはインタラクティブな教材・動画・シミュレーション・最新情報を提供できる。教師が授業をカスタマイズしやすくなる。Tablets and laptops offer interactive content, videos, simulations, and up-to-date information. They help teachers customize lessons.
Ethan（教科書賛成派）Ethan (pro-textbook)
画面は目の疲れや注意散漫を招く。教科書は安定していて充電不要、生徒の集中を助ける。Screens cause eye strain and distraction. Textbooks are reliable, need no charging, and help students focus.
模範回答（約115語）Model Answer (approximately 115 words)
主張Claim I believe that schools should gradually integrate tablets and laptops alongside textbooks rather than replacing them entirely.

言及Reference While I understand Ethan's concern about screen-related distractions, I agree with Priya that digital tools provide valuable learning opportunities that textbooks alone cannot offer.

理由Reason The key advantage of technology is that it allows students to engage with material in interactive ways. Digital platforms can include quizzes, videos, and real-time feedback, which help students understand difficult concepts more effectively than static text.

具体例Example For example, in my high school science class, we used a tablet application that let us explore 3D models of the human body, which made anatomy lessons far more engaging than reading from a textbook.

A balanced approach that combines both formats would give students the benefits of technology while preserving the focus that printed materials provide.

この模範回答は「バランス型」の立場を取っています。完全にテクノロジー賛成・教科書賛成どちらの立場でも、根拠がしっかりしていればOKです。Note: This model answer takes a "balanced" position. A fully pro-technology or fully pro-textbook stance is also acceptable, as long as it is well-supported.

よくあるミスCommon Mistakes to Avoid
✗ 立場が不明確Unclear position

"This is a very interesting topic..." で始めて立場を述べない → I believe that... ですぐ始めるStarting with "This is a very interesting topic..." without stating your stance. Begin with "I believe that..." immediately.

✗ 理由が2つで浅いTwo shallow reasons

2つの理由を浅く並べるより、1つの理由を深く掘り下げる方が高得点Listing two reasons briefly is weaker than developing one reason in depth with a concrete example.

✗ 他の学生への言及なしNo reference to classmates

ディスカッションなので他の投稿に触れることが期待されているThis is a discussion, so you are expected to engage with other students' posts. Always mention at least one.

✗ 具体例が長すぎるExample too long

具体例は2-3文で十分。長すぎると時間切れのリスクKeep your example to 2-3 sentences. A lengthy example risks running out of time.
