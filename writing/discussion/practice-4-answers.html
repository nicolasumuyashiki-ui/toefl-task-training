Discussion Recap
Dr. Chen
Should schools replace textbooks with tablets and laptops? Why or why not?
Priya (pro-technology)
Tablets and laptops offer interactive content, videos, simulations, and up-to-date information. They help teachers customize lessons.
Ethan (pro-textbook)
Screens cause eye strain and distraction. Textbooks are reliable, need no charging, and help students focus.
Model Answer (approximately 115 words)
Claim I believe that schools should gradually integrate tablets and laptops alongside textbooks rather than replacing them entirely.

Reference While I understand Ethan's concern about screen-related distractions, I agree with Priya that digital tools provide valuable learning opportunities that textbooks alone cannot offer.

Reason The key advantage of technology is that it allows students to engage with material in interactive ways. Digital platforms can include quizzes, videos, and real-time feedback, which help students understand difficult concepts more effectively than static text.

Example For example, in my high school science class, we used a tablet application that let us explore 3D models of the human body, which made anatomy lessons far more engaging than reading from a textbook.

A balanced approach that combines both formats would give students the benefits of technology while preserving the focus that printed materials provide.

Note: This model answer takes a "balanced" position. A fully pro-technology or fully pro-textbook stance is also acceptable, as long as it is well-supported.

Self-Check Checklist
☑ The first sentence clearly states your position (I believe that...)
☑ You reference at least one other student's opinion (I agree with... / While I understand...)
☑ You provide at least one clear reason
☑ You include one specific example
☑ Your response is at least 100 words
☑ You end with a concluding sentence (So overall... / A balanced approach...)
Common Mistakes to Avoid
✗ Unclear position

Starting with "This is a very interesting topic..." without stating your stance. Begin with "I believe that..." immediately.

✗ Two shallow reasons

Listing two reasons briefly is weaker than developing one reason in depth with a concrete example.

✗ No reference to classmates

This is a discussion, so you are expected to engage with other students' posts. Always mention at least one.

✗ Example too long

Keep your example to 2-3 sentences. A lengthy example risks running out of time.
