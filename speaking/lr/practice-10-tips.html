Practice 10 — 空港アナウンスフレーズをチャンクとリズムでマスターするPractice 10 — Master airport announcement phrases through chunks and rhythm

🎙️ Listen and Repeat 攻略ポイントListen and Repeat Strategy
1 チャンクで記憶する：空港アナウンス編Memorize in chunks: airport announcements
空港の案内文は「指示」「場所」「条件」の3層構造でできていることが多い。チャンク単位で役割を意識すると覚えやすい。 Airport announcements often follow a 3-layer structure: instruction / location / condition. Recognize the role of each chunk and memory becomes easier.
例：Example: "If you have a connecting flight, / please proceed to the transfer desk / to confirm your next departure gate."
→ 条件 → 指示 → 目的の3パートに分解→ Break it into condition → instruction → purpose (3 parts)
例：Example: "Please make sure / all your carry-on luggage fits / in the overhead compartment / or under the seat in front of you."
→ 依頼 + 対象 + 場所A + 場所Bの4層構造で捉える→ Read it as a 4-layer structure: request + object + location A + location B
2 リズムコピー：空港アナウンストーンを再現Mirror the rhythm: reproduce the airport announcement tone
空港の案内放送は、落ち着いた丁寧なトーンと、重要語を強調するイントネーションが特徴。 Airport announcements have a calm, courteous tone and intonation that emphasizes key words.
例：Example: "I am afraid your flight has been delayed" → "afraid" と "delayed" をやや強めに、申し訳なさそうなトーンでstress "afraid" and "delayed" slightly, in an apologetic tone
例：Example: "We would like to inform all passengers" → 丁寧でフォーマルなアナウンス調でuse a polite, formal announcement tone
単語を正確に言うだけでなく、空港の案内にふさわしい落ち着いたトーンを意識する。 Pronouncing each word correctly isn't enough — adopt the calm tone of an airport announcement.
3 空港特有のリンキングポイントAirport-specific linking points
空港フレーズでは、前置詞や関係詞のつながりが自然さを左右する。 In airport phrases, linking through prepositions and connectors decides whether you sound natural.
"has been delayed" → has-been-delayed （3語を1つの流れで）(three words as one flow)
"overhead compartment" → overhead-compartment （途切れずにつなげる）(connect without breaks)
"proceed to the" → proceed-to-the （d→tのつながりを意識）(focus on the d→t link)
4 長文は「役割」で分けて覚えるMemorize long sentences by role
24語の文も、役割ごとに分ければ覚えやすい。 Even a 24-word sentence becomes manageable when broken down by functional role.
Q7の例：Example (Q7):
① 導入: "We would like to inform all passengers"
② 情報1: "that complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the terminal,"
③ 情報2: "and charging stations can be found near every gate."
→ 導入→情報1→情報2の流れで自然に再生 ① Lead-in: "We would like to inform all passengers"
② Info 1: "that complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the terminal,"
② Info 2: "and charging stations can be found near every gate."
→ Reproduce naturally: lead-in → info 1 → info 2

1
「approximately」のストレスStress in "approximately"ap-PROX-i-mate-ly、2番目の音節に強勢ap-PROX-i-mate-ly — stress on the 2nd syllable
2
「compartment」は3音節"compartment" is 3 syllablescom-PART-ment、真ん中に強勢を置くcom-PART-ment — stress on the middle syllable
3
「complimentary」のストレスStress in "complimentary"com-pli-MEN-ta-ry、3番目の音節に強勢。"free"の意味com-pli-MEN-ta-ry — stress on the 3rd syllable; means "free"
4

🏋️ 自主練習の方法How to practice on your own
Step 1: 各文を、チャンクごとに区切って音読する（ゆっくり）
Step 2: チャンクを繋げて、文全体を通して音読する（自然なスピード）
Step 3: スマホのタイマーを制限時間にセットし、音読する → 時間内に言い切れるか確認
Step 4: スマホの録音機能で自分の声を録音し、聞き返す → イントネーションやリズムをチェック

目標： 短い文がスムーズに言えるようになれば、本番でも十分対応できる。長い文は完璧でなくてもOK — 途中で止まらず最後まで言い切ることが最優先！ Step 1: Read each sentence aloud, pausing at every chunk boundary (slowly).
Step 2: Connect the chunks and read the whole sentence at natural speed.
Step 3: Set your phone timer to the test limit and read aloud — check whether you can finish in time.
Step 4: Record yourself with your phone, then listen back and check intonation and rhythm.

Goal: Once short sentences flow smoothly, you'll handle the real test fine. Long sentences don't need to be perfect — what matters most is finishing without stopping.
