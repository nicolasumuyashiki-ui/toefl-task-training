Listen and Repeat — 攻略コツListen and Repeat — Strategy Tips

Practice 4 — チャンク記憶とリズムコピーで高得点を狙うPractice 4 — Chunk memorization and rhythm-mirroring for a high score

🎙️ Listen and Repeat 攻略ポイントListen and Repeat Strategy
1 チャンク記憶で長文を攻略Conquer long sentences with chunk memorization
長い文を一語ずつ覚えようとすると短期記憶がパンクする。意味のかたまり（チャンク）で覚えることで、脳の負荷を大幅に減らせる。 Trying to remember a long sentence word-by-word overloads short-term memory. Memorizing in meaningful chunks dramatically reduces the cognitive load.
例：Example: "Due to severe weather conditions, / all departures from this terminal / have been temporarily suspended / until further notice."
→ 16語でも4チャンクなら覚えられる！→ Even 16 words become memorable as 4 chunks!
  • 主語＋動詞をひとまとめにするGroup subject + verb together
  • 前置詞句（for / to / at / in...）を1チャンクにするTreat each prepositional phrase (for / to / at / in...) as one chunk
  • カンマやピリオドの位置がチャンクの区切り目安Commas and periods usually mark chunk boundaries
2 リズムコピーで自然さをアップMirror the rhythm for natural delivery
単語を正確に言うだけでは不十分。ネイティブの強弱リズムをそのままコピーすることが高得点のカギ。 Pronouncing each word correctly isn't enough. Copying the native speaker's stress pattern is the key to a high score.
🔊 "We aPOLogize for the deLAY and THANK you for your PAtience."
→ 大文字の部分を強く・長く発音。弱い部分は素早く通過する。→ Stress and lengthen the capitalized syllables; glide through the rest quickly.
  • 内容語（名詞・動詞・形容詞）は強く読むStress content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives)
  • 機能語（冠詞・前置詞・代名詞）は弱く短く読むReduce function words (articles, prepositions, pronouns) — short and weak
  • 音声を聞く時、メロディーとして捉える意識を持つWhen you listen, hear it as a melody
3 聞き終わってから2秒待つWait 2 seconds after the audio ends
音声が終わった瞬間に焦って話し始めると、最初の単語を噛みやすい。2秒間で文全体を頭の中で整理してから話し始める。 Rushing to speak the moment the audio ends often causes you to stumble on the first word. Take 2 seconds to organize the whole sentence in your head before starting.
⏱ 音声終了 → 1...2... → 自信を持ってスタート
→ 最初の単語がクリアだと、全体の印象が良くなるAudio ends → 1...2... → confident start
→ A clear first word lifts the impression of the whole answer
4 間違えても止まらないDon't stop when you make a mistake
途中で間違えても止まらずに最後まで言い切る。流暢さの評価が下がるより、沈黙の方がダメージが大きい。 Even if you mess up mid-sentence, finish the sentence. Silence costs more points than a small fluency drop.
✗ "All passengers traveling to... um... uh..." → 5秒沈黙 → 大幅減点
✓ "All passengers traveling to London should proceed to gate fourteen immediately." → 多少の言い間違いがあっても最後まで言い切る ✗ "All passengers traveling to... um... uh..." → 5-second silence → major deduction
✓ "All passengers traveling to London should proceed to gate fourteen immediately." → finish through, even with minor slips
🎧 空港アナウンス特有の発音ポイントPronunciation points specific to airport announcements
1
数字・固有名詞をハッキリNumbers and proper nouns must be crystal-cleargate fourteen, terminal B, flight twenty-three → 数字と場所名は特にクリアに発音gate fourteen, terminal B, flight twenty-three — numbers and place names need extra clarity
2
受動態の発音Passive-voice pronunciationhas been changed, have been suspended → been は弱く、過去分詞を強く読むhas been changed, have been suspended — reduce "been," stress the past participle
3
丁寧表現のトーンPolite-announcement toneWe apologize, Please have... → 丁寧なアナウンス調のトーンを維持するWe apologize, Please have... — keep a calm, courteous announcement tone
4
リンキングに注意Watch the linkingturns off the → "turnzoff-the"、thank you → "thankyou" と繋げて発音turns off the → "turnzoff-the"; thank you → "thankyou" — link the words together
5
文末の下降イントネーションFalling sentence-final intonationアナウンスは基本的に平叙文。文末は下げて落ち着いた印象を与えるAnnouncements are mostly statements; falling pitch at the end conveys composure
🏋️ 自主練習の方法How to practice on your own
Step 1: 各文を、チャンクごとに区切って音読する（ゆっくり）
Step 2: チャンクを繋げて、文全体を通して音読する（自然なスピード）
Step 3: スマホのタイマーを制限時間にセットし、音読する → 時間内に言い切れるか確認
Step 4: スマホの録音機能で自分の声を録音し、聞き返す → イントネーションやリズムをチェック

目標： 短い文がスムーズに言えるようになれば、本番でも十分対応できる。長い文は完璧でなくてもOK — 途中で止まらず最後まで言い切ることが最優先！ Step 1: Read each sentence aloud, pausing at every chunk boundary (slowly).
Step 2: Connect the chunks and read the whole sentence at natural speed.
Step 3: Set your phone timer to the test limit and read aloud — check whether you can finish in time.
Step 4: Record yourself with your phone, then listen back and check intonation and rhythm.

Goal: Once short sentences flow smoothly, you'll handle the real test fine. Long sentences don't need to be perfect — what matters most is finishing without stopping.
