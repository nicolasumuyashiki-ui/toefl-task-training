Practice 8 — レストラン接客フレーズをチャンクとリズムでマスターするPractice 8 — Master restaurant service phrases through chunks and rhythm

🎙️ Listen and Repeat 攻略ポイントListen and Repeat Strategy
1 チャンクで記憶する：接客フレーズ編Memorize in chunks: service phrases
接客英語は「決まり文句」が多いので、チャンクごとに覚えると効率的。 Service English is full of fixed expressions — memorizing them as chunks is the most efficient way.
例：Example: "Please let me know / if you have / any food allergies / or dietary restrictions."
→ 接客定型表現 + 詳細情報の2層構造で捉える→ Read it as a 2-layer structure: a polite formula + the specific request
例：Example: "We sincerely apologize / for the delay / and would like to offer you / a complimentary dessert."
→ 謝罪 + 理由 + 提案の3パートに分解→ Break it into apology + reason + offer (3 parts)
2 リズムコピー：接客トーンを再現Mirror the rhythm: reproduce the service tone
レストラン接客では、丁寧さと温かさを伝えるイントネーションが重要。 In restaurant service, intonation that conveys politeness and warmth matters most.
🔊 "May I take your order?" — やわらかく上昇する調子で丁寧にsoft, polite rising tone
🔊 "We sincerely apologize" — ゆっくりと誠意を込めたトーンでslow and sincere tone
単語を正確に言うだけでなく、接客にふさわしい温かいトーンを意識する。 Pronouncing words correctly isn't enough — adopt the warm tone of hospitality.
3 レストラン特有のリンキングポイントRestaurant-specific linking points
接客フレーズでは、単語のつながり（リンキング）が自然さを左右する。 In service phrases, linking between words decides whether you sound natural.
"would like to offer" → would-like-to-offer （4語を1つの流れで）(four words as one flow)
"grilled salmon with" → grilled-salmon-with （途切れずにつなげる）(connect without breaks)
"enjoyed your dining" → enjoyed-your-dining （d→yのつながりを意識）(focus on the d→y link)
4 長文は「役割」で分けて覚えるMemorize long sentences by role
18語の文も、役割ごとに分ければ覚えやすい。 Even an 18-word sentence becomes manageable when broken down by functional role.
Q7の例：Example (Q7):
① 条件: "If you enjoyed your dining experience today,"
② お願い: "we would greatly appreciate"
③ 内容: "a positive review on our website."
→ 条件→お願い→内容の流れで自然に再生 ① Condition: "If you enjoyed your dining experience today,"
② Request: "we would greatly appreciate"
③ Content: "a positive review on our website."
→ Reproduce naturally: condition → request → content

🏋️ 自主練習の方法How to practice on your own
Step 1: 各文を、チャンクごとに区切って音読する（ゆっくり）
Step 2: チャンクを繋げて、文全体を通して音読する（自然なスピード）
Step 3: スマホのタイマーを制限時間にセットし、音読する → 時間内に言い切れるか確認
Step 4: スマホの録音機能で自分の声を録音し、聞き返す → イントネーションやリズムをチェック

目標： 短い文がスムーズに言えるようになれば、本番でも十分対応できる。長い文は完璧でなくてもOK — 途中で止まらず最後まで言い切ることが最優先！ Step 1: Read each sentence aloud, pausing at every chunk boundary (slowly).
Step 2: Connect the chunks and read the whole sentence at natural speed.
Step 3: Set your phone timer to the test limit and read aloud — check whether you can finish in time.
Step 4: Record yourself with your phone, then listen back and check intonation and rhythm.

Goal: Once short sentences flow smoothly, you'll handle the real test fine. Long sentences don't need to be perfect — what matters most is finishing without stopping.
