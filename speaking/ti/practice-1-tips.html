🎙️ Take an Interview 攻略コツ

1 45秒の使い方テンプレート

① 主張（5秒）I believe / prefer / think that...
② 理由（15秒）The main reason is that...
③ 具体例（15秒）For example, when I...
④ まとめ（10秒）So overall, that's why I...

この4文さえ言えれば45秒を使い切れる！理由は「2つ浅く」より「1つ深く」が高得点。

2 沈黙を避けるフレーズ集

考え中
Well, let me think about that...
話し始め
In my opinion... / I would say that...
例を出す
For instance... / Let me give you an example...
まとめる
So overall... / That's why I believe...
話を展開
What I mean by that is... / To be more specific...
強制着地
In short, my main point is...

3 失敗パターンとリカバリー

✗ 5秒以上の沈黙 — 頭が真っ白になる
✓ "Well, let me think about that..." で時間を稼ぐ
✗ 理由が2つで中途半端 — 理由A→理由B→例なし→時間切れ
✓ 理由は1つに絞って具体例まで到達する
✗ 同語反復 — "It's good because it's good"
✓ "What I mean by that is..." で言い換える
✗ 途中で言い直して止まる
✓ ミスを直そうとせず、そのまま続ける。止まるのが最悪

4 使い回せる具体例ストック

作り話でOK。流暢さが大事！以下のネタを事前に準備しておこう：

  • 学校の課題やテスト勉強の経験
  • 友人とのエピソード
  • 家族との旅行や引っ越し
  • 部活・習い事の経験
  • テクノロジーの利便性（スマホ、アプリなど）

5 Q3 模範回答例

Q: Do you think people spend too much time on their smartphones?

Model Answer (約40秒)

"Yes, I definitely think people spend too much time on their smartphones." 主張 5秒

"The main reason is that excessive screen time can negatively affect both our physical health and our ability to focus on important tasks. Many people check their phones first thing in the morning and last thing at night, which disrupts their sleep patterns." 理由 15秒

"For example, last year I realized I was spending over four hours a day on social media. When I started limiting my usage, my grades improved and I felt much less stressed." 具体例 15秒

"So overall, I believe people should be more mindful about how much time they spend on their phones." まとめ 10秒
