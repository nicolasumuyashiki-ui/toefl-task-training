📖 予約ページの使い方（ご参考） 📖 How to book on the Japanese booking page (eeasy)

予約ページ（eeasy）は日本語表示です。基本的には次の流れでお進みください。

  1. カレンダーから空き枠（○ または △）の日付をクリック
  2. 表示された時間帯から希望の時間を選択（時刻は日本時間 JST）
  3. お名前・メールアドレス（このメールと同じものをご利用ください）等を入力
  4. 「予約を確定する」ボタンを押して完了。確認メールが eeasy から届きます。

The booking page (eeasy) is in Japanese only. Here is a step-by-step walkthrough so you can navigate it confidently.

  1. Open the calendar. You'll see a monthly calendar grid. Each date shows one of these marks:
    ○ = open slots available
    △ = limited availability
    × or blank = fully booked / unavailable
    Click any date marked ○ or △.
  2. Move between months using the arrows at the top of the calendar (「<」「>」or 「前月」/「翌月」).
  3. Choose a time slot. After picking a date, a list of available start times appears. Times are shown in Japan Standard Time (JST / UTC+9). You can switch the display to your own time-zone if the option appears. Click the time you want.
  4. Fill in the form. A short form opens. The fields are:
    ・お名前 (Name) → your full name
    ・メールアドレス (Email) → use the SAME email address as this page
    ・電話番号 (Phone) → optional; you can leave blank or enter "0000000000"
    ・ご質問・ご要望 (Notes / requests) → optional; you may write in English
  5. Agree & confirm. If a checkbox labeled 「同意する」(I agree) appears, tick it. Then click the green button labeled 「予約を確定する」or 「確定」(Confirm booking).
  6. Done. You'll see a 「予約完了」(Booking complete) screen and receive a confirmation email from eeasy in Japanese. A separate payment link will arrive shortly afterward.
💡 Tip: In Google Chrome, right-click anywhere on the page and choose "Translate to English". The whole interface becomes readable in English while you book.
